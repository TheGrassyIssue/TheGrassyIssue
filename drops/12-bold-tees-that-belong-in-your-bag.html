12 Bold Tees That Belong in Your Bag

12 Pieces

Twelve graphic tees from eleven brands, and every one of them says something about who you are on the course without requiring a conversation. This is the roundup for golfers who want their shirts to have a point of view — not a swoosh and a tagline, but actual design intent.

Quiet Golf's Monogram Washed Pocket Tee ($55) leads with tonal subtlety. Random Golf Club's S.F.E. Clubhouse Tee ($40) and Students Golf's Grass Obsession ($52) bring the community energy. Devereux, Manors, Radry, Metalwood Studio, and GGNC fill the middle with graphics that range from cactus art to medieval crests. Kingfisher Golf's Getting Worse Tee ($34.99) is the most honest shirt in the game. Walker Golf and Merrill Golf close it out. Prices run $34.99 to $62 — low enough to stock up, high enough that the blanks are actually good.

The Collection — 12 Pieces
Quiet Golf
Quiet Golf — Monogram Washed Pocket Tee
Quiet Golf does the quiet luxury thing without saying the words out loud. Washed pocket tee, tonal monogram, heavyweight cotton. The kind of shirt that gets a nod from the guy with taste at the range.
Random Golf Club
Random Golf Club — S.F.E. Clubhouse Tee
Random Golf Club’s Clubhouse tee for the San Francisco chapter. Black on black with the S.F.E. crest. It’s a membership card you can wear, and at $40 it’s the cheapest entry on this list.
Students Golf
Students Golf — Grass Obsession Tee
Students Golf screen-printed an actual grass obsession onto a tee, which feels honest. South Korea’s most interesting golf label keeps making things you didn’t know you needed.
Devereux
Devereux — Voodoo Cactus Tee
Devereux put a voodoo cactus on faded black cotton and priced it at $44, which is the most Austin thing on this list. Desert meets the fairway. No explanation needed.
Manors
Manors — MGA T-Shirt
Manors keeps the London minimalism tight. The MGA tee is understated to the point of being confrontational about it. Premium weight, clean badge, not trying to impress you.
Radry
Radry — Golf's Hard Tee
Radry just wrote “Golf’s Hard” and left it at that. $42, message received. The most relatable thing in your closet after a round where nothing went right.
GGNC
GGNC — To Fight A Mockingbird Tee
GGNC’s Mockingbird tee is literary and strange — exactly the energy you want from Gumtree Golf & Nature Club. Part course culture, part book club, entirely uncommitted to being normal.
Metalwood Studio
Metalwood Studio — Free Metalwood Tee
Metalwood went full manifesto at $62. The “Free Metalwood” tee reads like a protest sign for a brand that treats golf gear like a design exercise. Heavyweight, dropped shoulder, black.
Kingfisher Golf
Kingfisher Golf — Getting Worse Tee
Kingfisher out of Dallas said “Getting Worse” for $35, and honestly that’s the most accurate golf tee ever made. New indie label, no pretension, just self-awareness priced right.
Walker Golf
Walker Golf — Tool Shed Tee
Walker Golf Things out of Australia. The Tool Shed tee has a retro workshop graphic that looks borrowed from your dad’s garage wall. Premium 220g cotton, standard fit, screen-printed front and back.
Walker Golf
Walker Golf — Perched Tee Navy
Second Walker pick. The Perched tee puts their Kooka bird mascot on navy heavyweight cotton. Part of the Graphic Capsule collection — classic graphics fused with golf, course to pub.
Merrill Golf
Merrill Golf — Target Practice Tee
Merrill Golf closed it out with a vintage-washed target print that feels like it was designed in a garage, in the best way. Dropped shoulder, boxy fit, heavyweight cotton. The quiet closer.
