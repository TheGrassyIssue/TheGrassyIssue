Brand to Know — Devereux Golf

devereuxgolf.com Scottsdale, AZ 18 Pieces

Brothers Will and Robert Brunner launched Devereux Golf in 2013 out of Scottsdale, Arizona. Both TCU grads, both golfers, both convinced the game needed clothes with more personality. The brand went DTC in 2021 and hasn't looked back — Southwestern desert tones, a streetwear edge, and the Skull Caddie icon that's become their calling card. Collections run from Cactus Voodoo to Smokin' Azaleas to a Mount Gay rum collab. The ethos is 'Live Proper' — which apparently means printed mesh shorts and serpent-back tees.

Eighteen pieces here across six categories — polos, knitwear, tees, shorts, hats, and accessories. The Phoenician Polo is 100% cotton with a Johnny collar and open-knit texture. The Loose Knit Quarter Zip is jacquard cotton for those early tee times. The Cargo Short in ripstop with an elastic waist hits that sweet spot between functional and stylish. And the Shocker Skull Driver Cover in Café Racer PU leather is the loudest thing in your bag on purpose. Price range runs $38 to $128.
