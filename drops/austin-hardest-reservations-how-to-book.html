Austin’s Hardest Reservations

By Lenny Harrington, Founder & Editor · Updated November 23, 2026

Ten tables everyone wants, which app to use, when the window opens, and the backdoor at each one.

Field Note10 restaurants · booking rules checked November 23, 2026
The TGI Take

Golfers already know how this works. The good tee times go the minute the sheet opens, the people who get them set alarms, and everyone else learns where the walk-on slots are. Austin’s best restaurants now run on exactly the same logic. We have written before about getting onto the courses you can’t book; this is the dinner version.

Every one of these places has a system, and most of them tell you what it is. Some open 30 days out, some 60, one six months. One drops its seats twice a year on Instagram. And several hold back part of the room for whoever walks in: the bar, the patio, a chef’s counter. They say so on their own websites, but the booking apps don’t mention it.

Six of these ten restaurants keep seats open for people who just walk in. The app never tells you that.

So for each of the ten, we read it. Which app it books on, how far ahead the window opens and at what time, what the deposit and cancellation rules are, and the backdoor: the bar, the patio, the lunch service or the phone call that gets you in when the app says no.

Everything below comes from each restaurant’s own site or live booking page, checked on November 23, 2026. Where a restaurant doesn’t publish its window or drop time, we say whose number it is: usually The Infatuation, which tracks these drops closely. Rules change, so check before you set the alarm.

The Ten

Hardest First

We start with the two omakase counters that only open a few seats at a time and work down to Paperboy, where a little patience usually gets you a table. Each one has the same three notes: how to book, the backdoor for when the app says no, and the good to know details like deposits, party sizes and hours.

Photos: Nicolai McCrary for The Infatuation

Mueller

Tsuke Edomae

Edomae-style sushi omakase from Chef Mike, who trained in Ginza at Hakkoku under Hiroyuki Sato. 4600 Mueller Blvd. The Infatuation calls it easily the toughest table in town.

How to book
Tock, in batches. Tsuke doesn’t publish a schedule; The Infatuation tracks the drops at roughly twice a year, usually a Sunday at 9am, announced on Instagram at @tsuke_edomae.
The backdoor
There isn’t one. Follow the Instagram, set an alarm, and be logged into Tock with a card saved before the drop. When we checked, every date read “All reservations sold out.”
Good to know
Parties of two. Tock takes a $145-per-person deposit, plus fees, at booking.

East Austin

Toshokan

A 14-course omakase for six diners at a time from Chef Saine Wong, at 807 E 4th St. The Michelin Guide recommends it and places it inside Holey Moley, the mini golf bar, hidden behind a wall lined with books. Dinner, then nine holes.

How to book
Tock. Toshokan announces drops on Instagram (@toshokanatx) and to its newsletter, which promises “priority announcements, reservation drops.” The Infatuation reports seats open on the 1st of the month, usually at midnight.
The backdoor
Refresh. The Infatuation says stray seats come back about ten minutes after a drop, when unpaid carts expire.
Good to know
Two seatings, 6:00 and 8:30pm, for parties of one to six. A card hold is required; cancel inside 48 hours and Toshokan charges $180 per person plus a 20% service charge unless the seat is refilled. Tock lists it at $170–$180 per person.
Book on Tock ↗Website ↗

East 6th

Big Slick

A 22-seat steakhouse and bar from Michelin-starred chefs Phillip Frankland Lee and Margarita Kallas-Lee, open since the end of August. Fire-cooked and à la carte.

“Guests enter through an unmarked door on East 6th Street, ringing a bell before being welcomed into a candlelit bar.”Big Slick
How to book
SevenRooms. Big Slick doesn’t publish its window; The Infatuation reports reservations open 60 days out and disappear in about five minutes.
The backdoor
The bar. Big Slick’s own site: “Walk-ins accepted at the bar on a first come, first served basis.” The Infatuation says the bar serves the full menu and suggests lining up 20 to 30 minutes before the 5pm open.
Good to know
Wednesday to Sunday only. Dining room 5:30–10pm; the bar runs to midnight, and 1am on Fridays and Saturdays.
Book on SevenRooms ↗Website ↗
Downtown

Red Ash Italia

Wood-fired Italian from owner and executive chef John Carver, on the second floor of Colorado Tower. It reopened in 2024 after a fire, and has been hard to get into ever since.

How to book
OpenTable. Red Ash doesn’t publish its window; The Infatuation reports booking opens six months out, with prime times gone about four months ahead.
The backdoor
Pick up the phone. Red Ash’s own site: “Please call us if you do not see the reservation time that you would like.” Hosts call back within 24 hours.
Good to know
Dinner nightly, 5–10pm. Call for parties of eight or more. The house asks for “Proper Attire, please” — no athletic wear.
Book on OpenTable ↗Website ↗
West 6th

Sammie’s Italian

Old-school red-sauce Italian from MML Hospitality at 807 W 6th St, with a long bar, a back room called Mildred’s Room and a patio.

How to book
Only through Sammie’s own website; OpenTable lists it as not bookable there. Sammie’s doesn’t publish how far ahead tables open, so check the site often.
The backdoor
Friday lunch. It is the only daytime service Sammie’s runs, noon on Fridays.
Good to know
Tue–Wed 5–11pm; Thu and Sat 5pm–midnight; Fri noon–midnight; Sun–Mon 5–10pm. Valet out front. The online book takes parties up to 20.
Book on Sammie’s site ↗
South Lamar

Uchi

Tyson Cole’s James Beard-winning original, in a converted house on South Lamar for more than 20 years.

“We accept reservations and welcome walk-ins. Patio seating is available weather permitting.”Uchi Austin
How to book
SevenRooms, in a shared book with Uchiko and Uchiba. Uchi doesn’t publish its window; The Infatuation says one month out.
The backdoor
Happy hour, every day 4–6pm, with a walk-in welcome. It includes a nine-course happy-hour omakase for two at $120. The Infatuation says showing up at open often lands a bar seat.
Good to know
Sun–Thu 4–10pm; Fri–Sat 4–11pm. Large parties call 512-916-4808.
Book on SevenRooms ↗Website ↗
East 6th

Suerte

Masa-first Mexican from chef Fermín Núñez at 1800 E 6th St.

“Our booking window opens up 30 days in advance at midnight via Resy.”Suerte’s FAQ
How to book
Resy, 30 days out at midnight. It is the only restaurant on this list that publishes its exact drop time, so use it.
The backdoor
Suerte says it always saves “a portion of our seating for walk-in guests at our bar, in our dining room, and outdoors.” Peak waits run 15 minutes to an hour and a half, and they text you when the table is ready.
Good to know
Lucky Hour Mon–Fri 5–6pm and Sat–Sun 3–5pm. Online bookings take parties of eight or nine depending on the page; ten or more book the semi-private room.
Book on Resy ↗Website ↗
Manor Road

Este

Mexican seafood from Fermín Núñez and Sam Hellman-Mass, the Suerte team, at 2113 Manor Rd.

How to book
OpenTable, up to 30 days in advance for the dining room and covered patio, per Este’s own reservations page. The release time isn’t published.
The backdoor
Este says it always saves “a portion of our seating for walk-in guests including our bar, chef’s counter, and lounge.” Check in with the host in person. Or go to lunch, Friday to Sunday, noon to 4.
Good to know
Dinner daily 5–10pm; happy hour Mon–Thu 5–6pm and Fri–Sun noon–5pm. Free valet. Up to eight online; ten or more is a private event. Tables are held for 15 minutes.
Book on OpenTable ↗Website ↗
South Congress

Aba

Mediterranean with a California accent from chef CJ Jacobson, on South Congress, with one of the best patios in town.

“All three of our bars offer full-service dining and walk-up diners… just snag an open chair.”Aba Austin
How to book
OpenTable, 60 days in advance for groups up to eight, per Aba’s own reserve page. Regular reservations are indoor only.
The backdoor
The patio and the bars. Outdoor tables are first come, first served, and all three bars serve the full menu. You have to check in at the host stand in person; they won’t take your name over the phone.
Good to know
Weekday lunch 11am–4pm; weekend brunch 10am–3pm; dinner nightly. Closed Thanksgiving Day.
Book on OpenTable ↗Website ↗
East 11th & South Lamar

Paperboy

All-day brunch from Daybreak Hospitality Group that started as a food truck in 2015, now on East 11th and South Lamar. The only breakfast on the list.

“Reservations can be made via OpenTable for groups of up to 10 guests. We also hold plenty of walk-in space for walk-in guests every day!”Paperboy
How to book
OpenTable. The Infatuation reports bookings open on the 1st of the month, on a rolling two-month basis.
The backdoor
Walk in, and go late. The Infatuation puts weekend waits at one to two hours and says a 2pm table is much easier than a 10am one.
Good to know
Mon–Thu 8am–2pm; Fri–Sun 8am–3pm.
Book on OpenTable ↗Website ↗
The Last Word

Show Up

The apps reward speed, but almost every room on this list still saves seats for the people who walk through the door. Go early, go on a weeknight, sit at the bar, and be nice to the host. That gets you into more of these than any alarm will.

If we got a rule wrong or a restaurant changes how it books, tell us.
