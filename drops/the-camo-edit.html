Camo on the Course — 18 Picks That Blend In and Stand Out

15 Brands 18 Picks $28–$398

Camo on the course used to mean one thing — the guy who just came from the deer stand. Now it means Realtree collabs with Students Golf, Mossy Oak × Sunday Golf bags, leather headcovers hand-sewn in woodland patterns, and tiger-stripe polos from Devereux that have no interest in blending in. The golf world caught on: camo isn't hiding. It's a print. A texture. A vibe.

We pulled 18 picks from 15 brands across six categories — outerwear, polos, bags, gloves, hats, and accessories. Prices run from $28 (VivanTee's Arctic Camo glove) to $398 (Stitch Golf's SL2 Gen 2 in full camo). In between: Manors' Woodland gilet for cool mornings, BUNX's Texas Wedge snapback, Robert Mark's custom leather headcovers sewn in the USA, and a Mossy Oak × Sunday Golf stand bag that fits 14 clubs at under five pounds.

Eighteen picks, fifteen brands, one rule: if it's got a camo print, it'd better look good doing it.
