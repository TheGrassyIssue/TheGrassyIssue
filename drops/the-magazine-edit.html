The Magazine Edit — 18 Golf Magazines That Aren’t Golf Digest

18 Titles Print & Digital 7 Countries

Golf publishing doesn’t start and end at the Big Three. Somewhere between the corporate titles and the algorithm, a quiet scene of independent golf magazines has been growing — quarterlies with zero ads, biannuals printed in Melbourne, European titles covering golf culture through the lens of ecology and politics, Substacks that go deeper on course architecture than anything on a newsstand.

We pulled 18 titles across three tiers: premium quarterlies that treat every issue like a coffee-table book, indie print magazines from the UK, Europe, and Australia that cover the culture most outlets ignore, and digital-first editorial outlets doing the best long-form golf writing online. No Golf Digest, no Golf Magazine, no Golf Monthly. If it runs equipment roundups or swing tip listicles as its bread and butter, it didn’t make the cut.

Eighteen titles, seven countries, one rule: editorial first.

The Edit — 18 Titles, 3 Tiers
Premium Quarterlies
The Golfer’s Journal
Quarterly · ~$100/yr
124 pages, zero ads. Commercially quiet, editorially pure. Long-form storytelling, course photography, and cultural essays. The gold standard for independent golf publishing.
Visit The Golfer’s Journal ↗
LINKS Magazine
Quarterly · ~$26/yr
Hilton Head, South Carolina. Running since 1988. Golf travel, course architecture, and real estate — the intersection of golf and lifestyle, written for the destination golfer. ~$26/yr.
Visit LINKS Magazine ↗
Kingdom Magazine
Quarterly · 200+ Pages
Co-founded with Arnold Palmer. 200+ pages of luxury golf lifestyle — travel, course design, gear, culture. The heavyweight quarterly for the destination golfer who reads on the plane.
Visit Kingdom ↗
Catalogue 18
Annual · 300 Pages
Golf’s first subscription art magazine. 300 pages of course photography treated as fine art. Annual, collectible, and unlike anything else in the space.
Visit Catalogue 18 ↗
Golf Course Architecture
Quarterly · Global
Published by Tudor Rose. The global journal of golf design and development — new builds, renovations, routing philosophy. The one architects actually read.
Visit Golf Course Architecture ↗
Bunkered
10x/Year · Glasgow
Scotland’s biggest golf title, running since 1996. Ten issues a year with a lively, opinionated tone. Covers the game from a distinctly Scottish perspective.
Visit Bunkered ↗
Indie Coffee Table & Culture
Depeche Golf
From Europe · €22/issue
“From Europe with love.” Golf culture, arts, ecology, and politics. Five issues deep and getting better with every one. A magazine that treats golf as a lens on the world, not a sport in a vacuum.
Visit Depeche Golf ↗
The Links Diary
Triannual · Scotland · £17
Words, photography, and film from Scottish fairways. No swing tips, no product reviews. 14 issues in, with a quiet, literary approach to links golf that reads like a journal entry.
Visit The Links Diary ↗
Golf Out
UK Indie · £18/issue
Seven issues in. UK indie coffee-table magazine built on photography and feel. Sold through Soft Hands Club. A magazine that looks as good on a shelf as it reads on the couch.
Shop at Soft Hands Club ↗
McKellar Magazine
A Golf Companion · ~$14/issue
80 pages by an ex-Guardian golf correspondent and an architecture writer. Writing, illustration, and course profiles with a literary sensibility. “A Golf Companion.”
Visit McKellar ↗
Caddie Magazine
Biannual · Melbourne, Australia
Founded by William Watt. Golf history, sociology, and stories from Arctic courses to ancient Philippine driving ranges. No tour coverage, no gear reviews, no swing tips. The indie treatment, done right.
Read about Caddie ↗
Soft Hands Club
Journal · UK
The editorial arm of the UK’s best indie golf retailer. An alternative perspective on golf — where skate, surf, art, and design culture meet the course. Blog posts, interviews, and cultural essays.
Read the Journal ↗
Digital Editorial & Newsletters
The Fried Egg
Newsletter · US
The best golf architecture newsletter going. Deep course profiles, history, cultural essays, long-form writing. If you care about routing and design philosophy, this is your primary source.
Visit The Fried Egg ↗
No Laying Up
Podcast + Editorial · US
Started as a podcast, grew into the most candid editorial voice in golf media. Witty, opinionated commentary on tour golf, course culture, and everything the broadcast doesn’t say.
Visit No Laying Up ↗
The Quadrilateral
Substack · Newsletter
Thoughtful golf writing on Substack. Interviews, indie magazine coverage, cultural commentary. The kind of quiet, considered writing that doesn’t chase clicks.
Read on Substack ↗
Evalu18
Architecture Reviews · Online
Golf architecture reviews and indie magazine curation. Course-by-course evaluations, magazine directory, and the best single resource for finding which indie golf titles are still in print.
Visit Evalu18 ↗
Moegolf
Culture Blog · Online
Golf culture blog with a collector’s eye. Coffee-table book reviews, indie magazine coverage, and a curatorial sensibility that treats golf media as a design object.
Visit Moegolf ↗
By Design (ASGCA)
Quarterly · Free Digital
From the American Society of Golf Course Architects. Expert perspectives on course design from the people who build them. Free quarterly digital — the insider view of how courses get made.
Visit ASGCA ↗
