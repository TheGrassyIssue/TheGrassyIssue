Fall Golf Colorways: Six on Trend, Two That Never Miss

By Lenny Harrington · Updated November 2, 2026

The Edit · Eight pairings, hex codes included72 picks · in stock, checked November 2, 2026
The TGI Take

Fall is the best season to dress for golf. It is cold enough for layers, the light is low and warm, and the colors that look wrong in July suddenly look right. We picked eight pairings: six that are on trend for fall 2026 and two that have worked for fifty years.

What is on trend. The fall and winter 2026 runway and retail coverage keeps landing on earthy browns and olives, with a few strong accents: a deep racing green, burgundy, a soft butter yellow. Across the 200-plus brands in our index, the softer color we kept seeing in fall drops was sage, usually next to ecru or bone. Golf’s loud one is hunter orange, borrowed from the hunting world and showing up on everything from headcovers to blaze orange trucker hats.

How to wear a pairing. Pick the main color for the biggest piece, a jacket, a sweater or trousers, and use the second color for one or two smaller things: a cap, a glove, a headcover. With hunter orange, one piece is plenty.

Light against dark. Every pairing here is one darker color and one lighter one: racing green and butter, burgundy and stone, navy and heather gray. That contrast is what makes two colors look planned instead of random. If both pieces are dark, the outfit goes flat; if both are light, it washes out on a gray November morning.

Where the money goes. Spend on the big piece you will wear every round, a jacket, a sweater or a pair of trousers, and save on the accent. Most of the second colors in this post come in caps, gloves and towels under $60, so you can try a new pairing for the price of a range bucket and a beer before you buy the sweater.

Start with what you own. You probably already have navy, gray and something khaki. Pick the pairing closest to your closet, add one new piece in the second color, and wear it for a month. Chocolate and cream and camel and charcoal work with almost any golf trousers you have now; sage and hunter orange are the ones to add if you want something that feels new this year.

What is in here. Nine pieces per pairing, 72 in all, from 28 independent brands. Every piece was in stock in the color shown when we checked on November 2, 2026, and every price is in US dollars.

The Last Word

Two Colors Are Enough

The easiest way to look put together on the course is to stop at two colors. Pick a pairing, buy the big piece in the main color, and let a cap or a headcover carry the second one. Sage and hunter orange are the new ones this year; navy and gray will still be right when they are gone.
