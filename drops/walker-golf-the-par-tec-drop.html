Walker Golf — The Par-Tec Drop

walkergolfthings.com 10 Pieces

Walker Golf makes things for the course and the pub. That's their whole philosophy and the Par-Tec drop proves it. Ten pieces in a proprietary lightweight fabric engineered for movement, moisture-wicking, and temperature regulation — but nothing here screams "performance gear."

The Knockout V Polo in Maple is the newest addition and the standout: a V-neck knit collar that reads more European café than American pro shop. The Quarter Zip Fleece in Forest is the layering piece for morning tee times. Members Polo, Kooka Terry Polo, Featherlite Kooka — Walker keeps iterating on the polo silhouette without repeating itself. Shorts and pants in the $90-$100 range, caps at $55. An Australian brand that understands comfort doesn't have to look comfortable.
