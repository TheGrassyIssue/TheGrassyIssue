The Japan Golf Roundup

18 Picks ~$25 – $700 Japan Golf

Japan does golf differently. The obsession with craft, the fabric innovation, the brands that treat a polo shirt like a piece of architecture — it all adds up to a golf culture that’s decades ahead on design and miles apart from the strip-mall pro shop. From Sakata’s forging houses to Tokyo’s streetwear-inflected labels, Japanese golf is where performance meets intention. Every stitch, every alloy, every colorway has a reason behind it.

This is 18 picks across apparel, accessories, and equipment — from 18 different Japanese or Japan-rooted brands. The range runs from a $25 Fit39 glove made from synthetic rubber in Hiroshima to a $700 Honma BERES 10 cart bag with hand-finished geometric detailing. In between: Miura’s forged KM-700 irons from Himeji, Briefing’s military-grade stand bags, Mark & Lona’s CODE collection half-zip, and three new brands — CPH Golf, Kamakura Shirts, and SY32 by Sweet Years — making their first appearance on the feed. No mainstream. No big-box. Every pick is from a brand doing something different.
