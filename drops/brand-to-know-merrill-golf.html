Brand to Know — Merrill Golf

merrillgolf.com 5 Pieces

Merrill Golf calls itself "a design venture that aims to create timeless products through the lens of golf." No polos. No moisture-wicking polyester. Instead: acid-washed tees, heavyweight hoodies, five-panel caps, and dad hats that feel more tackle shop than country club.

The flying-duck logo tells you everything about the vibe — vintage Americana, outdoors-first, golf as a backdrop for the lifestyle they're actually selling.
