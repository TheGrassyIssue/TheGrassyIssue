The Fall Hoodie Edit: The 28 Best Golf Hoodies for Men in 2026

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 14, 2026

21 brands, four kinds of hoodie28 picks · in stock, checked October 14, 2026
The TGI Take

Late October is when Austin starts to get hoodie weather: a cold first tee and a warm back nine. We started with Après Golf, whose whole brand is the part after the round, then swept more than 80 stores in the TGI index for the best golf hoodies for men this fall. Three trends stood out, along with a color palette that is hard to miss.

Golf Hoodie Trends for Fall 2026

The hoodie went knit. Our hoodie edits in June and July were mostly cotton fleece. This fall eight of our 28 are knits that pass for sweaters: Macade made three, Austin’s own Criquet put its Chill-Knit into a hood, Eastside knitted its script into merino, and Aimé Leon Dore and J.Lindeberg went to wool. You can wear these to dinner.

Everything comes pre-worn. Malbon sun-fades its Union Made and Department of Game hoodies, Merrill garment-dyes its olive, Siegelman washes its cobalt and Aimé Leon Dore pigment-washes its terry. Brands want a hoodie to look like you have owned it for ten years on the first day.

The detail moved to the back and the hood. The graphic hoodies keep the front to a small logo and put the art on the back: Devereux’s Lone Star country club, Siegelman’s racing stable, Mount Tee’s mountaineering badge. And hoods got attention: a camo lining on Malbon’s Tour Divot, a zip that runs around the hood on Walker’s Canvas Hood, a bungee cord on Aimé Leon Dore’s knit.

What’s Freshest

Six of these went up in the last week. J.Lindeberg’s Kolton wool zip hoodie landed today, Sugarloaf’s Grid Tech Hoody on October 12, Aimé Leon Dore’s Olive Tree and wool knit hoodies and Criquet’s Chill-Knit on October 9, and Malbon’s Department of Game on October 8, as a preorder that ships by November 4.

The Colors of Fall: Olive, Oatmeal and Navy

Green is the color of the season. Seven of the 28 are green, and none of them is kelly green: sun-faded olive from Malbon and Merrill, Walker’s olive canvas, Jones’ loden, Macade’s pine twice and J.Lindeberg’s kelp. The greens look like a course in late fall, not in June.

Oatmeal is the new white. Another seven sit in the cream family: Après Golf’s cream, Malbon and Sugarloaf’s oatmeal, Macade’s sand, Palm’s bone, Criquet’s stone and Eastside’s oat knit. Nobody made a stark white hoodie we liked.

Navy holds, and one bright is enough. Devereux, Walker and Olydoe keep navy as the dark option, with Ghost’s burgundy for a change. The brights are single statements: Siegelman’s cobalt, Malbon’s blaze orange and Après Golf’s hand-dyed tie-dye.

Every hoodie here was in stock in a medium on the brand’s own store on October 14, 2026. Prices are in the store’s currency, with US dollars in brackets for the others. If you only buy three: the Après Golf Find Your Line ($98), Criquet’s Chill-Knit in stone ($178) and Macade’s Cashmere Tech Knit in pine ($150).

The Questions

What colors are in for fall 2026 golf?

Across the 28 hoodies we picked: muted greens (olive, loden, pine and kelp) and the cream family (oatmeal, sand, bone and stone), with navy as the dark option and single brights like cobalt and blaze orange.

What are the best golf hoodies for fall 2026?

Our 28 picks run from Après Golf's Find Your Line hoodie ($98) and Devereux's Lonestar Country Club hoodie ($84) to knit hoodies from Criquet, Macade, Aimé Leon Dore and J.Lindeberg, plus tech hoodies from Jones Sports Co., Students and Sugarloaf Social Club.

Can you wear a hoodie on the golf course?

At most public courses and munis, yes. Some private clubs still ban them, so check the dress code. A knit or tech hoodie like Macade's Flow Light Knit or Jones' Range Hoodie reads as a sweater or mid-layer and is the safer bet.

Does Après Golf make a good hoodie?

Après Golf's Find Your Line and I Will Not hoodies are its own private-label blank: a 9.4 oz, 80% cotton midweight fleece in a relaxed fit, $98. Its tie-dyed Sunset Twirl hoodies are $88 and dyed by hand.

What is a knit golf hoodie?

A hoodie knitted like a sweater instead of cut from sweatshirt fleece, usually in merino, cashmere blends or cotton. It is lighter, drapes better and can be worn to dinner. Examples here: Criquet Chill-Knit, Macade Flow Light Knit and Cashmere Tech Knit, Eastside Luxe Soft and J.Lindeberg Kolton.

What is the best performance golf hoodie?

For playing in, look for stretch, a water-resistant finish and a pocket that closes. Jones Sports Co.'s Range Hoodie ($148) is double-knit fleece with a DWR finish and a zip stash pocket; Students' Camargo Nylon Hoodie ($178) is a stretch-nylon shell; Macade's Flow Light Knit ($245) is a merino blend with four-way stretch.

How should a golf hoodie fit?

Loose enough to swing in without pulling across the shoulders, but not so long it catches on your belt at the top of the backswing. Raglan sleeves, like Aimé Leon Dore's Olive Tree and Mount Tee's Mountaineering Hoody, give the most room. Boxy, cropped cuts like Siegelman's and Parmore's are for after the round.

Are these hoodies in stock?

Every hoodie here was in stock in a medium on its brand's own store on October 14, 2026. Malbon's Department of Game hoodie is a preorder shipping by November 4, and Walker's Canvas Hood is down to one size.
