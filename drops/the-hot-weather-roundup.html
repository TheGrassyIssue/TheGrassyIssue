The Hot Weather Roundup

18 Picks $14 – $195 Beat the Heat

Texas summers hit different on the golf course. When the heat index pushes triple digits, everything you put on your body matters — the hat that blocks UV without trapping heat, the polo that wicks instead of clings, the shoe that breathes through 18 holes of cart-path concrete. This isn’t about looking good in the heat (though that helps). It’s about surviving it. The difference between a miserable round and a manageable one is fabric weight, ventilation, and whether you remembered sunscreen.

This is 18 picks across four categories — hats, shoes, shirts, and accessories — from 18 different brands. The range runs from a $14 Sun Bum face stick to a $195 G/FORE spikeless shoe. In between: a Students Golf × Sugarloaf Social Club scout hat built for Texas sun, Manors Golf’s lightweight performance polo from London, Nike ACG trail runners that handle cart paths and fairways, and a $16 Frogg Toggs cooling towel that drops your skin temperature on contact. No polyester boxes from the big-box pro shop. Every pick is from a brand doing something different.
