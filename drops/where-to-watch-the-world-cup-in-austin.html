Where to Watch the World Cup in Austin

austinfc.com 10 Pieces

The 2026 World Cup is coming to Austin, and the city is already planning where to watch. If you play nine in the morning and watch the match in the afternoon, this is your cheat sheet.

Haymaker is the obvious pick — American Outlaws HQ, 40+ taps, garage-door layout on Manor Road. Arrive early or don't bother. But the deeper list matters: Scholz Garten is America's oldest beer garden. Parley runs $3.65 drink specials themed by country. Auditorium Shores hosts Austin FC's free outdoor watch parties. Inn Cahoots is the official World Cup hub on East 6th. Ten spots, from soccer temples to beer gardens to the kind of patio where nobody judges your post-round outfit.

The Collection — 10 Pieces

Haymaker
Austin's #1 Soccer Bar · Manor Rd
Haymaker — Austin's undisputed #1 soccer bar. American Outlaws HQ, Chelsea and Man City home, garage-door indoor-outdoor layout with 40+ taps. Opens 6:30 AM Saturdays for EPL. For USMNT World Cup games, arrive an hour early or don't bother finding a seat. The bar that takes soccer seriously, year-round. 2310 Manor Rd.

Auditorium Shores
Austin FC Soccer Celebration · Free
Auditorium Shores — Austin FC's free Soccer Celebration, June 11–12 only. Big screens on the waterfront showing the four opening matches featuring Mexico, Canada, and the US. 5,000 capacity, first come first served, beer garden vibes, family-friendly activations, and Michelob Ultra on tap. Registration already at capacity — get there early. Auditorium Shores.
Shop ↗
Inn Cahoots
Official Austin FC World Cup Hub · E 6th St
Inn Cahoots — Starting June 13, Austin FC transforms this East 6th venue into a free 37-day World Cup hub called Casa VERDE. Every single match — all 100 games — through the Final on July 19. Daily programming, food, drinks, and FOX 7 Austin as the official broadcaster. The closest thing Austin has to an official fan zone. 1221 E 6th St.
Shop ↗
Scholz Garten
America's Oldest Beer Garden · San Jacinto Blvd
Scholz Garten — The oldest continuously operating beer garden in America, est. 1866. Communal outdoor tables, 20+ German beers on draft, and a crowd that's been gathering on this patio for over 150 years. Watching a World Cup match here hits different. Prost. 1607 San Jacinto Blvd.
Shop ↗
Mister Tramps
Dedicated Soccer Pub Since 2009 · N Austin
Mister Tramps — Austin's most dedicated soccer pub, showing every match every day since 2009. Flat screens surrounding the entire bar, 30+ beers on tap, soccer scarves and international flags everywhere you look. This is the one the real heads know about. The drive to North Austin is part of the ritual. 8565 Research Blvd.
Shop ↗
Parley
$3.65 Specials by Country · E Cesar Chavez
Parley — Your new neighborhood bar on East Cesar Chavez, running rotating $3.65 drink specials inspired by whichever country is playing that day. Irish pub roots, creative cocktails at accessible prices, and the kind of spot where you'll actually meet your neighbors over a group-stage match. 1628 E Cesar Chavez St.
Shop ↗
Hopfields
Country-Themed Drink Menus · Guadalupe St
Hopfields — Country-themed drink menus for every match. Mexican cocktails when El Tri plays, caipirinhas for Brazil, scotch deals for Scotland. 40-tap craft beer wall, French-fusion bar food, and reclaimed church pew seating in cozy alcoves. The most creative menu tie-in of the tournament. 3110 Guadalupe St.
Shop ↗
Murray's Tavern
Classic Pub Fare + Cocktails · Webberville Rd
Murray's Tavern — East Austin's classic cocktail tavern on Webberville Rd. Old New York speakeasy energy with signature drinks (Grandma Murray's Rob Roy, Frozen Brandy Alexander), prime rib, fish and chips, and a newly designed interior that feels like it's been there for decades. Opens at noon daily, full kitchen through midnight, and TVs behind the bar for every match. The kind of place where you order a proper drink and settle in. 2316 Webberville Rd.
Shop ↗
Bouldin Acres
Beer Garden + Pickleball + BBQ · S Lamar
Bouldin Acres — Enormous South Lamar beer garden with pickleball courts, cornhole, TVs everywhere, and CM Smokehouse BBQ on site. Show up for a group stage game, grab a rack of ribs, play pickleball between matches, settle in for the late kickoff. Built for a long summer afternoon of World Cup soccer. 2027 S Lamar Blvd.
Shop ↗
The Tavern
30+ Screens · Any Game, Any Time · W 12th St
The Tavern — "Any game, any time." 30+ screens and a staff that actually backs up the motto. Est. 1916, full kitchen, 11 AM open for early kickoffs, and a roomy upstairs patio. Austin's most reliable sports bar — if a match is happening somewhere in the world, it's on a screen inside this building. Also reportedly haunted. 922 W 12th St.
