The Fyfe Putter Cover Edit — Handmade in Scotland

fyfegolf.com 18 Covers $65–$75

Fyfe Golf makes headcovers the way Scotland makes everything it's proud of: slowly, from wool, and with total disregard for trends. We covered their sail cloth and marine suede drivers earlier this year, and their MacKenzie bag collab before that — but the putter covers are where the brand's whole "Woven To The Game" idea gets distilled to one small, perfect object. Harris Tweed woven in the Outer Hebrides, Waverley tweed from Selkirk, Italian Nappa leather, magnetic closures, fleece linings. Every one handmade in Scotland.

The range splits into a few personalities. The tweeds are the heart of it — houndstooth, herringbone, estate checks, and tartans that look pulled from a gamekeeper's closet. The GT leather line trades wool for grand-touring Italian leather in racing red, British green, cognac, and black. Then there are the outliers: a blue-striped seersucker for the summer bag and a waxed canvas Cannich that will patina like an old field jacket. Blades and mallets both covered, $65 to $75, and nothing here looks like anything in a pro shop.
