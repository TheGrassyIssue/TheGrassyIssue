The Retreat Guide — 12 Golf Retreats from Buddy Trips to Bucket Lists

12 Retreats 8 States & 5 Countries Budget to Bucket List

Golf retreats aren’t just Scottsdale villa rentals or corporate resort packages anymore. An entire parallel universe has opened up — community-run international trips, architecture-focused pilgrimages, nature immersions in remote landscapes, and subscriber-only access to elite private courses most golfers will never see.

We organized 12 retreats across four tiers, from the most approachable buddy-trip packages to the ultra-elite bucket-list destinations. Some are $150 a night. Some require a waitlist, a subscription, or a passport. All of them prioritize the experience over the score.

Twelve retreats, four tiers, one rule: the trip matters more than the handicap.

The Guide — 12 Retreats, 4 Tiers

Tier 1 — The Buddy Trip
Myrtle Beach
South Carolina · ~$150–200/night
The classic buddy trip. Eighty-plus courses, all-in packages under $300 per golfer for three nights and three rounds. Legends Golf Resort runs the best group deals. No pretense, no waitlist, just golf.
Plan a Myrtle Beach Trip ↗
Sweetens Cove
South Pittsburg, TN · 9 Holes
A King-Collins masterpiece ranked #49 in the U.S. The e9Retreat house sleeps 14 across the street — bourbon room, golf sim, concierge-stocked kitchen. Thirty minutes from Chattanooga. Nine holes, infinite loops.
Visit Sweetens Cove ↗
Tier 2 — The Destination Resort
Streamsong Resort
Central Florida · 3 Top-30 Courses
Three award-winning courses (Red, Blue, Black) by Coore & Crenshaw, Tom Doak, and Hanse & Wagner. Nine restaurants, sporting clays, guided bass fishing, a grotto spa. Ninety minutes from Tampa. Far from ordinary.
Visit Streamsong ↗
Sand Valley
Nekoosa, WI · 5 Courses, Walking Only
Twelve thousand acres of prehistoric dunes. Five courses including The Lido — a Golden Age recreation by Tom Doak. Coore & Crenshaw’s Sand Valley, Mammoth Dunes, Sedge Valley. Walking only. Remote, pure, intentional.
Visit Sand Valley ↗
Cabot Citrus Farms
Brooksville, FL · Cabot’s U.S. Property
Two 18-hole courses, a par-3 course, and a 10-hole short course on a former citrus farm. Camp Cabot package: two nights, two rounds, plus bass fishing, axe throwing, and clay shooting. Cabot quality, Florida casual.
Visit Cabot Citrus Farms ↗
Tier 3 — The Community Trip
Random Golf Club
Global · International Majors
A global community of local golfers. The Majors are 8-day international trips to bucket-list courses — Ireland, Japan, New Zealand, Australia in 2026. Culture-first, community-driven. The one you’ve heard of.
Explore RGC Majors ↗
Gumtree Golf & Nature Club
New York · Nature Immersions
Three-day immersions in remote landscapes — high desert Utah, the UK, the Catskills. Flora and fauna briefings, group meditation, fly fishing, dinners of local produce. Golf meets ecology meets craft. Made in Brooklyn.
Explore Gumtree Events ↗
Fried Egg Golf Club
Architecture-Focused · $120/yr Membership
Architecture-focused trips from the best golf design newsletter. 2026 events at Streamsong, Sand Valley, SW Michigan, Scotland, Ireland. Sell out instantly. Members get early access. The architecture nerd’s dream trip.
Explore Fried Egg Events ↗
Broken Tee Society
Members Only · TGJ Subscribers
Born from The Golfer’s Journal subscribers. Members-only access to elite private courses — Prairie Dunes, Aronimink, Olympic Club, Pinehurst. Requires a TGJ subscription. The Deadheads of golf.
Join The Broken Tee Society ↗
Tier 4 — The Bucket List
Bandon Dunes
Oregon Coast · 5 Courses + Par-3
Five courses on the Pacific cliffs, Scottish-style links on the Oregon coast. Walking only. Pacific Dunes, Old Macdonald, Bandon Trails, Sheep Ranch. The American pilgrimage. Wake up, walk to the first tee, repeat.
Visit Bandon Dunes ↗
Cabot Cape Breton
Nova Scotia · Oceanfront Links
Cabot Links plus Cabot Cliffs — two 18-hole layouts ranked among the world’s top 100, threading through dunes, forests, and cliffs above the Atlantic. Fall foliage packages in October. The Canadian pilgrimage.
Visit Cabot Cape Breton ↗
RGC Majors: Japan
Nov 7–14, 2026 · 8 Days
Eight days inside the most quietly obsessive golf culture on earth. Tokyo to Mount Fuji and back. The ultimate crossover of golf and travel — temple courses, izakayas, bullet trains, and a community that makes strangers feel like old friends.
Explore the Japan Major ↗
