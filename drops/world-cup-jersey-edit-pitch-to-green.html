World Cup Jersey Edit — 18 Kits From the Pitch to the Green

14 Nations 18 Kits $85–$175

The 2026 World Cup is the first 48-team tournament and the first hosted across three countries. It also produced the best collection of kits in World Cup history. Over 100 jerseys from 13 brands — and the design ambition has never been higher. Buenos Aires folk art on Argentina's away. Magritte surrealism on Belgium's. A buttoned granddad collar on Morocco's that could pass for a golf polo at any club in the country. The days of a badge-on-blank are over.

We picked 18 kits that work from the pitch to the green — jerseys you can wear to a watch party at Haymaker and then straight to a twilight nine at Lions. The range runs from the $85 Puma Morocco with its embroidered taping to $175 for an authentic Nike match jersey. In between: Japan's rainbow pinstripe away that literally looks like a golf shirt, the stealthy USA Away in obsidian with metallic silver, France's mint-and-copper Statue of Liberty tribute, and the Jordan Brand Brazil away — the first Jumpman kit in international football.

Fourteen nations, six categories, every kit linking directly to the shop. With Austin hosting World Cup matches this summer, the crossover between football culture and golf culture has never been more real. Wear the kit. Play the round. That's the whole point.
