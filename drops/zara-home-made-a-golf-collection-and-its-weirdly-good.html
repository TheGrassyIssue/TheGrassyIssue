Zara Home Made a Golf Collection and It's Weirdly Good

zarahome.com $30–$129 5 Pieces

File this under things nobody asked for but everyone needed: Zara Home — the furniture-and-candles wing of the Inditex empire — dropped a full golf collection. A leather golf bag that looks like it was pulled from a 1970s British Open broadcast. Matching headcovers.

A knit club cover with more texture than most sweaters. A green cardigan, a two-tone cap, and a leather notebook cover for the golfer who still keeps a paper scorecard. Deep greens, warm browns, soft creams — old clubhouse energy without the stuffiness.

A $70 divot tool that has no business being that beautiful. "Designed for the players and for the culture" is literally their tagline. They're not wrong.
