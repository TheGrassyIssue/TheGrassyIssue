Where to Get a Little Work Done in Austin

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 31, 2026

Austin, Texas15 spots · checked October 31, 2026
The TGI Take

Some days the best tee time is the one you can steal at 4pm. Working from a coffee shop near a course is the cheapest way to make that happen: close the laptop, drive five minutes and you’re on the first tee with daylight to spare.

With the clocks falling back on November 1, the window gets shorter. Sunsets in November come around 5:30 to 5:45pm, which means packing up by about 3:30 or 4 if you want a full nine. So this list is built backwards from the golf: 15 places in Austin where you can get a few hours of work done, each paired with the nearest course, range or simulator.

A note on laptops. Not every café wants you camping. KVUE reported on October 28 that Better Half now bans laptops in its booths, and plenty of places quietly feel the same. Every spot here either welcomes laptops or has a reputation for it, and all were open when we checked on October 31. Buy something every couple of hours, take calls outside, and don’t spread out over a four-top at lunch.

We’ve covered Austin coffee before, in our pre-round pour guide and the fourteen independents we keep going back to. This one is about the work, and the nine after it.

4 spots

Downtown, Close to Butler

Four spots · all within two miles of Butler Pitch & PuttButler is first come, first served, open until 8pm, and $14 on weekdays.

1/2
Downtown · 710 W Cesar Chavez St
Austin Central Library + Café Crème
Then play: Butler Pitch & Putt, about a mile
The best free office in town. Six floors, free city Wi-Fi, a rooftop deck and bookable rooms for a call, with Café Crème downstairs for crepes, breakfast tacos and a crème brûlée latte (daily 7am to 9pm). The library runs Monday through Thursday 9am to 8pm and shorter hours the rest of the week. Park in the garage under the building: the first hour is free, then $5 for two. Butler Pitch & Putt is across the lake.
Visit ↗
1/3
Zilker · 400 Josephine St
Carpenter Hotel Coffee Bar
Then play: Butler Pitch & Putt, a walk away
The closest pairing on the list: Butler is about 400 yards away. Visit Austin calls the coffee bar laptop-friendly whether you’re a guest or not, and it has the living-room setup to back it up, with leather couches, a big communal table, plenty of outlets and a record player. Coffee and breakfast tacos from 7am, then happy hour from 3 to 5pm, which is about when you should be walking over to the first tee.
Visit ↗
1/3
Downtown · 111 E Cesar Chavez St
Alfred Coffee at the LINE
Then play: Butler Pitch & Putt, about a mile
The LINE says it outright: stay and catch up on emails, with free Wi-Fi and plenty of spaced-out lobby seating facing the lake. Alfred’s iced vanilla latte and matcha, daily 7am to 5pm, with $3 pastries from 3 to 5pm. Parking is the catch: coffee customers get 30 minutes free, so plan on the street or the garage for a longer session.
Visit ↗
1/3
East Downtown · 500 San Marcos St
The Brew & Brew
Then play: Butler Pitch & Putt, about two miles
Coffee in the morning and around 40 beer taps by the afternoon, in one room near Rainey Street. Breakfast sandwiches and grits bowls, open 7am to 8pm every day, with happy hour from 4 to 6pm. A long-time favorite for a working morning that turns into a long lunch.
Visit ↗
3 spots

South Austin

Three spotsLate hours, big patios and Roy Kizer and Jimmy Clay to the south.

1/4
South Austin · 4204 Menchaca Rd
Radio Coffee & Beer (South)
Then play: Butler Pitch & Putt, a short drive up Lamar
The original, opened in 2014 in a 1940s house off Menchaca. Radio South opens at 7am every day and stays open until midnight Monday through Saturday, so you can work through the afternoon, step out for nine and come back for a beer and whatever band is on. Food trucks out back, including Veracruz All Natural, and coffee roasted under its own RCB label.
Visit ↗
1/3
Sunset Valley · 6405 Brodie Ln
Leona Botanical Café & Bar
Then play: Butler Pitch & Putt, about 15 minutes north
The prettiest office on the list: a garden and pavilion on five acres in Sunset Valley, from the teams behind DEE DEE and Veracruz All Natural. The kitchen opens at 7:30am, which is when to come for a working session; it turns into a bar later in the day. Bring headphones and claim a table in the garden.
Visit ↗
1/2
St. Elmo · 440 E St Elmo Rd
Spokesman Coffee (South)
Then play: Roy Kizer and Jimmy Clay, about 4.5 miles
Plenty of seating, plenty of parking and Glacier Freeze Gatorade on tap, which tells you something. House-roasted coffee, weekday hours of 7am to 9pm and happy hour from 4 to 7pm. When you log off, Roy Kizer and Jimmy Clay are about 11 minutes away, with a range and the Joe Balander short course for a quick loop.
Visit ↗
6 spots

East Austin

Six spotsMost of them are within three miles of Morris Williams.

1/3
East Austin · 916 Springdale Rd
Sa-Tén Coffee & Eats
Then play: Morris Williams, under three miles
The Infatuation describes it as a room usually packed with people typing at laptops, and the Wi-Fi rarely kicks you off. Japanese coffee and food at The Canopy: thick-cut toast, katsu curry, rice bowls and a brown sugar cappuccino, plus beer and wine. Open 7am to 9pm daily, so you can play first and work after.
Visit ↗
1/2
East Austin · 1023 Springdale Rd
Medici Roasting (Springdale General)
Then play: Morris Williams, about 2.4 miles
Medici’s roastery room at Springdale General is huge and full of natural light, with a covered, dog-friendly patio and a shared common area with Uroko and The Front Page for lunch. Open 7am to 7pm daily, with plenty of parking in the lot.
Visit ↗
1/3
Cherrywood · 2406 Manor Rd
Spread & Co. (Cherrywood)
Then play: Morris Williams, 1.5 miles down Manor Rd
A cheese shop and café that works for a half day: espresso and a sandwich, then a happy-hour charcuterie board when the laptop closes. Weekdays 7am to 7pm. Morris Williams is a straight shot down Manor Road.
Visit ↗
1/3
East Austin · 2023 Airport Blvd
Palomino Coffee (Airport Blvd)
Then play: Back Nine Golf, same address
The best golf pairing on the list: Back Nine Golf’s East Austin simulator lounge shares the address, at about $50 an hour. The Infatuation calls the roastery a great place to spend a morning working, with a buzzy front half and a calmer back half. Open 7am to 4pm, so it’s a morning office.
Visit ↗
1/3
East Austin · 701 Tillery St
Createscape Coworking
Then play: Morris Williams, under three miles
For when you need a proper desk and a booth for the 3pm call. A locally owned coworking space with day passes from $25 (an open desk) or $35 for a reserved desk with a monitor, weekdays 9am to 8pm, with fast Wi-Fi, printing, free coffee and tea, and free parking.
Visit ↗
1/2
East Austin · 2614 E Cesar Chavez St
Strangelove Coffee & Wine
Then play: Riverside Golf Course, about 3.5 miles
A 1930s bungalow with a courtyard, from the Figure 8 coffee people and the Small Victory team: coffee and house-made syrups by day, wine and cheese plates by night. Open from 7:30am. Work the afternoon, play Riverside, come back for a glass.
Visit ↗
2 spots

North and Central

Two spotsHancock and its $16 sunset rate are close by.

1/4
Hyde Park · 5011 Duval St
Flightpath Coffeehouse
Then play: Hancock, about 1.6 miles
Built for it since 1992. Flightpath pitches itself to people who need to study or get work done: fast internet, plenty of outlets, big tables, low music and a big dog-friendly patio. The Infatuation notes the unwritten rule is to step outside for calls. East Side Pies slices and Tacodeli tacos, open 7am to 7pm. Hancock’s walking nine is four minutes away.
Visit ↗
1/3
Windsor Park · 5811 Berkman Dr
Hank’s
Then play: Morris Williams, about two miles
The café side of a full restaurant, with plenty of room inside, a covered deck, a big parking lot and a full menu for a working lunch, including new East Coast-style pizzas. Open from 8am daily. Lunch meeting at Hank’s, range bucket at Morris Williams.
Visit ↗
