East Coast vs. West Coast: Three Golf Brand Matchups

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 25, 2026

The Edit · Three matchups · Six brands36 picks · prices checked October 25, 2026
The Idea

The two coasts dress for golf differently, and the best independent brands on each side show it. New York labels build for short seasons, cold first tees and a round that ends on a subway platform. West Coast labels build for fog, wind and a game that barely stops for winter.

So we put three of our favorites from each coast head to head: two clothing matchups and one for accessories. Metalwood Studio against Whim Golf, two labels that run like design studios. SLOAT against Rookline for clothes made to be played in. Jones Sports Co against Gumtree Golf & Nature Club for the things you carry. Each brand gets six picks, with the West on the left and the East on the right. We’ve left the verdicts to you.

It’s for the golfer who already knows which coast they dress like, and the one who wants a little of both. Every price and product was checked on the brands’ own stores on October 25, 2026.

Matchup 1 · Design Studios

Metalwood Studio vs. Whim Golf

Two labels that run like design studios and happen to make golf clothes. Los Angeles turns it up; New York keeps it quiet and natural.

West Coast

Metalwood Studio

Los Angeles, CA

Cole Young’s Metalwood has grown from heraldry and ’90s tour polos into a fashion label with a golf accent. The fall line runs on Western yokes, soccer-jersey piping and roomy fits, and the product copy reads like a group chat.

East Coast

Whim Golf

New York, NY

Colin Heaberg and Will Gisel met at Camp Dudley in 2006 and launched Whim in 2019, now working out of an office on Canal Street. Most of the line is American merino, knit in New England and South Carolina, then cut, sewn and embroidered in New York. This year it also took an installation to Milan Design Week.

Woven in Connecticut from RWS-certified American merino and left undyed, so each jacket carries small dark flecks from the wool itself. A RiRi zip, a cinched hem and paneled shoulders built for an easy swing. At $499, it’s the most expensive piece in the post.

Whim knits this long-sleeve polo from 100% American merino in a centuries-old Massachusetts mill, then finishes it in New York with horn buttons and a light green script. Mid-weight 6.5oz jersey, a standard fit and no synthetics.

SLOAT vs. Rookline

West Coast

East Coast

The Questions

Which East Coast golf brands are in this matchup?

Whim Golf from Manhattan, Gumtree Golf & Nature Club from Brooklyn and Rookline from Long Island, New York.

Which West Coast golf brands are in this matchup?

Metalwood Studio from Los Angeles, SLOAT from San Francisco and Jones Sports Co from Portland, Oregon.

What is the difference between East Coast and West Coast golf style?

In this matchup, the West Coast brands lean relaxed and loud, with roomy fits and pieces built for wind and fog, while the East Coast brands lean tailored and natural, with merino, cashmere and layers for short seasons and cold first tees.

How much do these golf brands cost?

The 36 picks run from a $20 Jones ball marker to the $499 Whim Woven Merino Swing Jacket. Clothing starts at $80 for the Whim golf short.
