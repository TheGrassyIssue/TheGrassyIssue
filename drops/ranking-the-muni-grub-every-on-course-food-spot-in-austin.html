Ranking the Muni Grub — Every On-Course Food Spot in Austin

GolfATX 4 Pieces

On-site food is just a vending machine — no kitchen, no menu, no one cooking anything. But Jo's Coffee is right around the corner with solid coffee and snacks before your round. Post-round grub is where Hancock wins: there's a great wing spot nearby and Otherside Deli, one of the best sandwich shops in Austin, is a quick five-minute drive.

The course itself has zero food game, but the neighborhood picks up the slack.
