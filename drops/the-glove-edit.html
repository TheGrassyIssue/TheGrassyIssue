The Glove Edit — 18 Picks From $10 to $48

16 Brands 18 Gloves $10–$48

The golf glove market splits into two worlds. On one side, the tour staples — FootJoy StaSof, Titleist Players, Callaway Triple Diamond — gloves that have been on more hands than any product in the sport. On the other, a wave of independent brands doing things the big names won't: bold prints, surf-inspired patterns, streetwear energy, NYC neighborhood-themed designs, heritage colorways that channel a different era entirely.

We pulled 18 gloves across both worlds. The range runs from the $10 Callaway Dawn Patrol — full leather at a price that shouldn't be possible — to the $48 Malbon Buckets Glove, where Fairfax Ave streetwear meets the first tee. In between: Ghost Golf's stealthy premium Cabretta, Pins & Aces' Colorado-born prints, Eastside Golf's culturally charged Swingman, Vivantee's NYC-inspired designs, and the cult-favorite Asher Evergreen in every color you didn't know you needed.

The edit covers premium leather, tour performance, design-forward picks, streetwear crossovers, indie labels, rain gloves, and value options. Every glove links directly to the brand. Sixteen brands, six categories, one page.
