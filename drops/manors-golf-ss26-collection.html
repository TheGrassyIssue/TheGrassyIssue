Manors Golf — SS26 Collection

12 Pieces

Manors keeps building the bridge between the pub and the first tee, and SS26 is their most complete collection yet. Recycled fabrics, organic cotton, earthy colorways — the whole thing feels like a weekend at a British links course, minus the rain. The Jones collab bag at $314 is the standout flex, but the Buttersoft Polo and GOAT Pique Polo at $116 each are where most people will start.

The Greenskeeper Jacket ($218), Highland Pullover ($191), and Course Gilet ($150) give you layering options that don't scream "I'm wearing golf clothes." Club Pant, Ranger Short, Stableford Short — every bottom in the lineup fits the same relaxed ethos. UK streetwear DNA, course-ready construction, no dress code anxiety.
