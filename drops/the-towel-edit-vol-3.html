The Towel Edit, Vol. 3

18 Pieces

A towel is the cheapest thing hanging off a bag, which is why eighteen brands treat that rectangle of terry cotton as the place to put a whole idea. The category reads better up close than it does from the cart path.

The best of them do something a towel has no obligation to do. Agronomy Workshop prints theirs to look like a crumpled paper bag. Seamus had Pendleton weave the Wyeth Trail as a jacquard rather than printing a picture of one. Mogshade and Parmore put blackletter type on cream and shot it against a wall covered in graffiti.

Prices run $29 to $58, with four brands charging in kronor, pounds and Australian dollars instead. Matchstick holds the bottom of that range and Malbon the top. Several of these are sold out, which counts for less on a towel than it would on a shoe, because they are repeat items and the brands restock them.

The Ones With a Story

1/2
Agronomy Workshop
Unusual Lies Towel · Earth
Agronomy Workshop prints this one to look like a crumpled paper bag, fake fold shadows and all, down to the small print about club membership. Underneath the trick it is 100% woven terry cotton, which is the only conventional thing about it. The brand’s own product copy signs off with “Won’t improve your lies,” and that is the posture of the whole operation.

Local Rule
LCLRL Towel · Dark Green · 399 SEK (~$42)
Local Rule is Swedish and prices in kronor, so 399 SEK comes out around $42 before shipping. The logo repeats across the dark green in a grid tight enough that it stops reading as branding and starts reading as pattern. The towel is also large, large enough that the product shot needs a person to hold it open.

No Membership Required Towel · £35 · Sold out
Mogshade and Parmore put blackletter type on cream and called the result No Membership Required. The pair shot it against a wall covered in graffiti rather than a seamless studio backdrop, which suits a towel carrying that phrase. The towel sold out at £35.

Devereux has knitted the graphic rather than printing it, which gives the skeleton caddie with his cigar and his bag of clubs real depth in the surface. A knit also will not crack after twenty washes the way a print does. The white and green version runs $38.

Plain, Done Properly

Malbon Golf
Ironworks Caddy Towel · Ivory · $58
Malbon Golf keeps the Ironworks towel to collegiate block lettering in pale blue on ivory, with no border and no trim to break up the field. The result sits at the quiet end of a brand that does not usually operate there. Malbon charges $58 for it.

Jones Sports Co has been making bags since 1971 and the Tour Towel is built like it belongs to one. The body is ribbed black with a woven Jones label at the fold and nothing else printed on it anywhere. At $38 it goes with a black carry bag and stays out of the way.

Left of Field works out of Sydney and prices in Australian dollars, so A$35 comes out near $23 before shipping. The pattern is tonal flowers on tan, quiet enough that it only registers as a pattern up close. The brown sold out.

Questions

How many golf towels does this list cover?

Eighteen towels from eighteen different brands. Every brand is one The Grassy Issue already follows and has covered or is tracking.

Are any of these towels sold out?

Six of the eighteen were sold out at the time of writing: Mogshade × Parmore, Birds of Condor, Radry Golf, Sugarloaf Social Club, Bluegrass Fairway and Left of Field. All six are repeat items their brands have restocked before.

What is the cheapest golf towel here?

The Matchstick Golf Roosevelt Pattern towel at $29, followed by Dormie Workshop's Paisley Towel at $30.

What is the most expensive?

Malbon Golf's Ironworks Caddy Towel at $58. Local Rule's LCLRL towel is 399 SEK, roughly $42 before shipping from Sweden.

Which towels are not priced in US dollars?

Four. Local Rule prices in Swedish kronor, Mogshade × Parmore and Sounder in British pounds, and Left of Field in Australian dollars. Convert before you check out.

Does this overlap with Vol. 1 and Vol. 2?

Some brands repeat, the products do not. Malbon, Jones, Walker Golf, Devereux, Birds of Condor, Sugarloaf Social Club and Seamus all appeared in earlier volumes with different towels.

Is the Seamus towel really Pendleton fabric?

Yes. It is a collaboration, and the Wyeth Trail jacquard is woven by Pendleton rather than printed to imitate it.
