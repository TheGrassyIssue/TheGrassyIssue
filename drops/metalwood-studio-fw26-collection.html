Metalwood Studio FW26 — The Whole First Drop, Plus the Lookbook

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 16, 2026

Los Angeles · Fall/Winter 202636 styles · prices checked October 16, 2026
The TGI Take

When we profiled Metalwood in June, it ran on medieval heraldry and ’90s tour polos. FW26 shows Cole Young building something bigger than a golf brand: out of 36 styles, one is a polo and seven are outerwear. There is a padded goalie jersey with a 94 on the back, a reversible tee and a faux-leather parka in ruby red. Golf still shows up in the graphics, like the iGolf hat and a tee about lost sleeves of balls, but the clothes are cut for the rest of the week.

Three design ideas run through it. Western yokes on the track jacket and blouson. Soccer-jersey details: mesh, piping, padding and big numbers. And hardware, from a custom metal belt-loop charm to a circular M badge and a rounded wordmark that looks like a 2000s kit sponsor. The fits keep getting bigger; Metalwood says the Trophy Short is cut like the billowy shorts Tiger wore in college.

Where is it heading? 2026 has been the collab year, from the adidas MC70 and Predator Sala to a G.H. Bass Sportocasin shot in Tokyo. FW26 follows that lead: a fashion collection with a golf accent, not golf apparel with a fashion edge. Outerwear now runs $188 to $298, and the “Pt 1” on the lookbook says more is coming.

Design Notes

What’s new this seasonThese are the six details we kept noticing across the drop, and several of them feel new for Metalwood.

Western yokes
Front and back on the Western Yolk Track Jacket, across the back of the Raglan Sleeve Blouson
Faux leather
The Oasis Parka: water-resistant polyester with a faux-leather finish, in black or ruby
The metal charm
A custom metal belt-loop charm on the Trophy pant and short and the Buster Short
Jersey details
Padded sleeves on the Goalie Jersey, mesh piping on the polo, mesh tape on the Surf Top
Two-in-one
The Dual Face tee reverses, cotton one side and polyester the other; the Trail Mix vest flips too
The marks
A circular M badge, a rounded sporty wordmark and the MW Place logo with a red cursor

The Questions

When did Metalwood FW26 come out?

Metalwood released Fall/Winter 2026 Part 1 in two drops, on October 11 and October 14, 2026, and published the FW26 Pt 1 lookbook on October 16.

How much is Metalwood FW26?

On October 16, 2026, tees were $54 to $72 (the reversible Dual Face tee is $138), hats $42 to $48, shirts and jerseys $148 to $198, pants and shorts $148 to $208, and outerwear $188 to $298. A sunglasses strap is $8.

How many pieces are in the collection?

Part 1 has 36 styles in 65 colorways: seven outerwear pieces, seven shirts and jerseys, two sweatshirts, seven tees, six pants and shorts, six hats and a sunglasses strap.

Who shot the Metalwood FW26 lookbook?

Ryan Thomas Murray and Chase Thompson, in Los Angeles, starring Malcolm Amin and Erin Murray.

Where can I buy Metalwood FW26?

Online at metalwood.studio, where some of the first-drop pieces were already down to their last sizes, and at Metalwood's store on La Brea Avenue in Los Angeles.

Is there a Metalwood FW26 Part 2?

The lookbook is titled Pt 1, which points to a second release, but Metalwood had not announced dates as of October 16, 2026.

How does Metalwood fit?

Big. Many FW26 pieces are labeled oversized or boxy, the Trophy Short is cut long and loose, and the Heavyweight tee is boxy and cropped. Most product pages include a size chart.
