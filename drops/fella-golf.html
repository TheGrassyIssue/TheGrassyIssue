Brand to Know — Fella Golf

fellagolf.com Amsterdam 18 Pieces

Fella Golf is an Amsterdam-based label registered as Fella Golf B.V. and built on a single line: "for the ones already too far gone. It only takes one good shot." That slogan runs through everything they make — from rib knit polos and cashmere vests to pizza-themed headcovers and ball markers stamped with the motto. The S/S '26 collection just dropped, and it covers the full spectrum: polos, tees, knitwear, outerwear, headwear, bags, and playful accessories that refuse to take the game too seriously.

What sets Fella apart is the range. A €190 cotton-cashmere golf cart intarsia crewneck sits next to a €15 golden pizza slice ball marker. A limited collab with Portland's Jones Sports Co on bags shares shelf space with a "Sandbagger" graphic tee and a bucket hat called the Cha-Cheng. The brand transcends golf from course to everyday wear — streetwear boldness meets traditional golf aesthetics, Dutch design meets global ambition. If you've been looking for the European answer to the indie golf wave, Fella is already too far gone.
