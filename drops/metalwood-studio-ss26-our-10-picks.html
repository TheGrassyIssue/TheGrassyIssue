Metalwood Studio SS26 — Our 10 Picks

10 Pieces

Metalwood Studio's SS26 collection is ten pieces deep and every single one looks like it was designed by someone who grew up on skate graphics and indie zines. The Double Plaid Windbreaker ($288) is the headliner — full patchwork in a relaxed cut that's loud enough for the first tee and light enough for the bar after. The adidas x Metalwood MC70 Golf Shoe ($200) anchors the footwear side of the collab.

In between, the King of the Greens Jersey ($168) and Micro Grid Button Up ($168) handle the technical-but-stylish lane, while the Digital Hombre Top and Short ($154/$136) push the pattern work into new territory. Hats at $48 and $58 keep the entry point accessible. Metalwood keeps doing what nobody else in golf is willing to try, and SS26 might be their most confident collection yet.
