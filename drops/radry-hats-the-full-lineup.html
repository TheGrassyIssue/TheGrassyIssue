Radry Hats — The Full Lineup

18 Picks $25 – $55 Hand-Drawn Indie Golf

Radry is the brainchild of Tony Knapton — artist, golfer, and the kind of guy who’d rather draw a cartoon fish on a hat than slap a corporate logo on it. Every piece starts as original hand-drawn artwork, screen-printed or embroidered onto quality blanks. The result is a hat wall that looks more like a gallery show than a pro shop display case. Hats have always been the core of the line, and this is the full current collection — 28 styles deep, from the newest mid/high profiles to deadstock finds and limited collabs.

We pulled 18 that cover the entire range: the brand-new Dive Team and Downwind alongside classics like the Custodian, plus the sold-out WDM collab and Slow Play 001 for the collectors keeping score. Most sit at $42, a few sale picks dip to $25, and the limited pieces top out at $55. All snapback or adjustable. All unmistakably Radry.
