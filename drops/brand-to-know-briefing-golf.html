Brand to Know: BRIEFING

By Lenny Harrington, Founder & Editor · Updated November 25, 2026

Tokyo, Japan · since 199828 picks · prices checked November 25, 2026
The TGI Take

It started as a bag brand with a military supplier. BRIEFING was launched in 1998 by Yuji Nakagawa as a label inside his Japanese company, Celts. He went to the United States looking for a factory that could build to U.S. military spec, and found one that made bags for the military, police and hospitals. The first BRIEFING bag was the Protection Tote, based on a military gun case.

Then the factory went to war. After September 11, 2001, BRIEFING says, its American factory switched entirely to military work and BRIEFING production stopped. It came back in 2003. Nakagawa set up Union Gate Group in 2011, and that group runs the brand today out of Tokyo.

“Paradoxically, Briefing’s design concept is to escape from ‘militaryism.’”BRIEFING, About Us

The materials are the point. BRIEFING’s main fabric is 1050-denier double-basketweave ballistic nylon, lined with 420-denier pack cloth, with YKK zippers and its red label tape on almost everything. The golf line, which it calls Urban Golf, adds Cordura in every weight, Cordura ripstop, and now Dyneema: the DL Series layers ECCO leather with Dyneema, which is why we put three of its covers in our favorite fabrics of 2026. For Fall/Winter 2025, BRIEFING restarted American production of golf pieces, in that 1050-denier ballistic nylon.

It collaborates like a golf brand. Pendleton’s Harding pattern this fall, A.P.C. Golf in 2024, forged wedges with EPON, a carbon stand with shaft maker Graphite Design. The bags are numbered, CR-3 through CR-14, and the CR-4 is the flagship stand bag.

A note on prices. BRIEFING sells worldwide from its Tokyo store, priced in yen, and has no U.S. shops; its stores are in Japan, Taiwan and South Korea. We show yen with an approximate dollar figure. Read on November 25, 2026.

The Last Word

Built for the Trunk

Most golf bags are designed around the course. BRIEFING’s are designed around everything else, too: the drive, the trunk, the airport, the walk from the car. If you’re going to own one bag for a long time, a luggage brand that started in a military supplier’s factory is a good place to look. If we got a detail wrong, tell us.
