Takomo Golf

Turku, Finland Direct-to-Consumer $89 – $679

Takomo launched in 2021 out of Turku, Finland with a premise that shouldn't be radical but somehow still is: forged irons, clean design, half the price. No pro shop markup, no retail middlemen, no tour-van mythology. They design the clubs, partner with top-tier foundries, and ship direct. The savings go straight to the price tag — a full set of S20C forged cavity backs for $649, when comparable Japanese-forged sets from Mizuno or Srixon run well north of a thousand.

The lineup spans every skill level. The 101 MKII is a hollow-body game-improvement iron that won MyGolfSpy's Best GI Iron — serious forgiveness at $579 for a seven-piece set. The 201T MKII, new for 2026, is a players precision iron aimed at better ball strikers who want workability without giving up distance. And the 301 CB is the crown jewel: single-billet S20C carbon steel, forged and finished with the kind of feel you associate with boutique Japanese smithing, not a DTC brand that also sells $15 gloves. Wesley Bryan played Takomo irons on the PGA Tour. The Skyforger wedge, at $89, uses the same 8620 steel alloy that Cleveland and Vokey charge nearly double for.

The Scandinavian design DNA shows up everywhere — minimal branding, matte finishes, clean toplines. No loud graphics, no oversized cavities screaming for attention. Takomo makes the case that the traditional equipment pricing model is broken, and then they prove it by shipping clubs that perform alongside irons three times their price. If you haven't heard of them yet, you will.

The Story — FAQ

What is Takomo Golf?
A direct-to-consumer golf club brand launched in 2021 out of Turku, Finland. It designs its own clubs, partners with foundries, and ships direct, which is how it sells forged irons at roughly half the price of comparable sets. The range runs $89 to $679.
Are Takomo irons any good?
The 101 MKII, a hollow-body game-improvement iron, won MyGolfSpy’s Best GI Iron award and sells for $579 for a seven-piece set. Wesley Bryan has played Takomo irons on the PGA Tour.
What Takomo irons are available?
The 101 MKII for game improvement, the 201T MKII players precision iron introduced for 2026, and the 301 CB, forged from single-billet S20C carbon steel. A full set of S20C forged cavity backs is $649.
How is Takomo cheaper than Mizuno or Srixon?
No pro shop markup and no retail middlemen. Comparable Japanese-forged sets from Mizuno or Srixon run well north of a thousand dollars; the savings go straight to the price tag.
What is the Takomo Skyforger wedge?
An $89 wedge using the same 8620 steel alloy that Cleveland and Vokey charge close to double for.
