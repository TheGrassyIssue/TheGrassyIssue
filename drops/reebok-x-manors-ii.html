Reebok x Manors II — The United Kingdom of Golf Lands August 11

manorsgolf.com Drops Tuesday, Aug 11 16 Pieces

Round one sold through last summer. Now Reebok and Manors are back for a second go, and this one leans all the way into where the game actually comes from. The collection is built around a full heraldic crest — lions, crown, Union Jack — stitched under the banner "The United Kingdom of Golf." In Manors' words, this is the sport handed back to the stubborn sods who actually play it: pot bunkers, decades-long waitlists, summers that never shine.

The lineup runs 16 deep. A crested Tour Shirt anchors the drop, backed by a Harrington Jacket, Club Pant, three polos (Recycled, Mockneck, and a GOAT Pique), an Organic 1/4-Zip Pullover, logo and graphic tees, three caps, a towel, a ball marker, stickers, and a new golf-ready Club C Revenge. On the footwear side, a Club C Revenge Golf in white/navy/red ($110) has already surfaced on release trackers, and an OG Pump Golf in navy/chalk is floating around GOAT — the follow-up to last year's $225 Pump that started all of this.

It goes live Tuesday, August 11 on manorsgolf.com. The first collab didn't stick around, and this one has a waitlist counting down. We'll have the full breakdown with every piece and price the morning it lands.
