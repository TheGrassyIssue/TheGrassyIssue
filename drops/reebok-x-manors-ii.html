Reebok × Manors II — The United Kingdom of Golf

manorsgolf.com Live now 17 Pieces · 30 Colorways

It landed this morning. Round one sold through last summer; round two is bigger, browner, and considerably more committed to the bit. The whole collection hangs off a full heraldic crest — two lions, a crown, a Union Jack — stitched under the banner The United Kingdom of Golf. Manors’ framing is the sport handed back to the people who actually play it in the weather it was invented in: pot bunkers, decade-long waitlists, summers that never quite arrive.

Seventeen products, thirty colorways. The two Reebok silhouettes are the reason most people showed up. The OG Pump Golf ($220) is the follow-up to the shoe that started this partnership — navy croc panels over chalk leather, gold eyelets, the Pump bladder still sitting on the tongue. The Club C Revenge Golf ($130) is the newer trick: a court shoe that has never been near a fairway, given a proper golf outsole and a checkered heel tab, in Legacy Green and Espresso.

The apparel is where the collection is easy to misread. Scroll the product grid and it looks like a cream collection with a few accents. It isn’t. Eleven of the seventeen pieces come in two or three colorways, and the Legacy Green run — a dusty sage, not a hunting green — is the best-looking part of the drop. The harlequin jacquard on the Mock Neck Polo is a genuinely different garment in green than it is in Pebble.

Below is every piece, grouped by color rather than by category, because that’s how this collection actually works. Sold-out variants are still listed and marked as such — the Pebble Mock Neck Polo and Pebble Tech Cap went first, which tells you something about which colorways people wanted.

It’s live now on manorsgolf.com. Reebok takes the collection global on reebok.com and in stores September 30, so if a size is gone today it isn’t necessarily gone forever — but the Manors-exclusive colorways likely are.

The Drop — FAQ

What is Reebok x Manors II?
The second collaboration between Reebok and London golf label Manors, released 12 August 2026. It runs to 17 products across 30 colorways, themed around a heraldic crest and the phrase The United Kingdom of Golf. The first collaboration launched in 2025 and sold through.
What shoes are in the Reebok x Manors II collection?
Two. The OG Pump Golf in Navy at $220, a golf build of Reebok's Pump silhouette with croc-textured panels and gold eyelets, and the Club C Revenge Golf at $130 in Legacy Green and Espresso, which puts a golf outsole and a checkered heel tab under the Club C court shoe.
What colors does the Reebok x Manors II collection come in?
Five: Pebble and Chalk (both off-white), Legacy Green, Espresso, and Gorse Yellow. Eleven of the seventeen products come in more than one colorway. Gorse Yellow appears on the two t-shirts only, and the OG Pump is Navy exclusively.
How much does Reebok x Manors II cost?
From $8 for the sticker pack to $220 for the OG Pump Golf. Tees are $45, polos run $80 to $95, the Tour Shirt is $120, the Club Pant and Club C Revenge are $130 each, the Organic 1/4 Zip is $105, and the Harrington Jacket is $180.
Where can you buy Reebok x Manors II?
It went live 12 August 2026 on manorsgolf.com. Reebok releases the collection globally on reebok.com and in stores on 30 September. Manors-exclusive colorways are not guaranteed to be part of the wider Reebok release.
What is The United Kingdom of Golf?
The theme of the collection. A full heraldic coat of arms with two lions, a crown, and a Union Jack, appearing on the Tour Shirt, the back of the Harrington Jacket, the Graphic T-Shirt, and cast in metal on the ball marker. Manors frames it as a claim on golf's origins on behalf of the people who play it in bad weather.
