SSC x Byrdie Golf — Social Wear Just Dropped

sugarloafsocialclub.com 9 Pieces

Sugarloaf Social Club and Byrdie Golf went to the Bahamas, shot a lookbook, and came back with a nine-piece collab that blurs the line between resort wear and golf gear. The whole collection is built around terry cloth — Cabana Shirt, Anorak, Short, and Mini Skirt, all in bottle green and sand stripes.

The Sugar-Loafer Suede Boat Shoe at $180 is either a golf shoe or a boat shoe depending on your commitment to the bit. A Tour Tote, Tour Towel, seersucker hat, and terry visor round it out. $40 to $180. Camp collar energy, Caribbean color palette, and the distinct feeling that the 19th hole starts at the first tee.
