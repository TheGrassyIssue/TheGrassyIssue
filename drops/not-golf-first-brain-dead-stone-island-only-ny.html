Not Golf First: Brain Dead, Stone Island and Only NY

By Lenny Harrington, Founder & Editor · Updated November 19, 2026

The Edit51 picks · prices checked November 19, 2026
The TGI Take

Most of what we cover here starts on the golf course. These three don’t. Brain Dead, Stone Island and Only NY are fashion brands first, and none of them is a golf brand. They are still the first places we look when we want something new to wear, on the course or anywhere else, because the design is better, the fabrics are better and the clothes have a point of view.

We kept it to what you can actually play in: polos, tees, sweaters, hoodies and pants. No button-downs, no jackets, no hats. Fifty-one pieces, all men’s, all in stock when we checked.

Brain Dead was started in 2014 by Kyle Ng in Los Angeles and Ed Davis, a graphic designer in Melbourne. The two met online and still work mostly online. It began with graphics drawn from post-punk, comics and skate culture and has grown into one of the best-made labels in streetwear: garment-dyed pique, hand-dyed stripes, alpaca knits and wide-leg pants, plus collaborations that range from A.P.C. to Brooks Brothers.

Stone Island was founded in 1982 by Massimo Osti in Ravarino, near Modena, around a single fabric: Tela Stella, a heavy, washed military canvas he used for a first capsule of seven jackets. Carlo Rivetti joined a year later and led the brand for decades. It is known for material research and dyeing, with more than 60,000 dye formulas, and for the compass badge on the left sleeve. Ghost, the line where the badge matches the garment, arrived in 2011. Stone Island has been part of the Moncler Group since 2021.

Only NY was founded in New York in 2007 by Micah Belamarich and Julian Goldstein. It describes itself as an independent brand driven by the city, the outdoors and the arts, and it holds official licenses with the City of New York, NYC Parks and the MTA. Its knitwear is the reason it is here: intarsia trout, retrievers and hero sandwiches, and a speckled polo sweater that would look right on any first tee. It also made a Long Island Golf long-sleeve this year.

How to Wear Them on the Course

Start with a polo. The Stone Island slate blue pique ($285) and the Brain Dead pique thermal ($175) are golf shirts in everything but the name. For colder rounds, the A.P.C. striped polo ($220) or the Only NY speckled knit polo ($148).

Then a sweater with room to turn. The Stone Island ribbed half-zip ($575) is the most golf-ready piece here. The Brain Dead wool commuter ($395) has back pockets for balls and tees. The Only NY intarsia crews are the fun ones, and most are under $170.

Pants that move. The Only NY Crosswind Active Pant ($148) has stretch and a bungee waist. The Brain Dead climber pants have the belt built in.

If you only buy one thing from each: the Brain Dead pique thermal polo, the Stone Island slate blue polo and the Only NY speckled knit polo sweater. Three polos, three cities, no golf logo in sight.

The Last Word

Dress Like You, Then Go Play

Golf clothes have come a long way, but the best-designed clothes still mostly come from outside golf. Brain Dead brings the graphics and the fabrics, Stone Island brings decades of dyeing and material research, and Only NY brings New York and a sense of humor. A polo from one, a sweater from another and pants from the third will get you around eighteen holes and through dinner after.
