The TGI Take

The best collabs in golf right now are not logo swaps. They take something one brand does well and put it somewhere new: a London clothing brand’s pattern on a Montana bag maker’s travel bag, a hockey sock turned into a headcover, a mahjong set from a golf brand.

How we picked. We went through the stores of the brands in our index and pulled every collaboration released since mid-September that was still in stock on November 3, 2026. These are the eleven we liked most, led by Manors x Sun Mountain.

What makes a good one. Each brand has to bring something real. Sun Mountain brings the bag, Manors the look. Sherwood brings real hockey socks, Dormie the leather work. Bluetross tries waxed canvas for the first time. When the collab teaches one of the brands a new trick, you get something neither would have made on its own.

What to buy first. If you only get one thing here, make it the Sherwood x Dormie hockey sock cover. It is the most original piece in the post, and some of the collection has already sold out. If you are planning a trip, the Manors ClubGlider3 is the practical pick.

What is in here. Eleven collabs and 24 pieces, all in stock when we checked, with prices in US dollars. The two Sun Mountain x Manors bags are listed as shipping November 9.

Collab 1

Manors x Sun Mountain

Released October 21

Manors, the London brand, and Sun Mountain, the bag maker from Missoula, Montana, put the same idea on two bags: golf should take you places. Sun Mountain’s copy says the best golf is “still out there waiting to be found.”

The result is Sun Mountain’s E-2.5 stand bag and ClubGlider3 travel bag in stone and charcoal, covered in Manors’ wave monogram. It is the most wearable collab here: quiet colors, serious bags, and a pattern you notice only up close.

Both are limited and listed as shipping November 9. If you have a golf trip planned for 2027, the ClubGlider3 is the one to get.

Collab 2

Paré x Minted Greens and Bluetross

Released October 14 and 26

Paré, the Filipino-American golf brand, had the busiest month of anyone, with two collabs two weeks apart.

The first, with Bluetross, is a driver cover and a blade putter cover with Paré’s Manila logo. Bluetross is known for leather headcovers made in the USA, and this is its first time working in waxed canvas. That is the right call: waxed canvas creases, darkens and picks up a patina, so the cover gets better the more you play.

The second marks Paré’s third anniversary and Filipino American History Month: a mahjong set made with Minted Greens, dedicated to a late uncle who taught the family to play. It comes in a green leather briefcase with 144 tiles and a custom green mat. It is the least golf thing in this post, which is exactly why we like it. Golf trips end at a table, and this is a better table game than cards.

Collab 3

Sherwood x Dormie

Released September 21 to October 7

Two Canadian shops: Sherwood has made hockey sticks since 1949, and Dormie Workshop has made leather headcovers since 2014. They call the collection North of Par, for everyone who plays hockey all winter and golf all summer.

The standout is the fairway cover made from real red Sherwood hockey socks, with leather stripes and a strip of patent leather stitched on like sock tape. The driver cover runs the Sherwood script into Dormie’s tap-in icon, and the range bag is modeled on the puck bag at practice. Some pieces have already sold out.

Collab 8

Walker x Hiroki

Released September 17

Walker, the Gold Coast brand, went across the ditch to Hiroki in Auckland, which makes some of the best Sunday bags around. Walker calls it “a handshake with our mates from across the ditch.”

The bag is almond leather from top to bottom, with a waterproof lining, a leather base and two big pockets embossed with the “Dancing Kooka,” a kookaburra mid-step. It is the most beautiful bag in this post and the one we would carry on a quick nine.

Collab 9

Sugarloaf Social Club x First Tee

Released September 13

This is the one with the best story. Sugarloaf Social Club handed the design work to kids from the First Tee, and every cover is named for the junior who drew it.

Camille put a golfer on top of a club sandwich. Julien drew the New York skyline for his Metro New York chapter and wrote “The Future of the Game” on it. Riya went with flowers and her favorite colors. Tiago’s driver cover and Aadya’s visor round out the set.

All proceeds go to the First Tee. That makes it the easiest thing in this post to justify buying.

Collab 10

MacKenzie x Fyfe

Released October 29

MacKenzie has made carry bags in Oregon since 1985, and Fyfe, from Fife in Scotland, is known for tweed and tartan headcovers. Their bags come in numbered editions, and the two newest landed this month.

The 23rd is black waxed canvas with tan leather and a tartan-lined pocket. The 24th is green leather and mahogany with Harris Tweed inside the pocket. It is the most expensive collab in this post and the one that will look best in 20 years.

The Last Word

More on the Way

A few more collabs from the last few weeks did not make the eleven. Sun Mountain also made a Western stand bag with Marucci, the baseball bat company, and Jones and Cph/Golf released a full collection that we covered in its own post.

Collabs move fast and most are limited. If one of these is the one, do not wait for the restock.
