The Best Wings in Austin — 16 Spots After the Round

Google Maps June 19, 2026 16 Spots

Austin's wing scene runs deeper than any city this size should allow. You've got a sports bar in Hancock running 28 homemade sauces. A food trailer on Rainey Street frenching every drumette into a lollipop. A Laotian bar in Far East Austin glazing wings in caramel fish sauce and crab fat. A ramen shop where the wings outscore the noodles. This is 16 spots — no chains, no crossover with the BBQ list, just wings.

The styles cover everything from classic buffalo and oak-smoked to Korean fried, Vietnamese fish sauce caramel, Thai PB&J, Jamaican jerk, and Baltimore Old Bay. Food trucks parked at dive bars. Craft breweries with creative kitchens. Century-old pubs with bacon-wrapped jalapeno-stuffed boneless birds. Late-night trailers with mystery sauces and 75-cent wing nights. Every neighborhood, every style, all within striking distance of a muni course.

The Classics — 3 Spots

Wingzup
28 Sauces · Hancock
Six wing styles — Original, Golden, Country, Grilled, Smoked, Boneless — across 28+ homemade sauces on a color-coded spice scale. Won the Austin American-Statesman 2024 Best Wings bracket. Ghost Wing Challenge: 25 ghost wings in 10 minutes. Since 2011. 1000 E 41st St.
Visit ↗
The Tavern
100-Year Austin Institution · Downtown
Signature "White Wings" — boneless, bacon-wrapped, jalapeno-stuffed. Classic bone-in wings with crispy, almost-charred exterior. 30+ TVs for game day. Lemon pepper and ghost pepper standouts. One of Austin's oldest bars. 922 W 12th St.
Visit ↗
Delray Cafe
Dive Bar Wings · East Austin
Detroit-style Coney food truck parked at the beloved Nickel City dive bar. Wings sold by the pound. Has done collabs with Franklin Barbecue and Uchiko. Open till 2am. The late-night move. 1133 E 11th St.
Visit ↗

Southeast Asian — 4 Spots

Loro
Franklin × Uchi Collab · S. Lamar
A collaboration between the teams behind Franklin Barbecue and Uchi. Thai wings with hot honey, Thai chili glaze, coconut, and Thai herbs. Hot wings with miso blue cheese. Texas smokehouse meets Southeast Asian flavors. 2115 S Lamar Blvd.
Visit ↗
Lao'd Bar
Laotian Fish Sauce Caramel · Far East Austin
Wings tossed in caramel fish sauce with crab fat. Savory, slightly sweet, impossibly crispy. A flavor profile you genuinely cannot find anywhere else in the city. The Infatuation 8.5. Named one of Austin's best new restaurants 2024. 9909 FM 969.
Visit ↗
Mam Mam
Vietnamese Fish Sauce Caramel · East Austin
Fish sauce caramel wings topped with a mountain of fried garlic. Sweet, salty, pungent, deeply addictive. The Infatuation 8.5. Walk-up window at Springdale General with very limited hours — plan accordingly. 1023 Springdale Rd.
Visit ↗
Sazan Ramen
Japanese Curry Rub · Highland
"Indomitable" wings seasoned with a salty, spicy, umami-laden curry rub. Crispy skin The Infatuation compared to a Dorito. A ramen restaurant where the wings steal the show. 8.4 rating. 6929 Airport Blvd.
Visit ↗

Korean Fried — 2 Spots

Hi Wings
Ultra-Crispy KFC · Allandale
Korean fried chicken specialist. Get the battered style for maximum crunch. Honey Garlic Parmesan, Gochujang, Soy Garlic, Sweet-and-Spicy. Garlic parm fries and honey biscuits are mandatory sides. Austin Chronicle Best of Austin finalist. 2525 W Anderson Ln.
Visit ↗
Spicy Boys
Pan-Asian Fusion · East Austin
Crispy thigh wings with Thai Red Curry BBQ, Citrus-Miso, Lemon-Sichuan dry rub, Indonesian Sambal, and their signature sauce. At Zilker Brewing and St. Elmo Brewing. Known to sell out — arrive early. 1701 E 6th St.
Visit ↗

Smoked & BBQ — 2 Spots

Stiles Switch BBQ
Oak-Smoked Wings · Brentwood
Oak-smoked wings flash-fried and tossed in sauce. Alabama White Sauce, Buffalo, Honey Barbecue. Real smoke flavor from a respected Austin BBQ institution — started as a Sunday football special, now available six days a week. 6610 N Lamar Blvd.
Visit ↗
CM Smokehouse
Triple Technique · S. Lamar
Smoked on the pit, fried, then tossed. Hot Honey Lemon Pepper is the move. Also does smoked cauliflower "wings." At Bouldin Acres with pickleball courts — wings and pickleball is the combo. 2027 S Lamar Blvd.
Visit ↗

Creative & Unique — 3 Spots

Tommy Want Wingy
Chicken Lollipops · Rainey St / S. Lamar / S. Congress
Every drumette frenched into a lollipop with a bone handle — mess-free and uniquely easy to eat. 10 sauces including Ranch On Fire (the cult favorite), Spicy Pineapple, Chile Limon, Holy Schnikes. Three locations at Austin's best bar patios. Since 2012.
Visit ↗
Hold Out Brewing
Thai PB&J Wings · Clarksville
Brined and fried wings with three creative sauces. The standout: Thai PB&J — peanut satay sauce with nam prik pao chili jam, served with miso ranch. The Infatuation called it "one of the most unique flavor combos we've come across." 8.2 rating. 1208 W 4th St.
Visit ↗
The Potluck
Mystery Sauces · South Austin
Late-night food trailer with cryptic sauce names — "Loose Cannon," "Star Dust," "Blue Magic," "Pineapple Sunrise." Huge wings with extra-fine breading for maximum crunch. 75-cent Wacky Wing Wednesday. You tell them your spice and wet/dry preference. 5700 Menchaca Rd.
Visit ↗

Around the World — 2 Spots

The Marylander
Baltimore Old Bay Wings · East Austin
Baltimore-style Old Bay wings — a concept almost nonexistent in Texas. Three varieties: Butter, Honey, or Buffalo, all on the same celery-salt-heavy base. The Honey version is the standout: tender, crispy, sticky glaze with a hint of spice. Infatuation 8.3. 2505 Webberville Rd.
Visit ↗
Winston's Kitchen
Jamaican Jerk Wings · South Austin
Jamaican jerk chicken wings from a food trailer. Smoky and tender with bits of char on the skin. Intense, deeply layered jerk seasoning. Sold by the wing so you can customize your order. Pair with oxtails for the full experience. 3520 W Slaughter Ln.
