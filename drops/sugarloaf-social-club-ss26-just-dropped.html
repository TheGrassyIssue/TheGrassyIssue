Sugarloaf Social Club — SS26 Just Dropped

12 Pieces

Sugarloaf Social Club just dropped SS26 and the collection reads like a country club catalog written by someone who actually has taste. The SSC Anorak at $165 is the hero — water-resistant, packable, and clean enough for a lawn party in Savannah. The Tournament Oxford at $120 is the button-up that works on the course and at dinner after.

Polos run $95 to $105 across solid, club stripe, mini stripe, and long-sleeve options. The Tech Vest at $115 slots in as the mid-layer you'll reach for more than you expect, and the Needlepoint Belt at $195 is the luxury play. Top it off with the Cotton SSC Arrow Cap or the High Crown Visor, both under $42. Georgia roots, no pretension, and a Sun Hoody that proves Sugarloaf isn't just a warm-weather brand.
