The Tee Edit — 14 independent tees for the fairway and beyond

thegrassyissue.com 14 Pieces

Golf retail is flooded with performance polos and quarter zips. Actual tees — the cotton ones you wear — get overlooked. This is 14 from independent brands that treat the graphic tee like it matters.

Gumtree Golf's To Fight A Mockingbird is the headliner: a $60 clap-back after Under Armour lifted elements of their design. Students Golf brings two — Grass Obsession and God Knows — both screen-printed in Texas. Malbon runs the Bermuda line. Agronomy Workshop's heavyweight work shirt at $136 barely qualifies as a tee but absolutely qualifies as a statement. Radry, Found Golf, Manors, Birds of Condor, Sugarloaf Social Club — 11 brands, three continents, $28 to $136. Not a dri-fit in sight.
