The Jersey Polo Edit — 14 Picks, No Buttons Required

June 22, 2026 14 Picks $40 – $178

The polo is evolving. The buttoned placket — that three-button front panel that's been standard since the 1920s — is quietly getting replaced by something cleaner: the jersey polo. No buttons, no fuss. Just a collar, a V-neck or Johnny collar opening, and fabric that moves. Think vintage European football kits meets the first tee. Think Cactus Jack pulling an England Total 90 out of the archive. Think Devereux putting a knitted chest pocket on something that looks like it belongs in a Milanese café.

This isn't a niche trend — it's happening across every tier. Kith is doing micro waffle. Malbon and Bettinardi linked up on a knit polo that costs more than most greens fees. Umbro made a $40 Johnny collar that's one of the best values in the edit. ASOS brought soccer graphics to a burgundy stripe. Manors Golf engineered theirs with Seawool and UV 50+ protection. And Read The Green dropped a '97-inspired nylon pullover that's more windbreaker than polo but belongs in the same conversation. Fourteen picks, fourteen brands, zero buttons.
