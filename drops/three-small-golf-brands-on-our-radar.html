On Our Radar: Three Small Golf Brands, Three Directions

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 12, 2026

Three small golf brands · England, the US and Spain21 picks · all in stock, checked October 12, 2026
The TGI Take

There is no single way a new golf brand has to look. Here are three small ones, all a few years old or younger, each making a small amount of clothing and each pulling golf style in a different direction. Three Putt makes heavyweight streetwear with hand-drawn back prints. Tarraing makes clean, traditional polos with a Celtic streak. Rebolf mixes football training tops, pleats and loud color blocking. Seven pieces from each, all in stock when we checked.

Three Putt launched in 2025 in West Yorkshire. Founder Graeme Whiles learned the game in his grandad’s field with charity-shop clubs, which is why a dairy cow turns up on half the range. Everything is heavy cotton, from 260gsm tees to a 430gsm hoodie, and built for the pub after the round as much as the round.

Tarraing takes its cue from the courses of Ireland and Scotland. The polos are named after people, the highland cow on the hats is called Douglas, and the VA quarter-zip is made in Boston.

Rebolf is from Barcelona, founded by a golf professional who, in the brand’s words, “felt he needed a change.” Its clothes are made at a local studio, in small runs, and look more like a football kit than a pro shop.

The Questions

Where is Three Putt Golf Clothing from?

West Yorkshire, England. It launched in 2025 and was founded by Graeme Whiles. Prices are in pounds, from £29.99 for a tee to £54.99 for a hoodie on October 12, 2026.

Where is Tarraing Golf from?

Tarraing draws on the golf of Ireland and Scotland, and its VA quarter-zip is made in Boston, Massachusetts. Polos were $85 and quarter-zips $125 to $130 on October 12, 2026.

Where is Rebolf from?

Barcelona, Spain. Rebolf was founded by a golf professional and makes its collections at a local Barcelona studio. Prices are in euros; on October 12, 2026 polos were €70 to €75 and pleated trousers €70 to €110.

What makes these three brands different?

Three Putt makes heavyweight cotton streetwear with hand-drawn prints, Tarraing makes traditional performance polos and quarter-zips, and Rebolf mixes football-style polos, pleats and color blocking.
