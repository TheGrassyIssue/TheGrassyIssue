7 Bags That Stand Out

Jones 14 Pieces

Seven bags from six brands, and every one of them was designed by people who think your bag should look as considered as the rest of your kit. This is not a roundup of whatever's on sale at the big-box stores. This is the short list for golfers who treat their bag like the centerpiece it is.

Jones leads with the 2026 Trouper 4.0 ($315) and the Heritage Trouper ($375) — two different takes on the same obsessive design philosophy. MacKenzie's 8-inch Waxed Canvas Mac ($740) is the heirloom play. Vessel's Lux Stand ($399), Stitch's SL2 Gen 2 ($398), and Shapland's Kantle 3.1 ($490) hold the premium middle. Ghost Golf's Anyday Kovert Ops ($415) rounds it out with a tactical edge. These are the bags people notice on the first tee.
