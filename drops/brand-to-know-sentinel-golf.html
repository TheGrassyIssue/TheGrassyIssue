Brand to Know — Sentinel Golf

sentinelgolf.us Minneapolis, MN 12 Pieces

Sentinel Golf is a product design lab based in Minneapolis that builds things no other golf brand would attempt. Founded on the principle “might as well make it,” Sentinel collaborates with manufacturers across industries — Danish leather houses, Kermit Chair Company in Nashville, Dyneema engineers, Cerakote coaters — to introduce ideas both foreign and forgotten to golf. The brand operates on a limited pre-order model: when a production window opens, you buy or you miss it. No restocks, no discounts, no unaccounted-for stock. It keeps the waste low and the demand permanent.

What makes Sentinel different isn’t the price points — though those range from $38 socks to a $3,900 geodesic dome tent — it’s the conviction. A hand-stitched Sørensen Walker carry bag at $1,750. A Dyneema rain suit at $540. An alignment stick with a Cerakote ceramic finish. A collapsible Kermit Chair re-skinned in ultralight performance fabric. These aren’t products designed to sell in volume. They’re designed to exist, and the people who find them tend to keep them forever. The sold-out archive is the real collection — and it reads like a design museum catalog.
