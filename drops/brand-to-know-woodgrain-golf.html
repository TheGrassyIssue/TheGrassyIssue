Brand to Know: Woodgrain Golf — How Golf Used to Look

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 12, 2026

Vintage-inspired golf clothes · New York14 picks · every piece in stock, checked October 12, 2026
The TGI Take

Woodgrain Golf is two people in New York making the golf clothes from old photos: wide trousers with double pleats, cable-knit vests, heavy knits and caps with real hardware on the back. Their pitch is simple. Somewhere along the way golf clothes got tighter, brighter and more technical, and Woodgrain wants the older shapes back, cut to actually play in.

The Legacy Double Pleated Trouser is the piece that makes the case. It is 100% cotton, cut wide, with two deep pleats on each side, and it hangs the way trousers did before every golf pant became a slim five-pocket. In cream with a navy knit, it is the best-looking thing on the page. The pleated shorts do the same job for an Austin summer, and at $130 in every size they are the easiest place to start. The hats are $49, and the brown plaid wool cap with “Swing smooth, Dress sharp” printed inside is the one we would wear first.

The Brand

Woodgrain started in New York with two founders, who sign the brand’s story “Christian & Em.” They write that they both grew up around golf and wanted pieces with “that older feel” that still made sense to golf in: “oversized polos, wider trousers, knitwear, old hats.” The current release is Collection 4, and the brand sells in numbered drops with early access by text. Orders ship in seven to nine business days, and US shipping is free over $100. All 14 pieces below were in stock on woodgrain.golf on October 12, 2026, several of them in only one or two sizes.

The Questions

Where is Woodgrain Golf from?

New York. The brand says it started there with its two founders.

Who founded Woodgrain Golf?

Two founders who sign the brand's story "Christian & Em." The site does not give their surnames.

What is the Legacy Double Pleated Trouser?

Woodgrain's main piece: a wide-leg, double pleated golf trouser in 100% cotton. It was $180 in cream, tan, chocolate brown and black on October 12, 2026, with only sizes M and L in stock.

How much does Woodgrain Golf cost?

On October 12, 2026, hats were $49, the mock neck $85, the cable-knit vest $95, the nylon vest $98, the double pleated shorts $130 and the Legacy trousers $180. US shipping is free over $100.

How long does Woodgrain take to ship?

The brand says orders are processed and shipped within seven to nine business days.
