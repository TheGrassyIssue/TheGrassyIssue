Brand to Know: BUNX Golf

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 20, 2026

Brand to Know · Utah · Logan & Paige Bailey22 picks · prices checked October 20, 2026
The TGI Take

BUNX Golf is a husband-and-wife hat company from Utah that writes better Texas jokes than most Texas brands. Logan and Paige Bailey say they knew they were committed the day Paige came home with her own set of clubs. They started BUNX because they were tired of brands slapping “an AI-generated logo on the same crappy products” and calling it premium. That’s a big swing for a small brand. After going through everything they make, we think they mostly back it up.

The hats are the point. Every one is the same structured five-panel A-frame snapback at $34 to $38, and the names do the selling. Long Iron Country is for the holdouts still hitting 3-iron off the tee. Club Champ celebrates a “hard-earned delusion.” The Fort Worth hat calls the city the beaten stepchild of Dallas, and the Tex is named for putting from off the green. The look is rodeo and western: camo, corduroy, chain-stitch script and a flying pig. That’s a crowded lane right now. BUNX stands out on the inside of the hat, with printed under-brims, branded taping and a woven label that just says “You look good.”

A hat built to be destroyed. Here’s what we want from a golf hat: design and construction good enough that we can absolutely beat it up. Sweat, sun, rain delays, the floor of the cart, the back seat of the car. A cheap hat just falls apart. A well-made one breaks in, and six months later it looks perfectly worn in and like nobody else’s. That’s the bar for a $38 hat, and a structured cotton crown with real chain-stitch embroidery is what you want for it.

Then they overbuild the small stuff. The towels have a magnetic hub and a built-in scrub pad. The DRT divot tool is CNC-machined stainless steel with a PVD coating, and BUNX jokes you’ll be buried with it. The V01 tees bend instead of snapping; MyGolfSpy’s tester needed about ten back-and-forth bends to break one. To be fair, at roughly 48 cents a tee they only pay off if you break more tees than you lose.

It feels made for Texas. The Tex and the Fort Worth hat are the obvious ones, but the whole range fits here: a desert javelina on the Moonlit Mulli, camo that already showed up in our Camo Edit, and hats you can wear to a muni at 7am and a patio at 7pm. The Father + Son sets are a smart touch for anyone bringing a kid to the range.

If you buy one thing, get the Long Iron Country in camo and natural. If you buy two, add the DRT.

The Questions

Where is BUNX Golf based?

Utah. BUNX was started by husband and wife Logan and Paige Bailey.

How much are BUNX Golf hats?

Adult hats were $34 to $38 on October 20, 2026. Youth hats were $28, and matching Father + Son sets were $57.

Are BUNX V01 tees really unbreakable?

They bend rather than snap. In MyGolfSpy's test it took about ten back-and-forth bends to break one. A 27-pack costs $13 for 2.75-inch tees or $10 for 1.5-inch.

What is the BUNX DRT?

A $45 divot repair tool CNC-machined from stainless steel, PVD coated, laser etched and magnetic, sold in several colorways.
