11 Water Bottles We'd Actually Carry

Various 11 Pieces

Hydration on the course is not optional — especially in Texas, especially in summer, especially when you're walking. These 11 water bottles are the ones we'd actually clip to a bag or carry in a cart.

Sentinel Golf leads with the Basecamp — ultralight titanium, single-wall, handcrafted with the same attention they put into their headcovers. $68. Gumtree Golf has two Nalgene collabs in the $46 range. Malbon did a Stanley ProTour Tumbler. Manors, Quiet Golf, and Kinto keep it clean. The Owala FreeSip at $28 is the sleeper — the mainstream bottle golfers are quietly adopting because the sip-or-swig lid works with one hand. Titanium craft to everyday carry, $28 to $72.
