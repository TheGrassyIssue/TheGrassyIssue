No Budget — The Most Expensive Golf Gear We Could Find

August 10, 2026 The Edit 24 Picks · $200 to $399,800

Every list we run is built around restraint. Good design, fair price, things you’d actually carry. This is the opposite of that.

We went looking for the top of the market — the clubs with gold in them, the bag that costs more than the clubs, the ball marker made of meteorite, the simulator that turns a garage into a launch monitor lab. Some of it is genuinely beautiful. Some of it is absurd. A few pieces you can’t buy at all, which is its own kind of price tag.

Everything here is real and current, with prices taken from the maker or the resort. Where a thing isn’t buyable — a tour-only putter, a members-only club — we say so.

The Trips — Four Ways to Spend a Car

Rates come from the resorts’ own published numbers and move with the season. Two of these carry an asterisk, and the asterisks are the interesting part.
Pinehurst · North Carolina
Bed, Breakfast & Golf · $660–$1,180 / night
Per person at The Carolina (Deluxe) depending on season, including a round a night and breakfast. No. 2 carries a $250 premium, No. 10 is $125, and you need two nights minimum to play either.
Shop ↗
Pebble Beach · California
Resort golf package · from ~$3,345
Per person for three nights and two rounds — though that figure comes from a golf packager. Pebble’s own seasonal rates differ, so check direct before committing.
Shop ↗
Adare Manor · Ireland
Stay & Play · from ~$1,953 / night
A night at the 2027 Ryder Cup host. One thing to know before booking: the course is only open to resort residents from May 26 to October 18, 2026.
Shop ↗
Te Arai · New Zealand
Links suites · NZ$525–840 / night
The reachable half of the best golf on earth. Tara Iti next door is invitation-only — you play it with a member or you don’t play it. That’s the real luxury item.
