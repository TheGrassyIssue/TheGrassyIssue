The 2026 Driver Drop — 12 New Sticks

Various 12 Pieces

Every major OEM dropped a new driver for 2026 and the technology war has never been tighter. Twelve sticks, eight brands, and a price range that runs from $500 to $5,500 if you're feeling unhinged.

TaylorMade's Qi4D leads — ranked #1 across multiple independent tests, Scottie Scheffler's gamer, and the most balanced driver in the field. Callaway's Quantum Triple Diamond Max and Titleist's GTS4 are right behind. PING and Mizuno hold the $500-$600 middle. Then there's the Honma Beres 09 5-Star at $5,500 — a driver that costs more than some memberships. Krank Golf rounds it out for the long-drive crowd. Best year for drivers in a decade, and the hardest year to pick one.
