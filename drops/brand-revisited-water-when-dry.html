Brand Revisited: water when dry, Tending the Garden Through Winter

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 24, 2026

Brand Revisited · Orlando · First covered June 15, 202621 picks · prices checked October 24, 2026
The TGI Take

When we first wrote about water when dry in June, we called it the garden metaphor that actually works: colorways named River, Leaf, Soil and Blackberry, a brand voice that should tip into self-help and somehow doesn’t, and eight pieces from $15 to $128. Four months on, the metaphor is still doing all the work, and there’s more garden than ever.

Who they are. water when dry is a small label out of Orlando, Florida. Its website shows a live Orlando clock in the header, and its golf days are rounds in Orlando. It doesn’t put a founder’s name on anything. Its oldest products date to early 2023, and the whole idea fits on one line from its About page: “Like any garden, what you tend to will grow.” The brand writes its name in lowercase, and so do we.

The new drop is built for cold weather. Fall/winter 2026 arrived in early October with a homepage line we liked: “The garden goes quiet. The colors don’t.” The lead piece is a crinkle-nylon Utility Pant in black, brown and green with zip cargo pockets and toggle hems, and the brand shot it in a field at sunset with a puffer vest, which is exactly how we’d wear it for a November nine. A five-panel Utility Cap, a blue collegiate tee with flowers growing out of the letters, and $15 socks round it out.

The pullover is the product everybody knows. The collared pullover, 450gsm cotton with a white collar and an oversized script across the front, first came out in fall 2024. Its own product copy calls it “the internet’s favorite pullover,” and one Trustpilot reviewer says he’s seen it on celebrities, influencers and sneakerheads. This fall it comes in brown and pink, made to order and shipping November 20–30. The black original and the green V2 are on sale, but only in a couple of sizes.

There’s more golf than you’d guess. water when dry isn’t a golf brand, but it keeps ending up on the course. The Camper Short, which led our June post, was shot on a golf course in the rain, towel on a club by the pond. The Utility Cap copy names golfing outright. And the brand runs golf days in Orlando, open to anyone: “No handicap required and none checked.” That’s the most TGI sentence any brand has written this year.

The community is the brand. Its Discord server, The Garden, has grown past 2,000 members. Members post what landed in the mail, what’s on their feet, their dogs and their records, and get early access to drops. The brand’s homepage runs a live feed from it. Customers on Trustpilot give the brand 4.7 out of 5 across more than 100 reviews.

What hasn’t changed: nothing costs more than $128, the colors still come from a garden, and the voice is still wholesome without being corny. Half the store is on sale right now, mostly the sizes at the ends of the run. If you’re buying one thing, get the Utility Pants. If you’re buying two, add a pullover before the made-to-order window closes.

How It Fits

Sizing, Fabrics and Where to Start

Most of the tees and layers are a boxy fit, and the brand suggests sizing down on the Canopy Fleece if you want it closer. The tees are 220gsm stone-washed cotton (the SEEDS pocket tee is lighter, the Raglan is a heavier 280gsm French terry), and the hoodie and sweats are 380gsm. The Camper Short fits true to size with a 7-inch inseam; the swim and Treehugger shorts are 6 inches. The made-to-order pullovers and burgundy cap ship November 20–30, and in-stock orders take one to two weeks to process. If you’ve never worn the brand, start with the $15 socks or a $30 SEEDS tee.

The Questions

Where is water when dry based?

Orlando, Florida. Its website header shows an Orlando clock, and the brand runs its golf days in Orlando.

What is new from water when dry for fall/winter 2026?

Utility Pants in black, brown and green ($108), a Utility Cap ($48, or $42 for the made-to-order burgundy), a Blue University T-Shirt ($58), Cream Quarter Socks ($15), and the collared pullover in brown and pink, made to order at $128 and shipping November 20 to 30, 2026.

What is the water when dry pullover?

A 450gsm cotton collared pullover with a white collar and an oversized script logo, first released in fall 2024. The brand describes it as feeling like a crewneck and wearing like a rugby polo.

Does water when dry run golf events?

Yes. It runs golf days in Orlando that are open to anyone, with no handicap required.

Does water when dry run true to size?

The Camper Short fits true to size. Most tees, hoodies and the Canopy Fleece are a boxy fit, and the brand suggests sizing down on the fleece for a closer fit.
