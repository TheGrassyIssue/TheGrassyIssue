Manors x Gentleman Jack: Whiskey on the First Tee

Manors Golf $21 – $283 9 Pieces

Manors teamed up with Gentleman Jack to do something that sounds like a stretch on paper but lands perfectly on the hanger. The entire capsule pulls its paisley print from engraved detailing on vintage Gentleman Jack bottles, and the fabric story is just as considered — Seawool made from recycled oyster shells and plastic bottles, UV 50+ protection, four-way stretch throughout.

The range runs $21 to $283, covering everything from course-ready pieces to the kind of lifestyle gear that plays equally well at a whiskey tasting or a Saturday morning tee time. Manors keeps proving they can do collabs without diluting their identity, and this one might be their most interesting yet. Whiskey branding on golf apparel shouldn't work. It does.
