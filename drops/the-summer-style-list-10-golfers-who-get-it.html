The Summer Style List — 10 Golfers Who Get It

thegrassyissue.com 10 Pieces

Golf style used to mean one thing: logo polo, pleated khakis, visor from the pro shop. That era is over. These ten golfers — tour pros, brand founders, content creators — are actually pushing the look forward. Anthony Kim's comeback wardrobe. Charley Hull's zero-effort cool. Stephen Malbon building an empire. Sam Jones documenting the whole movement as @thegolffashionguy.

What connects them isn't a single aesthetic. It's intention. They think about what they wear the same way they think about their setup. Ryan Peake blends athletic and editorial. Adam Scott keeps it minimal. Kieran Hall treats every round like a lookbook. Golf style has a roster now, and this is the starting lineup.
