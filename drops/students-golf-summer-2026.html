Students Golf Summer 2026 — Summer School Is in Session

August 9, 2026 Drop 30 of 69 Pieces

Students Golf doesn’t drop collections so much as publish a new course catalog. Founded in 2022 under the tagline “For Golfers Who Study The Game,” the brand filters streetwear, academic culture, and vintage Americana through a golf lens — and Summer 2026 is its deepest seasonal run yet: 69 pieces across polos, quarter-zips, button-ups, mesh sets, pleated shorts, lightweight pants, and hats.

The headline is the Anti-Gravity Lightweight Pant — Students calls it the most lightweight performance pant in golf, field-tested in Nevada heat, which makes it about the only pant argument that survives an Austin August. The Calculus Baggy Pleated Shorts are back for another season in new colors, carabiner clip and all, and the retro Manager Q-Zip and paneled TerraLuxe Ledgewood polo carry the top half. We pulled 30 pieces — 18 from the apparel run, 12 from accessories — that cover the full syllabus. Everything ships free in the U.S.

The whole brand reads like a course catalog on purpose. Product names come straight from campus life — Calculus, Librarian, Coordinator, Art Dept — the internal line is called the Course Studies Division, and the catalog now runs 500+ SKUs deep across apparel, hats, headcovers, and accessories. The lookbooks lean into it too: classroom sets, library shelves stacked with Students-branded book boxes for the PAYNTR collab, and a booth at the PGA Show they captioned “many Students attended class today.” Golf apparel by people who grew up on skate videos and college football Saturdays, not country club verandas.
