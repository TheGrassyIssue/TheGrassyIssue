Fyfe Golf — Between Tides. Sail cloth, marine suede, and waxed canvas, made in Scotland.

6 Pieces

Fyfe's new collection is built around the tidal window that lets you cross from mainland to links on the Scottish islands. Four material families: Crinan waxed canvas, Harbour seersucker, Marine Suede in light tan and optic white, and the limited-edition Oban line made from repurposed sail cloth. Headcovers, putter covers, a rangefinder case, a shoe bag, a hickory brush collab with Dimple & Divot, and two MacKenzie carry bags at $888.
