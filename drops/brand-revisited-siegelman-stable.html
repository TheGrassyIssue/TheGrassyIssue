Brand Revisited: Siegelman Stable

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 31, 2026

Brand Revisited · New York · Est. 202033 picks · prices checked October 31, 2026
The TGI Take

We wrote about Siegelman Stable in July, a few weeks after its Maxfli golf collection came out for the U.S. Open at Shinnecock Hills. Since then, the brand that Max Siegelman built on his family’s harness racing stable has had its busiest stretch yet: a Mets cap with New Era in July, a Bazooka gum capsule with a cap that has a pocket for exactly one piece of gum, and in August a 13-piece New York Yankees collection.

The Yankees. The Yankees collection went live on August 4 online and at Yankee Stadium, with headwear, jackets, shorts, sweatshirts and tees, and New Era caps. Former Yankees pitcher CC Sabathia is in the campaign. amNY noted the brand’s profile rose this year when Knicks guard Jalen Brunson kept wearing its hats in interviews. Max told amNY the collection is about “honoring tradition while creating pieces that feel timeless.”

The golf is still here. Most of the Maxfli capsule is still in stock: the heavyweight polo, the pullover jacket, both caps and the tote. Add the mesh-lined track shorts and you have a full outfit for a warm round. Max’s first job was caddying, for five years, and it shows in how much of this works on a course.

Fall/Winter ’26. The fall line has come in drops: a cobalt Racing Stable set on September 22, cotton cable knits and a ribbed zip cardigan on October 13, and a black-and-ochre track set on October 27. For a cold round, the knits, the sherpa and the hoodies are the ones we would reach for. The Prince Tennis caps and tees, first made in 2025, came back on October 8.

The Story

From the Stable to the Stadium

Max Siegelman’s father, Robbie, founded Siegelman Racing Stable in 1982 and trained harness racehorses for decades. In 2020, with $600, Max printed hats with the family’s horse-and-sulky logo and sent them to the NBA bubble. The logo on every piece in this post is the stable’s own.

Six years on, the brand has worked with the Knicks, Mets, Yankees, Rangers, Prince, Ford and the Indy 500, and golf has become part of it. “Together with Maxfli, we wanted to create something that feels rooted in the game, but also expressive, unexpected and true to how people are engaging with golf today,” Max said when the collection launched in June.

A portion of proceeds still goes to equine therapy, the work Robbie started decades ago.
