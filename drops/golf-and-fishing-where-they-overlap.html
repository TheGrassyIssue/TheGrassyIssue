Play Nine, Fish Nine

By Lenny Harrington, Founder & Editor · Updated November 24, 2026

Metalwood, Gumtree, Malbon, Duck Camp, Fins & Pins, Sunfish and more32 picks · 7 munis · prices checked November 2026
The TGI Take

Same hours, same people. Golfers and anglers both get up before the sun, spend hours mostly quiet, and drive home talking about the one that got away. Reading a green and reading a current are the same squint. Plenty of us do both, and the good days are the ones where you get to do both before lunch.

The fresh brands noticed first. Metalwood’s fishing shirt promises “a stroke off your score for every fish caught in-round.” Gumtree gave its 2024 collection over to fishing. Malbon now runs a Golf and Tackle line, Swag made a Gone Fishing pattern, and Fins & Pins built a whole headcover company on four words: Play 9, Fish 9.

None of it needs a membership. Both sports have a velvet-rope version, and that isn’t us. Every course below is a city or public course, most under $50 on a weekday, and the fishing next to them is free from the bank. You’ll need a Texas fishing license, and that’s about it.

Play Nine, Fish Nine

Winter is the window. Texas Parks and Wildlife stocks rainbow trout in creeks and park ponds from December through February, several of them a short drive from a muni. Play the morning nine, keep the waders in the trunk, and fish the afternoon.

Munis With Water Nearby

Places to Do BothSeven city and public courses in Austin and the Hill Country, cheapest first-ish, each with fishing close enough to do the same day. Fees are the courses’ own published weekday rates; stocking dates are from Texas Parks and Wildlife’s 2026–27 schedule.

Austin

Hancock

Austin’s oldest course is nine holes for $20, and Lady Bird Lake is a short drive south.

The round
$20 for nine holes, any day. Walking only.
The fish
Largemouth bass in Lady Bird Lake, which Texas Parks and Wildlife rates as excellent. Free bank access at Festival Beach and Auditorium Shores.
When
Year-round. Go early on a weekday for both.

Austin

Roy Kizer & Jimmy Clay

Thirty-six city holes in southeast Austin, with winter trout down the road.

The round
$35 on a weekday.
The fish
Texas Parks and Wildlife is scheduled to stock 2,600 rainbow trout in Onion Creek on January 10 and January 24, 2027. The ponds at Southeast Metro Park are TPWD community fishing lakes.
When
January and February, while the trout last.
Roy Kizer rates ↗
Fredericksburg

Lady Bird Johnson

The city course and a stocked trout creek share the same park.

The round
$47 to walk on a weekday, Monday to Thursday, plus tax.
The fish
Live Oak Creek runs through the park and past hole 6, which plays over it. TPWD is scheduled to stock 500 trout around January 10, 2027.
When
Kid Fish Day is January 9, 2027, the day before the stocking.

Landa Park

A city course that runs along the Comal.

The round
$50 on a weekday with a cart (2024 rate card; check before you go).
The fish
Landa Lake, inside the same park, is a TPWD community fishing lake.
When
Year-round. The Comal stays cool even in August.

Llano River Golf Course

A small-town course on the Llano, with clear river fishing a short drive away.

The round
Rates aren’t published online; call the pro shop.
The fish
TPWD is scheduled to stock 1,909 trout at Castell Crossing on December 10, 2026, and the Llano holds Guadalupe and largemouth bass year-round. Llano City Lake is in town.

The Last Word

Bring Both

Throw a rod in the trunk next to the clubs. The worst case is a round of golf, and the best case is a trout and a birdie on the same Tuesday. If you know a muni with good water nearby that we missed, tell us.
