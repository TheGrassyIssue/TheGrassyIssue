SSC Summer Sailing

Sugarloaf Social Club 8 Pieces $40 – $3,500

Sugarloaf Social Club's Summer Sailing drop is eight pieces that pull from the same nautical DNA — decommissioned sailcloth turned into one-of-a-kind MacKenzie golf bags, suede boat shoes that work on the dock or the clubhouse patio, and accessories that tie the whole thing together. It's a tight collection with a clear point of view: the overlap between sailing culture and golf culture is bigger than most people think, and SSC is leaning all the way in.

The headliners are three SSC × MacKenzie sail bags, each built from actual decommissioned sails and stamped with their original sail numbers. No two are the same. The rest of the drop — a suede boat shoe, canvas belt, ribbon bucket hat, summer fleet tee, and a set of custom BB&F "Don White" forged irons — rounds out the collection from $40 to $3,500.
