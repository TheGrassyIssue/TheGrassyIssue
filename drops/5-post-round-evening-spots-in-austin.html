5 Post-Round Evening Spots in Austin

5 Pieces

The round is over. The sun is dropping. You're sunburned, slightly dehydrated, and not ready to go home. Austin has you covered.

Five spots, five different vibes, all designed for the golfer who treats the 19th hole like the most important one. Equipment Room at Hotel Magdalena on Music Lane brings the craft-cocktail-and-vinyl energy. Only The Wild Ones on South Congress is the patio hang. Frazier's Long & Low on the South Shore keeps it low-key. Domo Alley-Gato on East 6th is the outdoor bar with games and tacos. And The Golden Horn on North Loop is the neighborhood spot where nobody cares that you're still wearing golf shoes. No reservations needed, no dress code enforced, no judgment for ordering a second round before 6 PM.
