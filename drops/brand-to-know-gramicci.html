Brand to Know — Gramicci

gramicci.com 18 Pieces

Gramicci started in 1982 when rock climber Mike Graham needed pants that could handle big-wall climbs without restricting movement. The integrated webbing belt, the gusseted crotch, the freedom-of-movement philosophy — it all started on the granite faces of Yosemite. Forty-plus years later, Gramicci makes everything from the original climbing pant to UPF-rated rashguards, and the brand has quietly become a favorite among golfers who walk munis in the summer heat. The same design principles that work on El Capitan — unrestricted range of motion, durable natural fabrics, easy-adjust fit — work just as well walking 18 in August.

The range runs from the $44 Nylon Gramicci Cap to the $170 Taos Canvas Jacket. Organic cotton twill, nylon-cotton blends with water-repellent finishes, UPF-Shield stretch fabric — Gramicci builds clothes for people who actually move. No logos screaming, no performance marketing jargon, just well-made pieces with real climbing DNA. Eighteen picks here that prove you don't need a golf brand to play great golf.

The Story — FAQ

What is Gramicci?
An outdoor clothing brand started in 1982 by rock climber Mike Graham, who needed pants that could handle big-wall climbing without restricting movement. The integrated webbing belt and gusseted crotch both originated on the granite of Yosemite.
Why do golfers wear Gramicci?
The design principles that work on El Capitan — unrestricted range of motion, durable natural fabrics, easy-adjust fit — work just as well walking 18 in August. It has quietly become a favourite among golfers who walk municipal courses in summer heat.
What is the Gramicci webbing belt?
An integrated belt built into the waistband of the pants, one of the brand’s original 1982 features and still its signature detail.
What fabrics does Gramicci use?
Organic cotton twill, nylon-cotton blends with water-repellent finishes, and UPF-Shield stretch fabric.
How much does Gramicci cost?
From $44 for the Nylon Gramicci Cap up to $170 for the Taos Canvas Jacket.
