14 Hoodies That Caught My Eye

Various 14 Pieces

Fourteen hoodies from twelve brands, and every single one was designed by people who actually play golf. This isn't the fleece wall at your local sporting goods store. It's the intersection of streetwear weight, course-ready cuts, and the kind of graphics that make you look like you know something the rest of the foursome doesn't.

Eastside Golf's Core Fleece Swingman sets the tone at ~$88 — premium cotton, Swingman embroidery, streetwear DNA. From there, Gumtree Golf goes heavyweight at $145, Malbon pushes into the $185 range with the Safety Buckets Fesque, and Casualist keeps it understated at ~$85. Brands like Sounder, Sugarloaf Social Club, and Jones Golf Bags fill the middle. Whether you need a layer for early morning tee times or a post-round pullover that doesn't scream "I just played 18," the edit covers it.
