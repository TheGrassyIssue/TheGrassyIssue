8 Bucket Hats Worth the Brim

8 Pieces

The bucket hat is having its quiet moment on the course — no logos screaming from the crown, no performance fabric monologues on the hang tag. Just a wide brim, some shade, and the vague suggestion that you know what you're doing out there. Manors' Greenskeeper is recycled fabric with a wide brim that matches their jacket.

Malbon covers the range from a clean $58 script logo to the reversible Velasco at $88. Students Golf went full boonie with a screen-printed marble camo that shouldn't work but does. And Birds of Condor — the Australian brand that treats golf hats like an art form — brings the Swing Kings reversible, a corduroy Rage, and the Fore Tiger.

Four brands, eight hats, zero visors.
