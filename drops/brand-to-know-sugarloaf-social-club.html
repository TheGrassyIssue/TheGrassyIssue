Sugarloaf Social Club — The Group Chat That Became a Golf Brand

33 Projects

Sugarloaf Social Club runs the widest price range in independent golf and holds a single point of view across all of it. The catalogue reaches from a MacKenzie sailcloth bag at $1,400 down to a paper fan at $14, and both read as the same company. It started as a group chat. A few college roommates were graduating in 2011 and wanted somewhere to keep talking about golf, so they named the thread after the course they had spent four years on. The chat became an annual trip, and the trip became an Instagram account that at one point had more followers than Golf Digest. Ian Gilley filed the LLC in 2017 and has described the sequence as “a happy little accident.”

The course they named it after is dead. Sugarloaf Mountain in Minneola, Florida was Coore & Crenshaw's first design in the state, built in 2008 across more than 250 feet of elevation, and it opened directly into the financial crisis. It never recovered and it is permanently closed. A brand named after a course you can no longer play is an on-the-nose metaphor for how golf nostalgia works, and to their credit SSC has never leaned on it.

Sugarloaf is a subsidiary of Pro Shop, Inc., the media company founded by Full Swing producer Chad Mumm and Joe Purzycki, which raised around $20 million from the PGA Tour and Powerhouse Capital and also owns Skratch and GolfWRX. Skratch discloses this in its own coverage. It explains how an independent-looking brand from McLean, Virginia ends up with an officially licensed THE PLAYERS capsule in the navy, gold and white it drew up for TPC Sawgrass.

The catalogue reads as thirty-three projects. MacKenzie bags built from retired sailcloth sit next to a $14 paper fan printed for a Japanese golf media brand, a teal-striped soccer kit made for a coffee bar, and a Western Golf Association line supporting the Evans Scholars program. Most of it has sold out.

The MacKenzie Bags

MacKenzie Golf Bags
Sail Bag “8185” · $975 · Sold out
MacKenzie builds this one from retired sailcloth, so each bag carries the number of the sail it came from — 8185, in this case. Three were made, at $975. It is the piece people bring up when they talk about Sugarloaf, and the reason the waitlist exists at all.

White leather on a Double Seve body makes this the most expensive thing Sugarloaf has ever put its name on. MacKenzie builds the same 8-inch bag in canvas for $850, so the hide accounts for the rest of the $1400. White is a bold choice for something you carry across wet grass.

The standard-issue Double Seve carries the same 8-inch MacKenzie body, in canvas rather than white leather, and it sold at $850. That is the cheaper of the two by a wide margin, though $850 is still $850. Canvas will also scuff and soften in a way the white hide never would.

BB&F · Don White
“Don White” Irons, 4–P · Set #B012 · $3000
Don White ground clubs for tour players for decades, and these are his Boyd Blade and Ferrule Blade heads, done by hand and sold raw. Lofts run 24° through 47° with a slight square toe and a notch muscle, and raw means they arrive with oxidation and will rust unless you oil them. The set ships in a foam-lined flight case at $3000, the most serious object Sugarloaf has ever sold.

Questions

Who founded Sugarloaf Social Club?

Ian Gilley. He is the founder and CEO, and the brand is based in McLean, Virginia.

How did Sugarloaf Social Club start?

As a group chat in 2011 between college roommates who had just graduated. It became an annual golf trip, then an Instagram account, then a brand. The LLC was filed in 2017. Gilley has described the whole thing as “a happy little accident.”

Where does the name Sugarloaf come from?

Sugarloaf Mountain Golf & Town Club in Minneola, Florida, the college hangout. It was Coore & Crenshaw's first Florida design, built in 2008 with more than 250 feet of elevation change, and it opened into the financial crisis. The course is permanently closed.

Who owns Sugarloaf Social Club?

Sugarloaf Social Club is a subsidiary of Pro Shop, Inc., the golf media company founded by Chad Mumm and Joe Purzycki that raised roughly $20 million from the PGA Tour and Powerhouse Capital. Pro Shop also owns Skratch and GolfWRX. Skratch discloses the shared ownership in its own coverage of the brand.

What is the most collectible Sugarloaf collaboration?

The SSC x MacKenzie Sail Bags, built from retired sailcloth and numbered for the sail each one came from. They sold at $975. The white leather Double Seve at $1,400 is the most expensive piece the brand has released.

What are the Evans Scholars products?

A line made with the Western Golf Association supporting the Evans Scholars program, which sends caddies to college on full tuition and housing scholarships.

Is Sugarloaf Social Club gear hard to get?

Most of the collaboration work is. Of the thirty-three projects catalogued here, the majority are sold out. The current in-house range — the Terry run, the Tour Towel, headwear — restocks more reliably.

What is the cheapest Sugarloaf collaboration?

The SSC x Clubhaus Sensu Fan at $14, a folding paper fan printed with the Clubhaus mark.
