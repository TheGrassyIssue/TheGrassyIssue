Golfer And: Gear for Six Blended Interests

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 19, 2026

Six pairings · golf plus the thing you love36 picks · prices checked October 19, 2026
The TGI Take

Nobody is only a golfer. The guy on the first tee at Lions is also a climber, or a car person, or someone who checks the surf report before the tee sheet. For a long time golf gear pretended otherwise: one look, one catalog, one kind of person. The independent brands are the ones changing that, because they’re started by people with a second obsession.

So we put together six pairings, six picks each. Some brands come from the other world and drift toward golf, like Free Fly, started by fly-fishing guides, or Kahala, a Honolulu shirt company since 1936. Some are golf brands that wear their other love openly: STITCH named its first bag after a superleggera, and Metalwood says its pockets are climbing-inspired. And a few are simply the real thing from the other world that happens to work on a course, like a down layer built to go under a climbing harness.

The picks run from a $14.95 trout ball marker to a $3,049 caddie that follows you around. Almost everything comes from a small, founder-led company, and a handful are new to TGI. If your second interest isn’t here, tell us. There are a lot more pairings to make.

The Questions

What golf gear is good for a golfer who also climbs?

Look for stretch pants with deep pockets and a small drawstring pouch. Metalwood Studio's Bomber Pant ($208) has climbing-inspired pockets, Howler Brothers' Reimers Trek Pants ($119) are a climbing pant from Austin, and an Arcade's Golf Stretch Belt Kit ($131.95) comes from a belt brand built for climbers. Prices checked October 19, 2026.

What is the best golf tech gift under $700?

On October 19, 2026 our picks were the Arccos Air tracker ($349.99), the Voice Caddie SC4 PRO launch monitor ($599.99), the Blue Tees Player Pro GPS speaker ($199.99), the Shot Scope LM1 launch monitor ($199.99), which has no subscription, and the Square Golf HE launch monitor ($699).

Where can I buy new persimmon or hickory golf clubs?

Louisville Golf in Kentucky still makes both: a Calamity Jane hickory putter replica ($225) and a Classic 50's persimmon driver ($375) on October 19, 2026.

What golf gear suits a surfer?

Look for ocean-rooted brands making golf pieces: Vissla's Bogey Man polo ($60), Kahala's Surf Check performance polo ($105), Dormie Workshop's longboard-striped Surfboard hybrid cover ($145) and Seamus Golf's hand-forged Big Wave ball marker ($36), all checked October 19, 2026.

Are there golf gifts for fly fishermen?

Yes. North Coast Golf Co. makes a trout-logo golf glove ($28), Pins & Aces a trout ball marker ($14.95), Smathers & Branson a fly-fishing needlepoint belt ($195), Free Fly, founded by fly-fishing guides, makes a polo it pitches for golf ($88), and Austin's Duck Camp makes a trout-patch performance hat ($36).
