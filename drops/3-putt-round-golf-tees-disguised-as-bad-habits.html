3 Putt Round — Golf Tees Disguised as Bad Habits

8 Pieces

European brand 3 Putt Round has one joke and they commit to it completely: golf is an addiction, so the accessories should look like vices. Tees come packaged in cigarette boxes and matchbooks. The branding leans hard into punchy colors, cheeky copy, and the kind of packaging that belongs on a shelf as much as in your bag.

The lineup runs from Golf Tee Matches at 20 euros to the Addiction Knitted Polo at 120 euros, with towels, caps, and various tee packs filling in the middle. The Vecchio x Lost Balls Towel collab adds some crossover credibility. Everything ships from Europe, everything is designed to make your playing partners do a double take when you pull tees out of what looks like a pack of smokes. It's novelty done with enough craft and humor that it actually sticks.
