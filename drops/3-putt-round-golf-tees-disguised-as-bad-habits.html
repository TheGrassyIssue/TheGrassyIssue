3 Putt Round — Golf Tees Disguised as Bad Habits

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 12, 2026

Golf accessories and clothing · Milan, ItalyRefreshed · 18 picks, all in stock, checked October 12, 2026
The TGI Take

3 Putt Round has one joke and commits to it completely: golf is an addiction, so the accessories should look like vices. Tees come in cigarette packs with warning labels, in snus tins and in matchbooks. It is novelty done with enough design sense that the packs end up on the dashboard of the cart, which is exactly where the brand wants them.

A lot has changed since we first wrote about them in June. There are now eight scented tee packs, gold packs and €75 collectible cartons, a silicone iPhone case with a tee holder built into the back, and a tweed version of the corduroy Addiction cap. Some of the original picks have sold out, so this is a fresh list: four kinds of tee pack, the Tee Case and towels, six caps and five pieces of clothing. The knitted polo from the Lost Balls collab is still the piece we would buy first.

The Brand

3 Putt Round was founded in Milan in 2023 and describes itself as “an addictive game born from the passion for the game.” It makes fun, unconventional golf accessories and has collaborated with Lost Balls, the Japanese-French golf brand, the Paris restaurant Vecchio and Casualist. Prices are in euros and everything ships from Europe. All 18 picks below were in stock on 3puttround.com on October 12, 2026.

Milan, Italy. The brand was founded there in 2023.

Golf accessories that look like vices: tees in cigarette-style packs, snus tins and matchboxes, plus scented tee packs, a phone case with a tee holder, towels, caps and clothing.

On October 12, 2026, a single tee matchbox was €6, a Golf Addiction or scented tee pack was €12.90, three Upper Decky tins were €26, three Golf Addiction packs were €31 and a collectible carton of eight packs was €75.

What is the 3 Putt Round Tee Case?

Does 3 Putt Round ship to the US?
