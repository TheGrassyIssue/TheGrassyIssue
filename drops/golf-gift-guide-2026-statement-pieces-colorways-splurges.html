The TGI Take

Part 2 is the part of the list where the gift has to say something. Part 1 covered the things a golfer uses every round; this one covers the things other people notice. Same format: each brand gets its own block, why it belongs in the category, and three or four gifts that prove it. Every brand is independent, and none of them appears in Part 1 or Part 3.

Three categories, thirty brands: pieces that get a reaction on the first tee, colorways nobody else in the group owns, and the splurge they would never put in their own basket. Prices run from a $10 ball marker to an $888 carry bag.

Where to start. For compliments, Parmore’s camo quarter-zip. For a new colorway, Criquet’s treetop green alpaca hoodie. For the splurge, the MacKenzie x Fyfe 23rd Edition bag.

Compliments Guaranteed

The piece somebody asks about before the first tee shot

Some gifts get used. These get noticed. Every brand in this category makes at least one thing that starts a conversation on the first tee, whether it’s a camo quarter-zip, a headcover cut from an old camp tent or a hat with a cowboy and a martini on it. Pick the brand that matches how loud the person you’re buying for likes to be.

The Last Word

Buy the Thing They Keep Looking At

The best statement gift is one they have already noticed on someone else. The best splurge is one they have already priced and closed the tab on. If you know which brand they follow, you are most of the way there.

Check the timing. Mogshade makes covers to order on about a three-week lead. Slice Town’s pitch tool and Fyfe’s Glove Caddy are on pre-order. Devereux’s quarter-zip, Breaker pullover and crew are final sale, so get the size right. Takomo’s iron prices are where each build starts.

Prices are as each store shows them to a US visitor. Mount Tee shows Australian dollars, so those dollar figures are approximate. Everything was available to order on November 9, 2026.
