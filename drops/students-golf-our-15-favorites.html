Students Golf — Our 15 Favorites

18 Picks $15 – $148 Streetwear × Golf

Students Golf doesn’t look like any other golf brand — and that’s the point. Founded in 2022 under the tagline ‘For Golfers Who Study The Game,’ the label filters streetwear, academic culture, and vintage Americana through a golf lens. Everything borrows from campus life: the Calculus pants, the Librarian polo, the 1st String sweater. Even the internal line is called ‘Course Studies Division.’ It’s golf apparel designed by people who grew up on skate videos and college football Saturdays, not country club verandas.

The catalog runs deep — 500+ SKUs across polos, pants, shorts, outerwear, hats, headcovers, and accessories. We pulled 18 pieces that represent the full range of what Students does. The Calculus Baggy Pleated Pants are the signature — oversized, carabiner-clipped, built from a custom trico-rayon-spandex blend. The Librarian Polo anchors the performance side with micro-waffle texture and UV protection. The hats and headcovers round it out. Everything ships free in the U.S.
