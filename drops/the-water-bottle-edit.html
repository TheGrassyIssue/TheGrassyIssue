The Water Bottle Edit

16 Pieces

Sixteen bottles from sixteen brands, and not a single YETI or Stanley in sight. We went through every brand in our universe — all seventy-plus of them — and pulled only the ones actually making drinkware. Vessel and Quiet Golf both partnered with MiiR for insulated stainless bottles. Manors built a minimal Explorer in brushed steel. MARK & LONA went full luxury with a skull-printed tumbler out of Tokyo. And Metalwood Studio tapped cycling culture for a bike-cage bottle that doubles as a walking-round essential.

The spread runs $14 to $55 across golf lifestyle collabs, heritage brands, limited drops, and a couple of golf-specific bottles designed for the course. Bivo's NASA-engineered nozzle, lululemon's straw-lid sport bottle, Dimpled Golf's PGA award-winning golf-ball texture — each one earns its spot. Small accessory, big statement.
