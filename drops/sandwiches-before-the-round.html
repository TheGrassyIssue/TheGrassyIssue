Sandwiches Before the Round

12 Spots Paired With the Closest Muni August 2026

A hot dog at the turn is fine. A gas station breakfast taco is acceptable. But Austin is one of the best sandwich cities in the country, and most of its greatest sandwich shops happen to sit minutes from a municipal golf course. That's not a coincidence you ignore — that's a pre-round ritual waiting to be built.

So here's the pairing guide: twelve sandwiches, matched to the muni they're closest to. Michelin-listed pastrami four minutes from Morris Williams. A Reuben that opens at 10 AM, six from Hancock. A chicken biscuit five minutes from Morris Williams that opens at 7. A fifty-year-old sub shop that sits on Muny's doorstep and puts money toward saving it. Eat first, then go hit it — nobody has ever played well hungry, and nobody has ever regretted the Rachel.

The Pairings — Sandwich × Muni
Closest muni: Jimmy Clay & Roy Kizer · 6 min
Tucci's Southside Subs · Tucci's Turkey · ~$18 full
The South Austin institution — New World Bakery bread baked fresh daily, Boar's Head meats, and subs big enough to split across the front nine and the back. The move is Tucci's Turkey: smoked Cajun turkey, garlic mayo, hot sweet peppers. Order it "Tucci's Way." Two locations: I-35 at William Cannon and Oak Hill.

Closest muni: Morris Williams · 4 min
Mum Foods Smokehouse & Delicatessen · The Rachel · pastrami on rye
Michelin Guide-listed and four minutes from the first tee at Morris Williams. New York Jewish deli meets Central Texas smoke: brined, oak-smoked pastrami and the Rachel — grilled rye, coleslaw instead of kraut, russian dressing. The best pre-round upgrade in Austin golf.
Visit ↗
Closest muni: Hancock · 6 min
Otherside Deli · The Reuben · house pastrami
Pastrami handled entirely in-house — brined, smoked, and steamed — at 1104 W 34th, six minutes from Hancock. Strictly a midday operation: doors open 10 AM on weekdays, which is exactly when you want a Reuben before a lunch loop on the oldest course in Texas.

Closest muni: En route to the southside munis
Home Slice Pizza · Italian sub · half or whole
Everyone knows the South Congress slice line; fewer know Home Slice quietly makes the best Italian sub in the city — ham, dry salami, capicola, genoa, provolone. Get the half unless you want to sleep through your tee time. Perfect grab heading south on Congress toward Clay and Kizer.

Closest muni: Jimmy Clay & Roy Kizer · 8 min
Knuckle Sandwich · The Knuckle · Italian
A trailer outside Ants Beer Cave on St. Elmo slinging the most serious Italian in town — Olympia Provisions mortadella, capicola, and salami, za'atar-dusted bread, Mama Lil's peppers, and a sheet of crispy baked parmesan. The Infatuation's highest-rated sandwich shop in the city, minutes from the southeast munis.
Visit ↗
Closest muni: Hancock & Morris Williams
Allday Pizza · Vodka chicken parm
A Hyde Park post office turned pizza shop with a sandwich menu that threatens the pies: crispy chicken cutlet, vodka sauce, and melted cheese on shatteringly crisp sesame bread. Split the difference between Hancock and Morris Williams and eat it in the parking lot like a professional.
Visit ↗
Closest muni: Jimmy Clay & Roy Kizer · 5 min
Distant Relatives · Beef brisket sandwich
Damien Brockway's modern African American barbecue, five minutes from the Clay/Kizer complex. The brisket sandwich comes on buttery brioche with a rotating pickled topping — when it's the green bean remoulade, cancel your afternoon. The best post-front-nine decision in southeast Austin.
Visit ↗
Closest muni: Lions Municipal · 3 min
ThunderCloud Subs · Lake Austin Blvd · since 1975
Fifty years of fresh, fast and healthy, and the Lake Austin Boulevard shop sits practically on Muny's doorstep. Better still, ThunderCloud is an official supporter of the Muny Conservancy — so the sub you grab before your Lions loop is quietly helping save the course you're about to play. That's the most Austin sentence in this guide.
Visit ↗
Closest muni: Hancock · ~10 min
Little Deli & Pizzeria · Hot pastrami & slaw poor boy
The Crestview corner shop that's been feeding the neighborhood since 1999, on Woodrow Avenue with a live oak out front and a line by noon. The hot pastrami and slaw poor boy is the order — piled stupid-high, dressed like a Reuben's Gulf Coast cousin. Roll down Red River after and you're on the first tee at Hancock.
Visit ↗
Closest muni: Hancock · 8 min
Nervous Charlie's · Pastrami, egg & cheese on an everything bagel
The dawn-patrol pick. Real New York-style bagels — boiled, seeded, chewy — on North Lamar, open early enough for the first tee times of the day. Pastrami, egg and cheese on an everything bagel is the correct order before a 7:30 loop; it eats one-handed while your playing partner is still looking for parking.
Visit ↗
Closest muni: Downtown · 10 min to Lions or Hancock
Meat & Bread · The porchetta · carved to order
The Vancouver cult sandwich counter's Austin outpost, downtown on the Northshore block. One thing done obsessively well: porchetta carved off the roast in front of you, crackling included, salsa verde on a crusty roll. Downtown detour material — ten minutes from either of the old central courses, and you won't regret a single one of them.
Visit ↗
Closest muni: Morris Williams · 5 min
Bird Bird Biscuit · The Queen Beak · chicken biscuit
Technically a biscuit, spiritually a sandwich, undeniably five minutes from Morris Williams. The Queen Beak — crispy fried chicken, bacon-infused chipotle mayo, a drizzle of honey on a biscuit that shatters and melts at the same time. Doors open at 7 AM on Manor Road, which is exactly what an 8 o'clock tee time wants to hear.
