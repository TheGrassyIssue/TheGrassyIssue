Brand to Know — Quiet Golf

quietgolf.com 18 Pieces

Quiet Golf explained: founders Christion Lennon (Museum of Peace & Quiet) and Raul and Diego Diaz, the Costa Mesa flagship, the Old Tom Capital backing, the adidas Samba collab, and what's new in the collection. Based in Costa Mesa, California — flagship store at 2949 Randolph Ave — they built a brand around the idea that golf style should be considered, wearable, and part of a larger rhythm. The owl crest, the QG monogram, the pennant flag: every detail is intentional without being loud.

The range runs from Supima cotton polos made in Portugal to cashmere sweaters, linen trousers, corduroy jackets, and collaborations with Adidas, Seamus, and Smathers & Branson. Old Tom Capital backed them in 2024. Nordstrom, Revolve, and Shopbop carry the line. Eighteen pieces here — from the $25 incense holder to the $380 cashmere cardigan — that show what happens when golf apparel starts with taste instead of tech specs.

Updated, August 2026: since our first post the story got bigger — The Old Ghosts profiled the Costa Mesa flagship this spring, the brand closed a seed round led by Old Tom Capital while promising to protect "the pace," and the collection keeps deepening: the new Monterey Hoodie, Jagger active-pique polo, pleated shorts, and a cable-knit Montecito in bone are below, and the pennant driver cover is back in red. The Story — FAQ
What is Quiet Golf?
A Southern California golf brand built on restraint — muted palettes, minimalist design, and the idea of golf as somewhere you go to disconnect. In the founders' words: “Less performance, more presence.” The range runs from Supima polos and cashmere knits to needlepoint belts and pennant headcovers.
Who founded Quiet Golf?
Christion Lennon — who launched the fashion label Museum of Peace & Quiet in 2019 — co-founded Quiet Golf with brothers Raul and Diego Diaz, carrying that same restrained sensibility into golf. “What felt missing to us was restraint. Not just visually, but in how the game is approached.”
Where is the Quiet Golf store?
The flagship opened in 2023 in Costa Mesa, Orange County — white stucco outside, four skylights inside, a Foresight simulator against the back wall, the headcover range in a custom oak shadowbox painted sage, and a locker room with tartan carpet and a tournament-style QUIET PLEASE sign. “Retail for us is less about transactions and more about context,” Lennon says.
Is Quiet Golf backed by investors?
In October 2024 the brand closed a seed round led by Old Tom Capital. Asked what he's most protective of as it grows, Diego Diaz answered: “The pace… If we lose that, we lose the whole point.”
What was the Quiet Golf x adidas Samba?
A golf version of the adidas Samba built with Quiet Golf — one of the brand's most sought-after drops, now sold out. Other collabs: hand-forged ball markers with Seamus Golf and needlepoint belts with Smathers & Branson (the flag belt is currently in stock).
What's new from Quiet Golf?
As of our August 2026 check: the Monterey Hoodie, the Jagger active-pique polo, pleated shorts in black and taupe, the Monogram Washed Pocket Tee, the Pro Shop Tee, and the Montecito cable-knit in bone — plus the pennant putter and driver covers back in red.
