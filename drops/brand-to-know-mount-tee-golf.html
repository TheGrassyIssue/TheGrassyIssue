Brand to Know: Mount Tee Golf — Not Everybody Has to Look the Same

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 14, 2026

Polos, pleats and headcovers · Melbourne39 picks · all in stock, checked October 14, 2026
The TGI Take

Mount Tee Golf makes the kind of golf clothes you would find in a good vintage shop, then makes them better. Waffle polos with leafy stripes, knit polos with script logos, pleated shorts with a place to keep your tees, and a wall of headcovers in corduroy, waxed canvas, suede, tartan and, new this Melbourne winter, a gray plush checkerboard the brand itself named “This Could Get Ugly.”

Three Friends in Melbourne

The brand’s own story is short. Mount Tee Golf “was created by 3 friends in Melbourne who believe golf is changing and want to see more expression on the course,” and it was built “on the ethos that not everybody has to look the same.” The products have been on sale since late 2022, and they read like a love letter to Australian golf: polos named Parklands and Woodlands, shorts named Peninsula, stripes named after wattle and eucalyptus.

It is a small shop, and some of the oldest pieces are down to one size. All 39 below were in stock on mountteegolf.com.au on October 14, 2026. Prices are in Australian dollars with an approximate US figure; the store shows US prices at checkout but does not publish a US shipping rate.

The Questions

Where is Mount Tee Golf from?

Melbourne, Australia. The brand says it was created by three friends in Melbourne who want to see more expression on the golf course.

What does Mount Tee Golf make?

Knit and waffle golf polos, pleated shorts with built-in tee storage, tees, layers, five-panel caps, and headcovers in plush, corduroy, waxed canvas, houndstooth, suede and tartan.

What is the Mount Tee Golf This Could Get Ugly collection?

The brand's newest drop, from June 2026: a gray and white plush checkerboard headcover set for driver, 3 wood, hybrid and putter, plus a matching tee bag, a Mountaineering Hoody and a Mountain Range cap.

How much does Mount Tee Golf cost?

On October 14, 2026, prices ran from A$12 for bamboo tees to A$130 for the Links Nylon Quarter Zip; polos are A$90 to A$110 and headcovers A$45 to A$80.

Does Mount Tee Golf ship to the US?

Its store shows prices in US dollars and other currencies, but the shipping policy only lists Australian rates, so check the total at checkout.
