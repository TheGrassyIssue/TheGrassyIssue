The Zero Torque Putter Edit — 12 Best for 2026

12 PuttersUpdated Aug 2026

Zero-torque putters are the technology story that's actually changing how people roll the ball. The concept is simple: balance the putter so the face doesn't want to twist during the stroke. The execution is anything but. In 2026 the category went fully mainstream — every major brand now makes one, and they're all approaching it differently.

Updated for August 2026, and doubled to twelve. L.A.B. Golf — the brand that started the whole movement in Eugene, Oregon — leads with the OZ.1i, its insert-faced flagship. The big news since our last pass: Scotty Cameron finally entered the low-torque world with the Phantom OC line, PXG's Bat Attack ZT took a milled-feel benchmark, Ping revived the Scottsdale name with the TEC Ketsch Onset, and SeeMore, Axis1, and a Ballesteros-designed curveball from Spain round out the field. One putter per brand, every price band from $249 to $549 covered. If your putting stroke has a twist problem, the fix might not be lessons — it might be physics.

Zero Torque — FAQ
What is a zero torque putter?
A putter engineered so the face doesn't want to twist during the stroke. By aligning the shaft axis with the head's center of gravity — L.A.B.'s Lie Angle Balance being the original method — the face stays square on its own instead of relying on your hands to time the release.
Do zero torque putters actually work?
For golfers whose misses come from face rotation — pushes, pulls, and inconsistent starts — yes. The tech removes the twist so the face returns square with less effort. Tour adoption has grown every season since L.A.B. broke through, and every major brand now makes one.
Are zero torque putters legal?
Fully USGA and R&A conforming. The balance engineering happens within standard equipment rules — nothing about a zero torque putter is restricted for tournament play.
Which zero torque putter is best for beginners?
The forgiving mallets: L.A.B.'s MEZZ.1 MAX is regarded as one of the most forgiving on the market, and Wilson's Infinite 606 delivers the tech at the friendliest price on this list.
