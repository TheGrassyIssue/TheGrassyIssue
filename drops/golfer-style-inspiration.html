Dress Like Them: Five Golfers, 58 Pieces Inspired by Their Style

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 21, 2026

5 golfers · 58 piecesIn stock when we checked, October 21, 2026
The TGI Take

Most golfers dress like the pro shop told them to. These five didn’t, and you could pick each of them out from the far side of the range. That’s the bar we used: a look so particular that you can build an outfit around it fifty years later, or from the other side of the world.

They make it look like their own. Palmer’s cardigans and Korda’s cargos have nothing in common except that neither looks like a costume. Palmer wore three colors that didn’t match and made it look deliberate. Korda put camo on at a major and made it look like she always had. Wear it with confidence and it reads as style.

You don’t have to copy them. Nobody needs Moe Norman’s pants exactly an inch too short or a sterling AK buckle. Take one idea per golfer: a cardigan over a polo, a mock neck under everything, one loud piece, a white tee and shorts on the range, a second watch. Each section starts with what they actually wore, then gives you a dozen ways in.

We mixed the brands the way they did. Korda, Lee and Palmer’s name are tied to big brands, so Nike, lululemon and Puma are here. Kim now wears Malbon, which started small, and independents from Perth to Stockholm fill in the rest. Everything was in stock when we checked on October 21, 2026.

Nelly Korda

Since moving to Nike in 2023, Korda has stuck to mock necks, clean lines and quiet colors. PGA.com’s fashion expert called it “classy, athletic and graceful — never overdone.” Then at the 2025 AIG Women’s Open at Royal Porthcawl she wore camo cargos and an oversized jacket, and Skratch called it the best golf fit of her career. These picks are menswear, borrowing from both sides of her wardrobe.

Arnold Palmer

Anthony Kim

Moe Norman

The Canadian two-time national amateur champion was golf’s purest ball-striker and one of its oddest dressers. He buttoned long-sleeve shirts to the chin in any heat, wore turtlenecks in summer, liked bright colors and mixed stripes with plaids, and his pants were always an inch too short. He wore two watches at once. He was frugal, too, so plenty of these picks are cheap.

The Questions

What did Nelly Korda wear at the 2025 AIG Women's Open?

Camo cargo pants with Nike tops and an oversized Nike Sportswear jacket at Royal Porthcawl. Skratch called it the best golf fit of her pro career.

What clothing brand does Nelly Korda wear?

Nike, since January 2023. Before that she wore J.Lindeberg. Her clubs are TaylorMade.

Why did Arnold Palmer wear cardigans?

Cardigans and sweaters were standard golf wear in his era, and Palmer made them his own in red, navy, foam green, gray and yellow. The winner of the Arnold Palmer Invitational has received a red cardigan since 2017.

What brand does Min Woo Lee wear?

lululemon. He became a global ambassador in January 2024, and his favorites include the ABC Slim Fit trouser, the Evolution polo and the Soft Jersey Half Zip.

What was on Anthony Kim's belt buckle?

His initials. In 2008 he wore big custom AK buckles that Golf Digest called his bling belt. In 2026 he became an equity partner in Malbon, which sells an AK 1% line.

How did Moe Norman dress?

Long-sleeve shirts buttoned to the chin even in heat, turtlenecks in summer, bright colors with stripes and plaids, and pants that were always a little too short. He also wore two watches.
