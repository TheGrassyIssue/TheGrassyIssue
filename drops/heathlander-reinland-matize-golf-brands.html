Three Fresh Golf Brands, Vol. 2: Heathlander, Reinland Golf Co. and Matize

On Our Radar 31 picks · prices checked November 19, 2026
The TGI Take

Three brands we hadn’t covered before, and three good reasons to look past the pro shop this winter. One makes a shoe and cashmere for golfers who walk. One makes headcovers by hand in a small town in Washington. One makes clothes in Portugal and releases them in small drops with a story behind each one.

Heathlander is named for the heathland courses of southern England. Co-founder Teddy Lynn grew up playing them, at Sunningdale, The Berkshire and Woking; Mark Himmelsbach grew up in a golfing family in Michigan, one of the few US states with real heathland golf. The two run a New York ad agency, and Heathlander is their brand for “proper golfers.” It started with a white golf shoe on a pink sole, the color of the heather, and has grown into Italian cashmere, a fully lined merino wind sweater and a down vest.

Reinland Golf Co. makes headcovers by hand in Walla Walla, Washington. It is a family business, the sister label of Muni Kids, and most covers are made to order with a fleece lining and a hidden elastic. The range runs from Pendleton wool and Harris Tweed to Drew Reinland’s hand-painted plaids, argyles and ocean swirls. It also brought back the furry bag strap covers from the 1980s and ’90s Sunday bag. A new cover lands every few weeks.

Matize was started by three friends from northern Portugal who wanted to make golf feel less stuffy at home. Everything is made in Portugal, and it releases its clothes in named drops, not seasons. The current one, Out of Bounds: Fall in Love, is about the quieter rounds and colder mornings of fall golf, and includes a headcover collab with Mogshade.

Which One Is for You

Heathlander is for the golfer who walks in any weather. Start with The Golf Shoe ($225), then the Wind Sweater ($320). On a budget, the Quilted Down Vest is $99.

Reinland is for the golfer who wants a bag nobody else has. Pick one loud cover for the driver, the Marathon or Figueroa, and a quiet wool for the rest. Everything is $80 to $95.

Matize is for the golfer who goes straight from the course to dinner. Start with the Sunday Overshirt ($159) over an Autumn Stripes Polo ($90). The Mogshade headcover ($89) is the gift.

If you only buy one thing from each: the Heathlander shoe, the Reinland Marathon and the Matize × Mogshade Autumn headcover. Your feet, your driver and your fairway wood, from three brands most people at your course have never heard of.

The Last Word

Walk It, Cover It, Wear It Out

None of these three is trying to be everything. Heathlander makes a shoe and a sweater drawer for golfers who walk. Reinland makes the covers, one at a time. Matize makes the clothes, a drop at a time, in Portugal. Put them together and you have a whole winter kit from three small brands.
