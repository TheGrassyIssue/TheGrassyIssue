Brand to Know — Radry Golf

radry.com 8 Pieces

Tony Knapton runs Radry Golf out of Sheboygan, Wisconsin, and the brand is exactly what happens when an artist decides golf gear is his medium. Every hat, crewneck, and headcover starts as one of Tony's illustrations — hand-drawn, art-school energy, zero corporate polish. The smiley-face logo is becoming one of the most recognizable marks in independent golf, and the brand's gallery arm through White Dot Market sells signed prints alongside $42 dad hats.

The lineup runs from Custodian Hats and Dive Team caps at $42 up through the Contrast Quarter Zip at $120, with the Bugging Blade Cover ($65) and Smoke Em' Ashtray ($55) filling in the weird-in-a-good-way accessories lane. Collabs with Seamus add credibility, but Radry doesn't need borrowed clout. This is a brand that treats golf like a canvas, and the canvas keeps getting bigger.
