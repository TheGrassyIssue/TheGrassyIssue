The Niche Grip Report, Vol. 2

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 12, 2026

Seven grip makers · Vol. 256 boxes · all in stock, checked October 12, 2026
The TGI Take

A new grip is the cheapest way to make an old set feel new. Vol. 1 covered five small grip makers in August. Vol. 2 brings back RIPIT with everything it has launched since, and adds six more: Cloud & Wind, 97golf, Gives Golf, Sweet Rollz, IOMIC and Cadero. Every colorway of a model sits in one box, so swipe through each gallery to see them all.

RIPIT is the art-grip brand of the group, with a 2026 Art Series by three artists and its first putter grips. Cloud & Wind paints blossom, waves and raked sand onto grips in a way nobody else does, all at $18. 97golf is the value play, with grips from $3.99. Gives Golf keeps it small: five grips, one price. Sweet Rollz makes nothing but putter grips, all of them loud. IOMIC is the tacky Japanese grip you have probably seen in a tour bag, and Cadero, from Tokyo, puts the color under a clear skin and installs with air.

Where to start: your wedges. If you are nervous about a loud grip, put it on your wedges first. They are the clubs you look down at most around the green, a fun grip there never fights with a matching driver and irons, and wedges get swapped out more often anyway. If you like it, work up the bag from there.

Getting them installed. You do not need a club fitter. PGA TOUR Superstore lists grip installation from $0.99 a club, and it will put on grips you bring in yourself, not just ones bought in the store. It is often done while you shop, and always within 48 hours of drop-off. Prices vary by location, so call your local store first.

Everything was in stock on October 12, 2026, and only colors you could buy that day are listed.

The Questions

How much are RIPIT grips in US dollars?

RIPIT sells in Australian dollars. On October 12, 2026 its full-swing grips were A$24.95 (about $17) and its square-face putter grips A$49.95 (about $35).

What are the cheapest grips in this list?

97golf's TW97 Tour grips at $3.99 each, followed by its T01 Tour at $7.99 and its J25 Hybrid and T01 Cord at $9.99 (October 12, 2026).

Does Sweet Rollz make full-swing grips?

No. Every Sweet Rollz grip in this list is a putter grip, at $34.95, most in Midsize or Skinny.

How much are Cloud & Wind grips?

Every single Cloud & Wind grip was $18 on October 12, 2026. A 3-grip set was $54, a 7-grip set $126 and 10-grip sets $160.

Where can I get new grips installed cheaply?

PGA TOUR Superstore lists grip installation from $0.99 a club (grips not included) on its regripping page, read October 12, 2026. It will install grips you bring in, often while you wait and always within 48 hours. Prices vary by location.

Which clubs should get a fun grip first?

Your wedges. They are the clubs you look at most around the green, a loud grip there does not clash with the rest of the bag, and wedges get replaced more often anyway.

Where are IOMIC grips made?

IOMIC grips are made in Japan. Prices here are from IOMIC's US store: $14.50 for the Sticky 2.3 on October 12, 2026.
