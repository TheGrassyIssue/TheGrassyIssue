Seven Golf Color Combos to Mix and Match, from Royal Blue + Chalk to Forest + Leaf

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 13, 2026

Seven pairings, hex codes included51 picks · in stock, checked October 13, 2026
The TGI Take

It started with a palette carousel we saved from a design account: seven two-color pairings, each one a dark color next to a soft off-white, each with its hex codes. Royal blue and chalk. Red wine and light sand. Orange and linen. It was made for graphic designers, but it kept reminding us of a golf bag.

That is because golf already dresses in pairs. A polo and trousers. A driver cover and a putter cover. A grip and a glove. The combos that look good on a first tee are almost never three or four colors; they are one strong color and one quiet one, used more than once. So we took the seven pairings and built each one out of things you can buy from independent brands right now: clothes, headcovers, grips and the small things that hang off a bag.

Below, each pairing gets its own swatch with the hex codes, then at least seven pieces: some carry the strong color, some carry the off-white, and a few, like Left of Field’s duckbill cap or RIPIT’s Clay and Cream grip, carry both. At the bottom is a gallery of the brands’ own photos, from the lookbooks and in the wild, so you can see the pairings on people instead of on a screen.

How to Use a Two-Color Combo

Pick one pairing and give the strong color to one or two pieces only, a polo and a headcover, or a hat and a grip. Everything else gets the off-white or a neutral. The second time the strong color shows up, on a towel, a putter cover or a cap, is what makes it look planned instead of accidental. Three times is plenty.

A few names have changed from the original carousel. The two colors it called milk are linen and cream here, and royal blue’s partner is chalk so that cream only appears once. The Questions

What are good color combinations for golf outfits?

Two-color pairings with one strong color and one soft off-white are the easiest to wear: royal blue and chalk, red wine and light sand, orange and linen, plum and cream, midnight indigo and vanilla, cherry and alabaster, or forest and leaf green.

How do I match my headcovers to my outfit?

Pick the strong color from your pairing and use it on one or two headcovers, then let the rest of the bag stay neutral. A grip, towel or cap in the same color ties it together without matching everything.

What is the easiest golf color combo to start with?

Navy and cream. Most golfers already own a navy polo or cap, and cream trousers or an off-white cover finish it. Criquet, Fella, Left of Field and Woodgrain all have pieces for it here.

Where do the hex codes come from?

From a palette carousel of seven two-color pairings by a graphic design account. We kept its hex codes and renamed the two colors it called milk as linen and cream, and called royal blue's partner chalk.

How much do these pieces cost?

On October 13, 2026 they ran from $14.50 (IOMIC grips) to US $388 (Late Nine's cable knit polo), with most between $18 and $130.
