The Towel Edit, Vol. 2

18 Pieces

Eighteen towels from fifteen brands, and every one of them would look better clipped to your bag than whatever came free with your last driver purchase. Sentinel Golf leads with two Japanese-made options — the Shinto Gauze and the Kontex — that prove the towel category has room for actual craftsmanship. Fyfe Golf turns vintage Guinness bar towels into one-of-a-kind links accessories in a workshop in Scotland. And Vessel's magnetic towel set the standard that half this list is now chasing.

The spread runs £25 to $60 across heritage artisan towels, streetwear culture plays, international imports, bold prints, premium designer pieces, and magnetic tech. No overlap with Vol. 1. Small accessory, massive personality differentiator.
