Brand to Know — Cloud & Wind Golf

cloudandwindgolf.com $18/grip Pacific Summer Now Live

Cloud & Wind Golf makes one thing — grips — and they make them differently. Every design draws from traditional Eastern aesthetics and Pacific Coast nature. The Blossom series pulls from floral motifs and Chinese poetry. The Cloud & Wave collection captures the mood of twilight rounds on the California coast. The Zen strips everything back to texture and tone. Each grip ships with a line of Chinese verse printed along the shaft — a detail most brands would never think to include.

The construction is straightforward: natural and synthetic rubber blend, Standard+ sizing with a gentle taper, 56–58 grams, rated for 60 rounds. At $18 a grip, they're priced below the big names but built with more intention than anything in the pro shop. Bundles drop the per-grip cost further — a 3-grip set runs $50, a 7-grip set is $112. The new Pacific Summer collection just landed with three colorways inspired by June Gloom mornings and SoCal sunsets. This is a grip brand for golfers who care about what they touch 50+ times a round.

The Story — FAQ
What is Cloud & Wind Golf?
Cloud & Wind Golf is a grip brand that makes one thing and makes it differently. Every design draws from traditional Eastern aesthetics and Pacific Coast nature — floral Blossom motifs, twilight Cloud & Wave colorways, stripped-back Zen textures — with a line of Chinese verse printed along each shaft.
Are Cloud & Wind golf grips any good?
The construction is legitimate: a natural and synthetic rubber blend, Standard+ sizing with a gentle taper, 56–58 grams, rated for roughly 60 rounds. They're built with more intention than most pro-shop grips at a lower price than the big names.
How much do Cloud & Wind grips cost?
$18 per grip, with bundles that bring the cost down — a 3-grip set runs $50 and a 7-grip set is $112.
Where can I buy Cloud & Wind grips?
Directly from cloudandwindgolf.com. New collections drop seasonally — the Pacific Summer series is the latest, with colorways inspired by June Gloom mornings and SoCal sunsets.
What's the story behind Cloud & Wind Golf?
In the brand's own words, “We dig back into our heritage and look for inspiration.” Cloud & Wind Golf brings traditional Eastern design elements into the golf bag — inspired by nature, rooted in heritage, and crafted with passion. Rather than chase logos, the team builds grips around a feeling: capturing the beauty of the course so you can carry it in your hands.
What inspires each grip design?
The brand says the best part of golf is “the privilege to immerse yourself in the nature on the course,” so every colorway captures a specific moment. Cloud & Wave traces the Pacific horizon where the sky meets the ocean. The Blossom: After the Rain marks the moment the grey clears and the flower remains. The Zen Grip is built for the bunker — a reminder to find calm in precision: hit the shot, rake the sand, move on. Palace Wall relives blooming branches against red palace walls in early spring.
How are Cloud & Wind grips built?
Every grip uses a natural and synthetic rubber blend for touch, performance, and durability, in Standard+ sizing — a slightly wider diameter with a gentle taper toward the lower hand for a soft, relaxed handle. They weigh roughly 56–60 grams depending on the design, are rated for about 60 rounds, and can be built up to mid-size by adding three wraps of tape.
