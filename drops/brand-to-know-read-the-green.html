Brand to Know — Read The Green

June 15, 2026 Brand to Know 12 Pieces

Read The Green started as three words shouted at a buddy who missed the pin on a miniature golf course. Four lifelong friends turned that inside joke into a full lifestyle brand — premium hats and apparel that move between the course and the city without trying too hard. Their Founder's Cap is the signature piece, a 5-panel corduroy snapback with embroidered details and a custom interior lining, but the clothing line has taken on a life of its own.

The '97 Vintage Pullover went viral and put RTG on the map — a lightweight nylon layer with rib-knit cuffs and a drawstring waist that works on the back nine or a Saturday morning coffee run. The Grounds Crew Jacket and Oversized Knit Sweater pushed the brand into premium territory, while the Varsity Crewneck and Club Tee keep things accessible. Cody Bellinger, Jasson Domínguez, and Lexie Hull all wear RTG, but the brand still feels like it belongs to the weekend golfer who cares about what they put on. That's the point.

The Story — FAQ
What is Read The Green?
Read The Green (RTG) is a premium hat and apparel brand started by four lifelong friends — the name comes from three words shouted at a buddy who missed the pin on a mini golf course. The gear moves between the course and the city without trying too hard.
What is the Read The Green Founder's Cap?
The brand's signature piece: a 5-panel corduroy snapback with embroidered details and a custom interior lining. It's the hat that put RTG's hat program on the map.
What is the '97 Vintage Pullover?
The piece that went viral — a lightweight nylon layer with rib-knit cuffs and a drawstring waist that works on the back nine or a Saturday coffee run. The Grounds Crew Jacket and Oversized Knit Sweater followed it into premium territory.
Who wears Read The Green?
The As Seen On roster runs from MLB — Cody Bellinger, Jasson Domínguez, Merrill Kelly, Austin Pope — to Lexie Hull of the Indiana Fever and NFL veterans C.J. Mosley and Tyron Smith. But the brand still feels like it belongs to the weekend golfer.
Where did the name Read The Green come from?
Boardwalk miniature golf. Four lifelong friends, one buddy who missed the pin, three words shouted at him — a name over a decade in the making before it became a brand. RTG says the phrase now means taking every obstacle in stride and letting adversity point you toward the next shot, on the course and off.
Who runs Read The Green?
The same four friends who started it. Customer service emails are answered directly by a founder, and the house rule on product is simple: if they wouldn’t wear it themselves, they won’t sell it.
What’s new from Read The Green in 2026?
The Grounds Crew Jacket in green and tan, corduroy Founder’s Caps in Midnight, Espresso, Golden Hour, and Brownstone, mid-profile Club Caps named for New York neighborhoods — Brooklyn, Little Italy — and short-sleeve versions of the viral ’97 Vintage Pullover for summer.
