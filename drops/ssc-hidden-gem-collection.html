Sugarloaf Social Club — The Hidden Gem Collection

August 11, 2026 Drop 15 Pieces · $15–$850

Sugarloaf Social Club dropped the Hidden Gem collection this morning, and it is a very SSC list: an $850 MacKenzie bag sitting next to a $16 frisbee.

That range is the whole brand. SSC describes itself as “a beacon for golf nerds” chasing “a healthy mix of high and low combined with the ever-active hunt for that next golf adventure.” Hidden Gem is that sentence turned into product — USA-made wool headcovers and a Portland-built leather bag on one end, a Nalgene and a folding camp chair on the other. It reads less like a golf collection and more like packing for a weekend where golf happens to be involved.

They run it out of McLean, Virginia. Three pieces sold out on day one. Everything below is what dropped, at what it costs, with notes on what’s already gone.

Who SSC Is

Sugarloaf Social Club runs out of McLean, Virginia, and describes itself as “a beacon for golf nerds with the singular focus of delivering joy to our global community through quality goods, conversational content and creative activations.” Which is a lot of words for: they make good stuff and they’re funny about it.

The product copy is half the appeal. The knit headcovers are sold with a line about your wife being annoyed at another SSC package showing up. The license plate cover is a joke about slow play in traffic. The tech shorts are recommended on the grounds that a guy named Ian wears his every day. Nobody else in golf writes like this.

The manufacturing is more serious than the tone suggests. Bags from MacKenzie in Portland. Headcovers from Fore Ewe in American wool. Coolers from Hudson Sutler. A scorecard holder from Winston Collection. Irons hand-ground by Don White. They partner with the right small makers and then refuse to be precious about any of it.

They’ll also mail you free stickers if you send a stamped, self-addressed envelope to their McLean address. That is the brand in one sentence.
