The Pre-Round Pour — 18 Austin Coffee Shops for Golfers

18 Shops Institutions, Roasters, New Wave Austin, TX

Austin was named the No. 1 coffee city in America by Food & Wine in 2026. That’s not a surprise if you’ve been paying attention — the city runs on independent roasters, trailer setups, and patios that double as community hubs. For golfers, the pre-round coffee stop is as essential as the first tee warm-up.

Eighteen independent Austin coffee shops organized three ways: the institutions that built Austin’s coffee culture, the roasters doing the most serious bean work in the state, and the new wave spots rewriting what a coffee shop can be. No chains. No franchises. Just local roasters and neighborhood spots that feel like Austin.

Eighteen shops, one rule: get there before the first group goes off.

The Guide — 18 Pre-Round Pours
The Institutions
Radio Coffee & Beer
South Lamar / East Austin
Voted Austin’s best coffee shop 10 years running. Three locations, all opening at 7 AM. Espresso bar, food trucks, live music on a massive patio. 15 minutes from Jimmy Clay.
Visit Radio Coffee ↗
Jo’s Coffee
South Congress
The iconic SoCo patio and home of the “I love you so much” mural. Austin original since 1999. Iced turbo and breakfast tacos at 7 AM. Ten minutes to Roy Kizer.
Visit Jo’s Coffee ↗
Caffé Medici
Clarksville / West Campus / S. Lamar
Roasting since 2006. The Clarksville original is a converted house on West Lynn — quintessential old Austin. Ten minutes from Lions Municipal.
Visit Caffé Medici ↗
Mozart’s Coffee Roasters
Lake Austin
300-seat deck overlooking Lake Austin. Flame-roasted on-site every 48 hours. Live music Thursday through Sunday. Open 7 AM to midnight. The view alone is the ritual.
Visit Mozart’s ↗
Cuvée Coffee
Downtown (East Ave)
Texas’ first craft coffee roaster, est. 1998. Pioneers of nitro cold brew. Super-premium beans sourced direct from farmers. Nearly three decades of Austin coffee history.
Visit Cuvée ↗
Flightpath Coffeehouse
Hyde Park (Duval St)
Old Austin institution at 51st and Duval. Shaded patio, no live music, great cold brew. The anti-scene coffee shop. Perhaps the only quiet public space in Austin. 7 AM daily.
Visit Flightpath ↗
The Roasters
Desnudo Coffee
East Austin / Downtown / S. Lamar / W. 6th
Direct-trade Colombian beans roasted locally. Four locations, single-origin micro lots, tasting notes that change weekly. Opens 7 AM. The most serious coffee on this list.
Visit Desnudo ↗
Greater Goods Coffee Co.
Dripping Springs
Micro Roaster of the Year (Roast Magazine). Scandinavian-inspired warehouse, charitable mission. A short detour if you’re heading to courses west of town.
Visit Greater Goods ↗
Houndstooth Coffee
North Lamar / Downtown / East / Domain
Pioneer of Austin specialty coffee. Four locations, barista-driven, 6:30 AM weekday opens. The shop that raised the bar for every roaster that followed.
Visit Houndstooth ↗
Figure 8 Coffee Purveyors
East Austin (Chicon St)
Roasts their own beans on-site. Industrial-chic space, pour-over focused, natural wine selection. Texas and West Coast roasts in an East Austin storefront.
Visit Figure 8 ↗
Merit Coffee
Mueller / Seaholm / Triangle / S. Lamar
San Antonio-born specialty roaster. Sources 25+ single origins per year from Central America, South America, and Africa. Barista-led experience across four Austin locations.
Visit Merit ↗
Barrett’s Coffee
North Austin (St Johns Ave)
Fifteen-plus years in Austin. Small-batch roaster with free next-day home delivery. Community cuppings, barista education, sustainable sourcing. Open 7 AM daily.
Visit Barrett’s ↗
The New Wave
Cosmic Coffee + Beer Garden
South Austin (Pickle Rd)
Permaculture garden with fire pits, koi pond, and Leroy & Lewis BBQ truck. Coffee to cocktails in a single compound. Dog-friendly. The best outdoor hang in South Austin.
Visit Cosmic ↗
Terrible Love
Hyde Park (Avenue B)
Tucked in a 1911 schoolhouse boiler room. Named after a song by The National. The “trrbl latte” is famous. Greenery-wrapped walkway, shaded benches. 7:30 AM weekdays.
Visit Terrible Love ↗
Civil Goat Coffee
Westlake (Cuernavaca)
Hidden gem in West Lake Hills. Pygmy goat mascot named Butters. The Cuernavaca original has that tucked-away neighborhood energy. Multiple Austin locations now.
Visit Civil Goat ↗
Ani’s Day & Night
East Riverside
Coffee-to-cocktails in a remodeled house. Big patio, food trucks, art shows, natural wines. Community-centered and dog-friendly. The all-day neighborhood spot.
Visit Ani’s ↗
Lau Lau
North Austin (Guadalupe)
Asian American specialty coffee rooted in Chinese heritage. Mala Latte, Chinese Five Spice Cortado, dim sum pastries, Mahjong nights. The most creative menu in Austin coffee.
Visit Lau Lau ↗
Flora Coffee & Culture
North Central (Anderson Ln)
Inside Retreat yoga studio. Locally roasted, ethical sourcing, used book wall. Horchata latte and Cajeta Crème Brûlée Capp. Opens 7 AM. Calm energy before the round.
Visit Flora ↗
