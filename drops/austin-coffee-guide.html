The Pre-Round Pour — 5 Austin Coffee Shops to Hit Before Your Tee Time

5 Shops July 23, 2026 Austin, TX

Austin was named the No. 1 coffee city in America by Food & Wine in 2026. That’s not a surprise if you’ve been paying attention — the city runs on independent roasters, trailer setups, and patios that double as community hubs. For golfers, the pre-round coffee stop is as essential as the first tee warm-up.

We picked five independent Austin coffee shops that open early enough for a dawn patrol tee time and serve coffee good enough to make the drive part of the ritual. No chains. No franchises. Just local roasters and neighborhood spots that feel like Austin.

Five shops, one rule: get there before the first group goes off.

The Guide — 5 Pre-Round Pours
Radio Coffee & Beer
South Lamar / East Austin
Voted Austin’s best coffee shop 10 years running. Three locations — South, East, and Rosewood — all opening at 7 AM daily. Espresso bar, local craft beers, food trucks, and live music on a massive patio. The wood-covered cabin feel and laid-back energy make it the ideal staging ground before a morning tee time at Jimmy Clay or Roy Kizer, both 15 minutes south.
Visit Radio Coffee →
Desnudo Coffee
East Austin / Downtown / S. Lamar / W. 6th
Started as a single trailer, now four Austin locations — all serving direct-trade Colombian beans roasted locally. Founded by Juan and Sergio with a mission to work directly with small coffee farmers. The East Austin original on Webberville opens at 7 AM weekdays. Pure, single-origin micro lots with tasting notes that change weekly. The most serious coffee on this list, and the most personal.
Visit Desnudo →
Caffé Medici
Clarksville / West Campus / S. Lamar
Roasting in Austin since 2006. The Clarksville original — a converted house on West Lynn — is the quintessential old Austin coffee experience. Comfy seating inside and out, tucked on a quiet street. Multiple locations maintain consistent quality, and the Clarksville Blend is a staple. Ten minutes from Lions Municipal, Austin’s most storied muni course.
Visit Caffé Medici →
Jo’s Coffee
South Congress
The iconic SoCo patio and home of the “I love you so much” mural. Keeping Austin running since 1999 with hot coffee, breakfast tacos, and a corner spot that defines the neighborhood. The South Congress location opens at 7 AM daily. Tourist-famous, yes — but the iced turbo and the patio energy are the real deal. Multiple Austin locations plus Houston and Nashville.
Visit Jo’s Coffee →
Greater Goods Coffee Co.
Dripping Springs
Founded in 2015 by Khanh Trang and Trey Cobb, Greater Goods was named Micro Roaster of the Year by Roast Magazine. The warehouse space features Scandinavian-inspired design — light, bright, and built around a serious roasting program. Every cup supports their charitable mission. A short detour if you’re heading to courses west of town, and the purest cup on this list.
Visit Greater Goods →
