The Summer Practice Guide

Austin, TX 10 Spots Updated July 2026

Austin summers are not a suggestion. They are 105 degrees with humidity that makes your grips feel like wet rope. The easy move is to put the clubs away until October. The better move is to know where and when to practice so you come out of the heat with a game that’s sharper than it was in April.

This is a guide to every serious practice option in the Austin metro — outdoor ranges with grass tees and lights, the best short game facility in town, and the indoor simulator spots where you can work on your game in air conditioning. Plus the timing and hydration strategies that make summer practice sustainable instead of miserable.

Beat the Heat

Before we get to where — the when matters more in July and August. The heat index in Austin regularly breaks 110 between noon and 4pm. That’s not practice weather. That’s hospital weather.

Summer Practice Rules

Tee times before 11am or after 5pm. Temperatures drop 10–15°F outside peak hours. Dawn patrol range sessions are the move — most facilities open at 7am.

Hydrate the night before. By the time you’re thirsty on the range, you’re already behind. Drink water the evening before and morning of. On the range, aim for 8oz every 15 minutes.

Light colors, performance fabrics. Cotton holds heat. Polyester blends with UPF protection are your friend. A wide-brim hat beats a baseball cap.

Twilight range sessions. Jimmy Clay’s lit range stays open until 9pm. Hitting balls as the sun drops behind the tree line is one of the best feelings in Austin golf.

Go inside. When the heat advisory hits, the simulators are not a consolation prize. TrackMan and Full Swing give you real data in 72-degree air. Use June through September to dial in your numbers.

Driving Ranges

Austin’s outdoor range options run from no-frills muni setups to full-service practice complexes. The best ones have grass tees, lights for evening sessions, and enough space to actually work on your game without feeling rushed.

Round Rock Driving Range
Round Rock · Lit Range til 10:30pm
A 35-acre practice facility just north of Austin on Greenlawn Blvd in Round Rock. Fully lit with Musco high-output stadium lights — open until 10:30pm every night, which makes it the best after-dark range in the metro. Long range, short range, wedge range with target greens, chipping green, and putting green. A mobile 104ft Covershot Canopy lets you hit off grass in the shade any time of day. Well priced and well maintained.
Fully Lit $10–$20 / bucket Open til 10:30pm

Jimmy Clay / Roy Kizer
SE Austin · Lit Range
The muni range that serves both courses on the south side. Grass tees when conditions allow, mats as backup. Big putting green, chipping area, pitching green, sand trap, and a 4-hole practice course. The lights stay on until 9pm — the best twilight range session in Austin. Cheapest serious range in town.
Lit til 9pm $7–$11 / bucket 4-Hole Short Course

Falconhead Golf Club
West Austin · Full Practice Complex
Championship-level practice setup attached to a PGA Tour-designed 18-hole course near Lakeway. Grass and mat hitting stations, chipping area with a practice bunker, and a large putting green. Five teeing options on the course from 5,170 to 7,181 yards. Hit a bucket, then play 18 on one of the best public tracks in the metro.

Three-level covered range with misters and Toptracer data in every bay — carry, ball speed, launch angle, spin. The data is legitimately useful for dialing in distances. Full bar and kitchen. Best use case: evening sessions when you want numbers without the sun, or when non-golf friends want to come along. Half-price Tuesdays.

Short Game

The fastest way to drop strokes in the summer is to stop hitting driver on the range and go work on your wedges and putter. Austin has one facility that does this better than anywhere else in central Texas.

Butler Pitch & Putt
Downtown · 9-Hole Par 3
Austin’s most beloved par-3 course, tucked between Zilker Park and Lady Bird Lake. An original since 1949. Nine holes from 60 to 110 yards — pure wedge and putter work on real greens. Large practice putting green for pre-round warm-up. No tee times, no dress code, first-come first-play. The best place in Austin to sharpen your short game without the pressure of a full round. PGA instruction available with Nick Pateman.
Walk-Up 60–110 Yards 8am–8pm Daily Practice Green
Visit ↗

Round Rock Driving Range’s chipping green and putting green also deserve a mention here. And the putting green at Jimmy Clay is solid for a quick session before or after hitting the range. But for dedicated short game work on real grass with real breaks, Butler is the spot.

Indoor Simulators

This is where summer practice gets interesting. When it’s 107 outside, the simulator spots aren’t a backup plan — they’re the primary facility. TrackMan and Full Swing give you tour-level data on every swing: club speed, ball speed, launch angle, spin rate, carry distance. You can play 100+ courses, run skill challenges, or just groove your 7-iron in air conditioning. Austin has more indoor golf per capita than almost any city in Texas.

Golfinity
NW Austin · 22 Bays
The biggest indoor golf facility in Austin. 20,000 square feet across two stories with 22 simulator bays running TrackMan, Foresight, Uneekor, and GEARS motion capture. Play 100+ courses including St. Andrews, Pebble Beach, and Bandon Dunes. Full bar and restaurant. PGA coaching on staff. Leagues and community events. The serious practice destination when it’s too hot for grass.
22 Bays TrackMan + Foresight Full Restaurant

Southside Golf Co
South Austin · TrackMan 4
Small, serious, and run by a PGA Professional. Southside uses TrackMan 4 — the same tour-trusted radar system used by 90 of the top 100 players in the world. Private lessons, leagues, club repair, and same-day regripping. No country club pressure. Built for golfers who want real data in a relaxed space. Near Manchaca, Circle C, and Buda.

Back Nine Golf
3 Locations · 24/7
Three locations across Austin — East, Southwest, and Westlake — all open 24 hours a day, seven days a week. No membership required. Book a bay online, show up, and play. Rental clubs available. The 24/7 access is the differentiator — early morning sessions before work, late night range sessions after dinner. Whatever your schedule allows.

The Old Muni

Lions Municipal deserves its own mention. The practice area is modest — an irons-only, mats-only range and a putting green — but the course itself is a short game masterclass. Tight fairways, mature oaks, small greens. Opened in 1928, it’s the oldest municipal course in Texas. Ben Hogan, Byron Nelson, Tom Kite, and Ben Crenshaw all played here. If you want to practice course management and creative shot-making on a real layout, this is your classroom. Walk-on rates are the lowest in town.

Lions Municipal Golf Course
Central Austin · Est. 1928
The oldest muni in Texas. Eighteen holes through mature live oaks near downtown on Enfield Road. The range is irons-only and mats-only, but the course is the real practice tool — tight windows, creative wedge shots, and greens that demand touch. Walk-on twilight rounds in the summer are some of the best golf value in Austin.
Irons Only Range Est. 1928 Central Location

Summer Practice Plan

Dawn patrol (6–10am): Outdoor range at Round Rock Driving Range or Jimmy Clay. Grass tees, real sun, reasonable temps. Focus on full swing.

Midday (11am–4pm): Go inside. Golfinity, Southside, Back Nine, or Austin Indoor Golf. Work on swing data, play a simulated round, or take a lesson in AC.

Twilight (5–9pm): Jimmy Clay’s lit range or a quick 9 at Butler Pitch & Putt as the temperature drops. Short game focus.

Weekend: Early tee time at Lions or Falconhead, followed by a cold beer. Don’t overthink it.
