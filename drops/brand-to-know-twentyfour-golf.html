Brand to Know — TwentyFour Golf

twentyfourgolf.com 12 Pieces

TwentyFour Golf was born on the sun-drenched east coast of Australia by a founder who believes golf shouldn’t be bound by rigid traditions. The name captures a simple idea: the game doesn’t end when you walk off the 18th. From early tee times to post-round pints, it’s a lifestyle — and the apparel should move with you through all of it.

The lineup is tight but deliberate. The Motion Polo is the centrepiece: a custom fabric blend of 92% recycled polyester and 8% spandex with four-way stretch, UPF 50+ sun protection, heat-bonded seams, and colour-lock dye. The Clubhouse Pants pair nylon-spandex with a water-repellent finish for a silhouette that works on the course and after. Their SignatureFit 5-panel cap — short brim, structured front, low crown — has been refined over time into something that sits right out of the box. Always In Play.

Updated, August 2026: a stock-check reality: the caps keep selling out. Most OG Cap colorways are gone, so we've trimmed the collection below to what you can actually buy today and added the two newest in-stock pieces — the TFG Sports Cap in white and the Don't Blame Your Clubs cap in black. The AW26 drop is live, with a plaid-collar quarter-zip and a houndstooth cap teased on Instagram. Australians in the comments were right: get the cap when you see it.

The Story — FAQ
What is TwentyFour Golf?
An Australian golf apparel brand born on the country's sun-drenched east coast, built on the idea that the game doesn't end when you walk off the 18th — hence the name, and the tagline: Always In Play. In the founder's words, golf "shouldn't be bound by rigid traditions. It should embrace individuality, creativity, and fun."
What is the Motion Polo?
The centrepiece of the range: a custom blend of 92% recycled polyester and 8% spandex with four-way stretch, UPF 50+ sun protection, heat-bonded seams, and colour-lock dye. About $65 USD (A$100), in Midnight Navy and Storm Green.
Why do the caps keep selling out?
The SignatureFit 5-panel — short brim, structured front, low crown — is the brand's calling card, and as of our August 2026 stock check most OG Cap colorways (Black, Red, Camo, Woodland, Tour, both Signatures) are gone. What's actually buyable: the Dune Trucker, the TFG Sports Cap in white, and the Don't Blame Your Clubs cap in black. When a restock hits, don't deliberate.
Where does TwentyFour Golf ship?
Worldwide, with AUD pricing at checkout — US buyers pay roughly two-thirds of the AUD sticker. Free shipping over A$150 within Australia and New Zealand.
What's in the AW26 collection?
The AW26 drop carries the Motion Polos, Clubhouse Pants, Thermal Digital Camo, and the cap line — with new pieces teased on Instagram, including a quarter-zip with a plaid collar and a houndstooth cap. Both are in the In the Wild grid above.
