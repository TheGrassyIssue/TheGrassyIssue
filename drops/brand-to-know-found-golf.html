Brand to Know — Found Golf

found.golf 5 Pieces

Melbourne's Found Golf treats the course like a studio and the dress code like a memo it never received. Fashion-first golf from a city that already knows how to blend streetwear with fine art.

The Box Par Tee is the entry point — a heavyweight graphic shirt that reads like an art-school print, not tournament merch. The Daisy Chain Tee runs the same energy. The Reusable Ciggie Box is actually a tee holder, which is the most Found Golf thing imaginable. Dad cap in olive and The Shiz Vest round out a tight five-piece lineup. Everything priced in AUD, shipped from Australia, but the aesthetic is universal: golf gear for people who went to art school.
