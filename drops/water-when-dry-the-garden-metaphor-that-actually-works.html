Water When Dry: The Garden Metaphor That Actually Works

waterwhendry.com $15 – $128 8 Pieces

Water When Dry is built on a garden metaphor — what you water will grow — and somehow it's not annoying. Colorways named River, Dusk, Leaf, Soil, Blackberry. Membership called the Garden Club. It should tip into self-help territory, but the clothes are too good for that.

The Camper Short at $88 is the entry point and the piece that shows up in every TGI roundup for a reason. Canopy Zip Hoodie in Leaf and Charcoal. Canopy Fleece in Blackberry for cold mornings. The April Showers Tee and Greener Grass Tee bring graphic energy without trying too hard. $15 to $128, a tight product range, and a brand voice that's wholesome without being corny. Harder than it sounds.
