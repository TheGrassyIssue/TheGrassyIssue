The Home Golf Decor Edit — 18 Picks for the 19th Hole

14 Brands 18 Picks $14–$945

The best golf homes don't announce themselves. No neon "19th Hole" signs, no plastic putting greens draped across the living room, no dusty trophies from the 2014 member-guest. The best golf homes whisper. A hand-bound Assouline book on the coffee table. A soy candle that smells like April in Augusta. A wooden bag stand in the mudroom that looks like it came from a furniture showroom, not a sporting goods aisle. Golf decor, done right, is just good decor.

We pulled 18 picks across six categories — storage and organization, indoor putting, wall art, coffee table books, barware, and display pieces. The range runs from a $14 Dibdaub illustrated course print to the $945 Assouline Impossible Collection, delivered in a handcrafted case with white gloves. In between: the PrimePutt tour-quality mat that stimps like a real green, Par × Design's gallery-grade course prints, Fairway Candle Co.'s Birdie Box (three course-inspired scents hand-poured in Toronto), and Vagabond House's decanter set with a 3D golf ball stopper that belongs on any real home bar.

Eighteen picks, fourteen brands, one goal: make your home look like it belongs to someone who plays, not someone who cosplays.
