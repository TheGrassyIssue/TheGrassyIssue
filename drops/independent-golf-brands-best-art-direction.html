13 Independent Golf Brands With the Best Art Direction

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 17, 2026

13 brands · 39 piecesPrices checked October 17, 2026
The TGI Take

This is why we cover independent golf. A small brand has no committee standing between the person with the idea and the thing you end up holding, so you get a point of view: a logo lifted from a 1938 strobe photograph, a grip with poetry printed on it, a cap rebuilt from an old St Andrews cap found in a thrift shop in Vietnam. That design language is half the fun of playing now. It turns up in the bag, on the first tee and in conversation with whoever you’re paired with.

The ones that stand out commit to a single idea and carry it everywhere. Kingfisher’s founder came from PepsiCo’s creative department, and you can see it in the cigarette-box tee pack and campaign images lit like film stills. Forden built a whole range around one historical photograph. Whim took a golf object to Milan Design Week. Fyfe and Mogshade let the material lead, from Harris Tweed and old golf jackets to rescued mountain wool, so every piece comes with a story about where it came from. None of them looks like anything in the pro shop, and none of them looks like each other.

Where is it going? Look at what these brands sell besides clothes. Found sells sculpture and a bench. Mogshade makes hand-loomed wool posters. Hidden Links has a skateboard deck, and Whim sells a concrete paperweight. Golf brands are starting to make things for the house as well as the course, and we expect more of it, along with more one-of-one pieces cut from old garments, more artist collaborations and more brands throwing their own events the way SLICE TOWN does in Copenhagen. The game is becoming a reason to make things, and the independents are getting there first.

These 13 do it best, in the pictures they take, the graphics they draw and the objects they make. Each gets a short take, three of its own photos and three pieces, priced on October 17, 2026.

The Questions

Which independent golf brands have the best art direction?

Our 13 for 2026 are Left of Field, SLICE TOWN, Sentinel Golf and Mogshade for photography; Kingfisher Golf, Forden Golf, Radry, Hidden Links Society and Puttwell for graphics; and Whim Golf, Found Golf, Cloud & Wind and Fyfe Golf for product and object design.

How did TGI choose these brands?

On lookbooks and photography, graphics and logos, and product design. Only independent brands qualified, and we left out any brand featured in our ten most recent posts to keep the list fresh.

What is the story behind the Forden Golf logo?

Forden's Swingman logo comes from Doc Edgerton's 1938 strobe photograph of a golfer at MIT, which captured one swing as dozens of overlapping figures.

Which golf brand is run by a former art director?

Kingfisher Golf in Dallas. Founder Fiona Cohen was an art director at PepsiCo, specializing in animation and motion design, before starting the brand.

What is the cheapest way into these brands?

Kingfisher's tee box at $9, a Cloud & Wind grip at $18, or a Hidden Links Society Play Faster cap at $37, as of October 17, 2026.
