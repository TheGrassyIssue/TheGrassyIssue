Brand to Know: Radmor

By Lenny Harrington · Updated November 3, 2026

Brand to Know · Seattle, WA54 picks · in stock, checked November 3, 2026
The TGI Take

Radmor is a Seattle golf brand started by Scott Morrison and Bob Conrad, who played on the University of Washington golf team together. The name is half of each of their last names. Morrison came from denim: he founded Paper Denim & Cloth in 1999 and later Ernest Sewn, so he knew how clothes are made long before he made golf clothes.

The idea. Most golf clothing is polyester, which Conrad calls an oil-based plastic. Radmor set out to make performance golf clothes without virgin polyester: Peruvian Pima cotton, Tencel and other plant-based fibers, with only a small amount of recycled polyester or nylon where it is needed.

Why it stands out now. Look past the polos and Radmor has quietly become one of the best knitwear brands in golf. The Whitman, Langley and Inness knit polos, the RADMerino cashmere-blend sweaters and the Enzo knit bomber look like they belong in a menswear store, and they still move like golf clothes. Morrison put it simply: fall is right in Radmor’s wheelhouse.

The fun side. Then there is BobRad, a dancing mascot that turns up on leather headcovers, keychains and a record slipmat, plus a comic-book Pop-Art headcover set, a Golf Needle collab for Seattle’s 12th Man and two Bauhaus tees. The clothes are serious; the accessories are not.

Where to start. A Taylor polo is the easy entry at $115. If you want to see what Radmor does best, buy a knit polo. If you just want a little of the brand in your bag, the BobRad headcovers and the $22 keychain do the job.

What is in here. 54 pieces, all in stock on radmorgolf.com when we checked on November 3, 2026, in US dollars. A few headcovers and the socks were on sale; we list both prices.

The Last Word

Seattle, in Knit

Plenty of brands talk about sustainability. Radmor built its whole line around one rule, no virgin polyester, and then made the clothes good enough that you would buy them anyway. The knits are the proof.

Radmor has turned up on TGI before, in our technical brands roundup and summer tee edit. This is the first time we have given it the full page.
