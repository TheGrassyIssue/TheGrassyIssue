Aimé Leon Dore SS26 Golf: Croc-Embossed FootJoys and a Sweater Your Grandfather Would Approve Of

aimeleondore.com $40–$495 8 Pieces

ALD's third annual golf capsule dropped April 23 and it's the most complete yet. The headliner is the ALD × FootJoy Premiere Series Marquis in croc-embossed leather — $375, two colorways, waterproof, with a custom co-branded insole that goes harder than it needs to. Blackwatch plaid runs through a lightweight Tips windbreaker ($495) and a Cordura pullover ($295).

The Clubhouse Sweater ($395) is 100% merino with an intarsia-knit golfer on the chest — the kind of piece that makes you look like you belong at a club even if you don't have a membership. Leather-and-Cordura putter cover, performance socks, a flask, and a cigar lighter round out the accessories. Three years in, Teddy Santis keeps treating golf like it's always been part of the culture.
