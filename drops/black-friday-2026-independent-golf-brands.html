Black Friday 2026: The Best Deals From Independent Golf Brands

The Edit 42 brands · 126 picks · deals checked November 21, 2026
The TGI Take

Black Friday is the one week a year when the small golf brands we cover put real money on the table, and most of them do it quietly, in a banner at the top of their own site. So we went and read the banners. On November 21 we checked every independent brand in the TGI index with a website, and 42 of them had a Black Friday deal live or announced.

This is that list, with the big names left out. For each brand you get the deal in one line, any code, the end date where they gave one, and three marked-down pieces we would actually buy. Prices are from each store that morning; non-US brands show their own currency with a rough dollar figure.

The biggest numbers: Puttwell at 60% off sitewide, Mantra at up to 60%, Eastside and Kingfisher at 40% off everything, and Palm Golf Co. with any two hats for $75. The brands we have covered before: Radmor and Devereux at 30%, Sunday Golf at 25%, Howie Benson at 25–40%, Hiroki at up to 40%, and Casualist’s Black Friday kits. Codes to remember: Après Golf’s category codes, Morning People’s BlackFriday for 36% off, CASUAL10 at Casualist and BLACKFRIDAY at Pluto.

Deals change by the hour this week and stock goes fast, so check the brand’s site before you count on a price. Five brands, including Dormie Workshop and Woodgrain, open their sales later in the week; we will update this page when they do.
