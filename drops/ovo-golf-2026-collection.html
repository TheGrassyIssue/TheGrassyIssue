OVO Golf — The 2026 Collection

octobersveryown.com Toronto, ON · Golf Launch 2025 “Toronto luxury meets traditional golf”

Drake’s first swing at golf came through NOCTA, his Nike sub-label, with a 10-piece recycled-fiber capsule in September 2021. Three years later, he moved the operation under his own roof. OVO Golf launched in July 2025 with a Callaway × Vessel equipment capsule — Chrome Tour golf balls, an Odyssey putter, Opus wedges, and a Vessel Player stand bag — all in the brand’s signature black-and-gold monogram. The whole thing sold out within hours. The OVO Golf Classic followed: a 36-foursome charity tournament with a helicopter ball drop, Drake tour tickets on the line, and proceeds going to the Scarborough Shooting Stars Foundation.

The 2026 collection is where OVO Golf stops being a capsule and starts being a brand. Drop 01 introduced a full apparel line — stretch nylon polos, vests, and chinos in Midnight Green, Deep Denim, and Sandstone; basketweave sweater vests that reference old-school country club knitwear; open-knit sweater polos with contrast tipping; camp shirts that go from the course to dinner without a wardrobe change. The materials are technical — 4-way stretch, water-repellent, UV-cut — but the design language is pure Toronto: restrained, elevated, and rooted in the city’s luxury-meets-street identity. Drop 2 landed July 10, 2026, bringing a fresh Callaway × Vessel equipment round — the Opus SP 60° chrome wedge, Chrome Tour Triple Diamond balls, and the Monogram Lux stand bag. Most of it sold out inside 72 hours.

OVO Niko, not Drake, is the face of the campaign — photographed in full kit with Jordan golf shoes, an iced-out Rolex, and a cigar. The message is deliberate: this is golf as lifestyle, not golf as sport performance. At $18 for argyle socks to $698 for the Vessel stand bag, the range covers accessory buys and investment pieces. International shipping is live. The OVO Golf Classic is set to become an annual tradition. Here are 18 picks from across the collection.
