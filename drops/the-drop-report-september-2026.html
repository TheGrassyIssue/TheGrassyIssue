The Drop Report — Everything That Landed, Brand by Brand

9/14/26 Drops & Brands 11 Brands · 23 Pieces

Twenty-six brands went under the lamp for the six weeks to 9/14, read from their own product records rather than from press releases — which is how Malbon's fourteen unannounced Arsham pieces turned up. Eleven brands had something new. Here it is, brand by brand, priced and stock-checked on the day.

Already Gone

Everything below sold out before we could publish it.

  • Mogshade — every piece with a partner credit: Only Human 2.0 (fifteen made, five days), Fabricaal Verdant, both Draw Golf caps, the four hand-painted KIKO CLJ covers, the Depeche Magazine bundle.
  • Sugarloaf — a $3,000 numbered set of Boyd Blade & Ferrule irons hand-ground by Don White, plus the $850 MacKenzie bag and both headcover runs from Hidden Gems.
  • Students × PAYNTR — both colourways of the $240 SL, eight sizes each, one pair per customer.
  • Jones — the Players Series relaunched on 8/25 in three colourways and sold out in all three.
  • Gamut — the Shibori cover, hand-dyed in house and one-of-one by construction, lasted six days.
  • Swag — nine of twenty-five Cubs pieces in under four weeks, including a $777.77 Wrigleyville putter.

And Four That Went Quiet

The absences a new-arrivals feed will never show you.

Kingfisher Golf has password-gated its entire storefront and paused orders while it moves warehouses. Radry has published nothing since 3/8, Casualist nothing since 3/31, and Odd Ritual nothing since a ten-piece drop on 7/15. Fella Golf's newest product is from 7/29. None of that is failure — small brands release when they have something — but it is half the picture, and nobody posts it.
