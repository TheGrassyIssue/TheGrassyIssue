Sentinel Golf x 1733 — Saltex Bags & V4 Brush

8 Pieces

Sentinel Golf linked up with Chicago bag maker 1733 on a capsule built around Saltex — a triple-layer Japanese nylon that's salt-bath heat-treated for a dense, organic texture you won't find anywhere else in golf. The material is the story: rigid out of the box, softens with humidity, snaps back when it dries. DWR coated throughout.

The lineup covers a shoe bag ($138), tote ($320), and duffle ($320), all in Saltex Charcoal. Sentinel also dropped the V4 Bag Brush in Olive ($128) alongside the collab pieces. Everything is made in Chicago, everything is limited, and the material process alone justifies the price tags. Two brands that obsess over craft, working in a fabric that most golf brands wouldn't even know how to source.
