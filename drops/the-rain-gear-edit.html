Rain Gear — 18 Picks for Foul Weather Golf

18 Picks $20 – $402 Jackets, Pants, Hats, Umbrellas & Bag Covers

Rain gear used to mean one thing: a shapeless pullover that made you look like a crossing guard and swung like a straightjacket. That era is over. The waterproof golf market in 2026 runs from $20 snap-on bag covers to $402 Pertex Shield Pro jackets with 30,000mm waterproof ratings — and the best options aren’t always the most expensive ones. Sun Mountain’s Monsoon delivers 15K waterproof protection for $100. Sugarloaf Social Club built an umbrella that handles 55mph Texas thunderstorms. KJUS engineered a bucket hat from 100% recycled polyester with sealed seams and a drawcord system.

This is 18 picks from 18 brands across jackets, pants, hats, umbrellas, and bag covers. The range runs from a $20 Stitch Golf snap-on rain hood to Manors Golf’s 2.5L Pertex WP Jacket — the highest waterproof rating on this list. In between: Eastside Golf’s culture-forward Script Umbrella, Birds of Condor’s recycled ripstop all-weather jacket from Australia, a Jones Sports Co. rain hood towel that’s the smartest dual-purpose accessory in golf, and a Galvin Green GORE-TEX jacket on sale for $233 that’s usually $389. Every category, every price tier, all from brands doing their own thing.
