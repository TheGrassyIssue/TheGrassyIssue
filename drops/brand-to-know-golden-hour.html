Brand to Know: Golden Hour — Golf Clothes for the Last Light

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 5, 2026

Golf and streetwear from the UK18 picks · checked October 5, 2026
The TGI Take

Golden Hour dresses golf like a clubhouse in a 1970s photograph: red and cream striped polos, contrast collars, pleated shorts and a lot of script logos. Two friends, Lewis and Louis, started it in the UK in 2024. It began as an Instagram page about golf and clothes, and the name came to them on a fairway at sunset. In their own words: “The golden light hitting just right, the perfect moment of calm before the night set in—thats when the name came to us.”

It suits Austin better than most British golf brands, because it is built for the round that starts at six and ends in the dark. The polos are cotton and cut loose, the shorts are a cool bamboo blend, and the track jacket is light enough to throw in the bag for when the wind picks up on the back nine at Lions. The heavy tees and the rugby sweatshirt are for the few cold months and the patio after.

The Story

The founders tell it plainly on their site. They met through mutual friends in 2024 and “clicked-sharing the same interests, the same passion for style, and the same love for golf.” The Instagram page “soon became a thriving community,” and the brand followed. They were not sure at first whether it would be about golf or fashion, and settled on both. The post ends, “Goldenhour is only getting started!”

On value, Golden Hour is cheap for what it is. Most pieces cost £40 to £50, about $54 to $67, for 100% cotton polos and 300gsm tees, and the track jacket is £70. The Stripe Pique Polo and the Lightweight Track Jacket are the two to buy first. The catch is stock: the brand makes small runs, and most polos are down to S and 2XL, so check your size before you fall for a colour. Prices were read on Golden Hour’s own store on 5 October 2026.

The Questions

Who founded Golden Hour?

Two friends, Lewis and Louis, who met through mutual friends in 2024. It started as an Instagram page about golf and fashion before they turned it into a clothing brand.

Where is Golden Hour based?

In the United Kingdom. Its store, goldenhourclubhouse.co.uk, prices in pounds and also shows prices in US dollars and other currencies.

How much does Golden Hour cost?

On 5 October 2026, from £10 for crew socks and £18 for the tote and £40 for tees up to £49.99 for the cotton polos and £70 for the Lightweight Track Jacket, roughly $13 to $94.

Why is it called Golden Hour?

The founders say the name came to them walking a fairway at sunset, when the light was golden and the course went calm before dark.

How does Golden Hour fit?

Relaxed. The polos are boxy, the Double Pleated Shorts are baggy and the brand suggests sizing down or wearing a belt, and the Golden Stripe long-sleeve is slightly cropped. Several pieces list the model as 6ft 1in wearing a medium.
