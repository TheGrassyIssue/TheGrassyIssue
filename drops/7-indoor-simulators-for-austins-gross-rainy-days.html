7 Indoor Simulators for Austin's Gross Rainy Days

7 Pieces

Austin's weather is great for golf about 300 days a year. The other 65, you need a backup plan. These seven indoor sim spots cover every vibe — from Southside Golf Co's no-frills TrackMan warehouse in Manchaca to Five Iron Golf's 12-bay downtown location coming later in 2026.

Golfinity in NW Austin runs 22 bays across multiple tech platforms. ROK Golf has Trackman setups on S Lamar and in Bee Cave. ATX Indoor Golf Club brings Trackman iO with 4K projection. X-Golf in Cedar Park adds a sports bar to the equation. And Back Nine Golf covers three locations — SW, East, and Westlake — with 24/7 access for the truly obsessed. Rain check, cancelled.

The Collection — 7 Pieces
Southside Golf Co
Manchaca · TrackMan 4 Bays
Three TrackMan 4 bays in a converted warehouse south of Slaughter. Southside Golf Co is the most low-key sim spot in Austin — no velvet ropes, no $18 cocktails, just clean bays with tour-level radar and a lounge with pool tables. PGA instruction available, leagues running, walk-ins welcome when a bay's open. The vibe is closer to a buddy's garage setup than a golf bar, and that's the whole point.

Golfinity
NW Austin · 22 Bays · Multi-Tech
The biggest indoor golf facility in the country, and it's on 620 in NW Austin. Twenty-two bays across 20,000 square feet running Trackman, Foresight, and Uneekor — you pick your tech. Full bar, PGA instructors on staff, unlimited membership access. Golfinity is where you go when you want the full sim experience with serious practice infrastructure. Not cheap, but you won't outgrow it.
Shop ↗
ROK Golf
S Lamar + Bee Cave · Trackman
Five Trackman bays, a 19th Hole Lounge with beer and wine, and enough sports bar energy to make it feel like a hangout, not a homework session. ROK has two Austin-area spots — the original on South Lamar and a second in Bee Cave's Hill Country Galleria. HD video capture in every bay, 400+ virtual courses, and PGA pros available for lessons. Good middle ground between serious practice and social.
Shop ↗
ATX Indoor Golf Club
SW Austin · Trackman iO · 4K
The boutique play. ATX Indoor Golf Club runs Trackman iO with 4K laser projection and 500+ virtual courses in private bays off Highway 290. Self-serve pantry for snacks and drinks. It's quieter and more focused than the bar-scene spots — if you want to lock in and work on your game without background noise, this is the move. Hourly rentals, no membership required.
Shop ↗
X-Golf
Cedar Park · 7 Bays · Sports Bar
Seven bays in a 6,000-square-foot space in Cedar Park, running X-Golf's proprietary sim tech that analyzes 6,000 data points per second. Sports bar setup with food, drinks, and TVs. The energy is casual and group-friendly — good for a rainy Saturday with three friends who all think they drive it 280. League play available if you want to make it a weekly thing.
Shop ↗
Back Nine Golf
SW · East · Westlake · 24/7
Three Austin-area locations — Southwest, East, and Westlake — all running Full Swing and Trackman tech with private bays. The differentiator is 24/7 membership access, so if you're the type who wants to hit balls at 11pm on a Tuesday, Back Nine has you covered. Clean, modern spaces focused on practice over party. Monthly memberships or hourly drop-ins.
Shop ↗
Five Iron Golf
Downtown · 12 Bays · Coming 2026
Not open yet, but worth watching. Five Iron Golf signed a lease for a 13,500-square-foot flagship at 501 Congress Avenue — downtown, ground floor of The Loren hotel. Twelve Trackman bays, a full bar and restaurant, duckpin bowling lanes, Callaway club fitting, and league play. If it follows the playbook from their NYC and Chicago locations, this will be the biggest indoor golf destination in Austin when it opens late 2026.
