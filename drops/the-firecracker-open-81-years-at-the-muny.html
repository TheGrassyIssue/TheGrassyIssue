The Firecracker Open — 81 Years at the Muny

8 Pieces

Lions Municipal opened in 1924 as Austin's first public course — nine holes cut through the oaks on Enfield Road by the Lions Club. In 1950, it quietly became the first desegregated public course south of the Mason-Dixon line. Since 1945, the Firecracker Open has drawn the best amateur players in Texas every Fourth of July weekend.

Crenshaw, Kite, and Uresti all won here before turning pro. 54 holes, no cut, and a field that mixes college stars with 60-year-old regulars who've played the course a thousand times.
