The Collection — 8 Pieces

Lions Municipal Golf Course
The Muny — Est. 1924
Lions Municipal opened in 1924 as Austin's first public course — nine holes cut through the oaks on Enfield Road by the Lions Club. In 1950, it quietly became the first desegregated public course south of the Mason-Dixon line. Since 1945, the Firecracker Open has drawn the best amateur players in Texas every Fourth of July weekend. Crenshaw, Kite, and Uresti all won here before turning pro. 54 holes, no cut, and a field that mixes college stars with 60-year-old regulars who've played the course a thousand times.

Lions Municipal Golf Course
2901 Enfield Road · Central Austin
The Muny sits on 141 acres between Enfield Road and MoPac, ten minutes from downtown Austin. It's where Harvey Penick taught for decades, where Ben Crenshaw hit his first balls, and where 70,000 rounds still get played every year. The course was designed by John Bredemus and has been in continuous operation for over a century. Walk-on green fees are among the lowest in Austin.

Firecracker Open
Texas' Oldest Amateur Tournament
The Firecracker Open is Texas' oldest continuously running amateur golf tournament — 81 years and counting. Every Fourth of July weekend, 100+ players tee it up for 54 holes of stroke play at the Muny. Past champions include Ben Crenshaw, Tom Kite, Bill Penn (Texas Golf Hall of Fame), Randy Petri, and Omar Uresti. The 2026 qualifiers are June 20 and June 23.

2024 Firecracker Open
New 8,000 sq ft Putting Green
The 2024 Firecracker Open showcased a brand-new 8,000-square-foot putting green — the centerpiece of ongoing renovations backed by The Muny Conservancy and the City of Austin. Afternoon rains paused Saturday's round, but the field pushed through. Brannan Cox won on the first playoff hole, continuing the tournament's tradition of dramatic finishes.

Lions Municipal Golf Course
First Desegregated Course in the South
In late 1950, following the Supreme Court's ruling in Sweatt v. Painter, Lions Municipal quietly became the first public golf course in the southern United States to desegregate — four years before Brown v. Board of Education. No ceremony. No headlines. The course just opened its tee sheet to everyone. That history is part of why the USGA, the Conservancy, and players like Jordan Spieth have fought to protect it.

Lions Municipal Golf Course
Where Crenshaw & Kite Learned the Game
Harvey Penick spent over 50 years teaching at the Muny. His students included Ben Crenshaw, Tom Kite, Sandra Haynie, and Kathy Whitworth. His 'Little Red Book' became the bestselling golf instruction book ever written — and most of it was learned on these fairways. The practice area where he gave lessons is still in use today.

The Muny Conservancy
Renovations · Ongoing
The Muny Conservancy was formed to protect and enhance Lions Municipal for future generations. Their work has funded new irrigation, tee box renovations, bunker restoration, and the 2024 putting green. The course was added to the Texas Registry of Historic Golf Courses and selected by the Texas Golf Hall of Fame. Spieth is among the conservancy's most visible supporters.

2026 Firecracker Open
July 3–5 · Lions Municipal
The 2026 Firecracker Open runs July 3–5 at Lions Municipal. Non-exempt players qualify through two rounds on June 20 and June 23. Entry is $285 for qualifiers. The field is a mix of college players, former champions, and Austin locals who've been grinding at the Muny their whole lives. It's the most important amateur tournament in Central Texas — and one of the best weekends on the Austin golf calendar.
