The Technical Brands Roundup

16 Brands Fabric Is the Story Graphene, GORE-TEX, 3D Knitting & More

Most golf brands talk about “performance fabric” and leave it at that. Some brands actually mean it. They have materials scientists on staff, proprietary textile technologies with real names, and products where the fabric itself — not the logo, not the cut, not the colorway — is the reason to buy.

This is 16 of them. Swiss-engineered graphene for thermal regulation. A 30-year exclusive with GORE-TEX. Laser-cut micro-perforations instead of mesh panels. Filium-activated cotton that repels liquids and kills odor without chemicals. 3D-knit sweaters from a single strand of yarn. Merino wool that outperforms polyester. Japanese-loomed Warpstreme. These brands treat the material as the product — everything else is just design around it.
