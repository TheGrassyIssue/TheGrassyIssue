Brand to Know: Puttwell, the Hawaii Golf Brand That Knows Its Roll

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 4, 2026

Hawaii, since 202129 picks · in stock October 4, 2026
The TGI Take

Puttwell is a golf brand made by people from the music world, and it shows. It started in Hawaii in 2021 with a crew from music and art, including Jami Ablan, a DJ with the Nocturnal Sound Crew, and it still dresses like it: a tagline of “Know your roll,” a Deep Cuts tee about the overlooked tracks, and a collaboration with Extra Butter called Drop Zone, a golf rule and a needle on vinyl at the same time. The brand promise fits on a business card: “dress well, play well, treat others well... Puttwell.”

Its best work is the Hawaii work. The Sig Zane collection put the kou flower on polos and caps, and the Waikahalulu capsule with Sig On Smith maps Honolulu’s Chinatown into a tonal jacquard polo and Puttwell’s first golf bag. That sense of place is what separates it from the dozens of streetwear-golf labels that started around the same time. The everyday line — clean stretch polos, a wind jacket, putter covers and ball markers — is solid and fairly priced, from $10 markers to $120 layers.

The Story

Puttwell started in Hawaii in 2021, founded by what the brand calls “a collective of individuals heavily connected to both creative worlds: music and art.” Jami Ablan, a DJ with the Nocturnal Sound Crew, is one of them, and the brand has kept that crew feeling. Its own account of why it exists is about who gets to play: Puttwell “was born with a fundamental belief in fostering inclusivity within the realm of golf,” and it treats the game as “social connectedness and a shared time that brings people together.”

After a quiet 2024, it came back in February 2025 with an event at the WM Phoenix Open, hosted with Seamus and the lifestyle brand Timeless. The Sig Zane collection followed: eleven pieces built around the kou flower, a native Hawaiian bloom, launched with a two-day pop-up at the International Market Place in Waikiki, with putting contests, long-drive competitions for new clubs and a DJ set from Ablan. Drop Zone, the collaboration with Extra Butter, took the golf-and-vinyl idea to New York, and the Waikahalulu capsule with Sig On Smith brought it home to Honolulu’s Chinatown.

The brand now sells from Hawaii to Los Angeles, New York, Japan and Australia, but it still dresses like a group of friends who play together and make clothes about it.

The honest caveat: it is a small brand that moves in drops. It went quiet for much of 2024, and right now only about 30 styles are in stock, many down to one color or a few sizes. If something catches your eye, buy it now. Prices were read on Puttwell’s own store on October 4, 2026.

The Questions

Where is Puttwell from?

Hawaii. Puttwell was founded in 2021 by a collective from music and art, and now sells from Hawaii to Los Angeles, New York, Japan and Australia.

Who founded Puttwell?

A group of creatives from music and art. One of the founders is Jami Ablan, a DJ with the Nocturnal Sound Crew. The brand does not name the others on its site.

What does Know Your Roll mean?

It is Puttwell's tagline, and it appears on tees, towels, ball markers and putter covers. The Know Your Roll tee spells it out: know your roll, play your game.

What is the Waikahalulu collection?

A capsule with Sig On Smith, a creative house in Honolulu's Chinatown. It includes a jacquard map-print polo, Country Club tees and Puttwell's first golf bag, which cost $325 on October 4, 2026.

Which brands has Puttwell collaborated with?

Sig Zane Designs in Hawaii, the New York boutique Extra Butter on the Drop Zone collection, and Sig On Smith on the Waikahalulu capsule.
