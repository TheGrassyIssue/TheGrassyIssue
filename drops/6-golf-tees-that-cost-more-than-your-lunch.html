6 Golf Tees That Cost More Than Your Lunch

6 Pieces

Six golf tees. All of them cost more than the fast-food lunch you grabbed on the way to the course. And somehow, every one of them makes a case for why the upgrade is real.

Flightpath's Diamond Tee ($23 for eight) is polycarbonate, diamond-shaped, and packaged in a matchbox — the aerodynamics pitch actually holds up under testing. Bird Darts went bamboo at $6.99 for 18. Western Birch hand-paints striped birch tees. Lignum machines micro-sized wooden tees in Germany. Green Swing does sustainable bamboo. And Brimz Supply made cigarette-shaped tees called Tee Box Smokes ($12.99 for 10), which is either genius or unhinged depending on your sense of humor. The humble golf tee got interesting when nobody was looking.

The Collection — 6 Pieces
Flightpath Golf
Flightpath Golf — Diamond Tee
Flightpath made a diamond-shaped polycarbonate tee and put it in a matchbox. The pitch is less drag, more distance — and the shape science actually checks out. $23 for eight, so you’re paying for engineering, not wood.
Bird Darts
Bird Darts — Premium Bamboo Tees
Bird Darts put bamboo tees in a cigarette box and made it look intentional. Natural material, clean branding, the kind of thing that sits well in a golf bag side pocket. $6.99 for eighteen — the packaging alone is worth half that.
Western Birch
Western Birch Golf Co. — Striped Birch Tees
Western Birch names their colorways after classic cars — Fleetwood, Thunderbird, Mustang. Striped birch hardwood in dozens of combos, which means your tee game now has a palette. $9–$12 for fifty, and they look better than they need to.
Lignum
Lignum — Microwood Tees
Lignum is German-engineered microwood — wood fiber pressed with natural adhesives into something that lasts 50 rounds. Anti-spin head, ring height system, the whole Teutonic playbook. About $6 for twelve, which is a bargain for tees that refuse to break.
Green Swing
Green Swing — Bamboo Tees
Green Swing out of the UK does limited-edition bamboo tees in colors that actually pop — yellow, black, natural. Eco-focused without making it their whole personality. About $6 for thirty, which makes them the volume play on this list.
Brimz Supply
Brimz Supply — Tee Box Smokes
Brimz called them Tee Box Smokes and put them in a magnetic cigarette box, which is either brilliant or unhinged. Bamboo tees, ten per pack, the kind of accessory that starts conversations on the first tee. Packaging game is strong.
