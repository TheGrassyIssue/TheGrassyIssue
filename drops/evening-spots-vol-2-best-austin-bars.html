Evening Spots Vol. 2: 15 of the Best Bars in Austin

By Lenny Harrington · Updated October 10, 2026

The Roosevelt Room, Midnight Cowboy, Hula Hut, Deep Eddy and more15 bars · Austin, TX · verified October 10, 2026
The TGI Take

Vol. 1 was five easy spots for the hour after the round. This one is bigger: fifteen of the best bars in Austin, for every kind of evening. Sometimes you want a quiet room with a perfect drink, sometimes a patio until midnight, and sometimes a dive where nobody minds the golf shoes.

The list is split three ways. The cocktail rooms are for the nights you dress up, the patios are for warm evenings, and the dives and the honky-tonk are Austin institutions. One of them, Boni’s, only opened this year. Every bar was checked against its own site on October 10, 2026, and the hours in each card are theirs.

Start Here

If you only go to one, make it The Roosevelt Room on a weeknight. Grab a seat at the bar, pick a drink from the decade of your choice, and come back on a Thursday. For a sunset, it’s Hula Hut. For a cheap night, it’s a mug of Lone Star at Deep Eddy.

5 spots

Cocktail Rooms

Five rooms · Downtown, East and North LoopFor when you clean up after the round. Austin’s most-awarded cocktail bar, a speakeasy behind a buzzer, a North Loop neighborhood favorite, an absinthe bar and a mezcal room.

1/5
W 5th St · Since 2015
The Roosevelt Room
Austin’s most-awarded cocktail bar, in a 1929 building in the Warehouse District. The classics menu runs to 80-plus drinks, organized by era, from the turn of the century and Prohibition through tiki to modern classics. Table service and no dress code. Reservations are strongly recommended. Open daily from 3pm, until 2am Thursday to Saturday.
View ↗
1/5
E 6th St · Speakeasy
Midnight Cowboy
Behind an unmarked door on Dirty Sixth, in a building that used to be a brothel posing as a massage parlor. Ring the buzzer. Inside it is small, dark and quiet, with a tarot-themed menu that got it nominated for CultureMap’s 2026 Bar of the Year. Book on OpenTable, parties of eight or fewer. Closed Mondays.
View ↗
1/5
North Loop · Since 2012
Drink.Well
A small neighborhood cocktail bar and kitchen on East 53rd Street, and one of the most awarded in town since it opened in 2012. It was a CultureMap Bar of the Year nominee in 2025. The menu turns with the seasons, and the bartenders will make you any classic. Martini Monday, the Tuesday Night Agave Social and happy hour until 5:30pm. Closed Sundays.
View ↗
1/5
W 4th St · Since about 2009
Péché
Billed as Austin’s first absinthe bar, with pre-Prohibition cocktails and a French bistro kitchen behind them. Order a Sazerac or let the bartenders walk you through the absinthe; they like the questions. Happy hour runs all night on Sunday and Monday.
View ↗
1/3
Mueller · Opened 2025
La Mezca
A small mezcal bar from Reyna and Maritza Vazquez, the sisters behind Veracruz All Natural, next door to their Mueller restaurant. It pours only artisanal and ancestral mezcal from family producers, with no orange slices and no salt. The Austin Chronicle named it Best Bar for Mezcal Nerds in 2026. Vinyl DJs on Friday and Saturday, open until 2am.

Patios and Backyards

Five patios · All over townFor a warm night when nobody wants to be indoors: a tree-covered East Side patio, a new Spanish backyard bar, martini night on Manor Road, a beer garden behind an old machine shop and a deck over Lake Austin.

1/5
E 5th St · Patio
Yellow Jacket Social Club
The East Side patio, under a canopy of trees, with a full kitchen that runs until 2am. Cocktails, draft beer, Frito pie and sandwiches, and a different cheap special every night of the week. Open every day, 11am to 2am.
View ↗
1/4
S 1st St · New in 2026
Boni’s Bar Next Door
The newest bar here, opened in March by the couple behind Lenoir, in a 1934 bungalow next door to the restaurant. Spanish-leaning cocktails ($12–$15, start with the gin tonic), Spanish wines from $10 and tapas like stewed pork meatballs. It’s named for a great-grandfather who ran rum during Prohibition. Closed Tuesdays.
View ↗
1/6
Manor Rd · Patio and yard
The Long Goodbye
An indoor cocktail lounge with covered patios and an outdoor yard on Manor Road. Tuesday is $10 martini night, Wednesday is half-off wine, and Palm Pizza slices are on Monday and Tuesday. Walk-in only. Open until midnight Wednesday to Saturday.
View ↗
1/5
E Cesar Chavez · Beer hall
Central Machine Works
A brewery and beer hall in a 1940s machine shop, with an 18,000-pound vintage lathe behind the bar and a big beer garden out back. The American Lager took bronze at the 2026 Texas Craft Brewers Cup. There’s a full bar, frozen drinks, free shows and trivia on Tuesdays. Closed Mondays.
View ↗
1/5
Lake Austin · Since 1993
Hula Hut
A patio built right out over Lake Austin, with bamboo, shade and thousands of colored lights, and a Tex-Mex menu with a Hawaiian twist. You can arrive by boat. It’s the sunset pick on this list, so order a rita and stay until the lights come on. No reservations.

5 spots

Dives and Honky-Tonks

Five institutions · Since 1951For when you’re still in your golf shoes. Three dives with a combined 152 years, the best honky-tonk in East Austin and the busiest Irish pub in town.

1/2
Lake Austin Blvd · Since 1951
Deep Eddy Cabaret
Austin’s classic dive, opened in 1951 and named for a swimming hole on the river behind it. There’s no kitchen: beer, liquor, wine, snacks, pool and a jukebox, and Lone Star by the mug. Open every day, noon to 2am.
View ↗
1/3
Hyde Park · Since 1971
Posse East
Fifty-five years on Duval Street, at the edge of Hyde Park and walking distance from the stadium. A big patio, cheap happy hour, a third-pound cheeseburger and a crowd that has been coming since the 1970s. The bar is open until midnight Monday to Saturday.
View ↗
1/4
W 5th St · Since 2004
The Mean Eyed Cat
A Johnny Cash shrine in an old chainsaw repair shop, with a wood patio under a live oak that is more than 300 years old. Order the Mean Marg (tequila with habanero, serrano and jalapeño). Happy hour on weekdays, 4 to 8pm. Open daily, 11am to 2am.
View ↗
1/5
E 6th St · Since 2011
The White Horse
The best honky-tonk in East Austin, by its own count and plenty of others’. Live country bands every night, two-step lessons Thursday to Sunday at 7pm (donation-based, so go), and the Bomb Tacos truck parked outside until 1:30am. Willie Nelson has been known to drop in. No reservations, cover after 7:30pm. Open daily, 3pm to 2am.
View ↗
1/5
E 6th St · Since 2024
The Dead Rabbit
The Austin outpost of the New York Irish pub that was named World’s Best Bar in 2016. It opened on Sixth Street on July 4, 2024. It’s a proper pub with Guinness, the house Irish coffee, live music and a Sunday roast, and it’s always packed. Book on Resy, but there’s always room for walk-ins.
View ↗

The Questions

What are the best bars in Austin?

This guide has 15. Cocktail rooms: The Roosevelt Room, Midnight Cowboy, Drink.Well, Péché and La Mezca. Patios: Yellow Jacket Social Club, Boni's Bar Next Door, The Long Goodbye, Central Machine Works and Hula Hut. Dives and honky-tonks: Deep Eddy Cabaret, Posse East, The Mean Eyed Cat, The White Horse and The Dead Rabbit.

What is the best cocktail bar in Austin?

The Roosevelt Room in the Warehouse District calls itself Austin's most-awarded cocktail bar, with 80-plus classic cocktails organized by era. Midnight Cowboy, behind a buzzer on East Sixth, is the speakeasy alternative, and Drink.Well is the neighborhood pick in North Loop.

Where can you watch the sunset with a drink in Austin?

Hula Hut's patio sits right over Lake Austin on Lake Austin Boulevard. Deep Eddy Cabaret, a few minutes away, is the cheaper dive option.

What are the best patio bars in Austin?

Yellow Jacket Social Club's tree-covered patio on East 5th, The Long Goodbye's covered patios and yard on Manor Road, Central Machine Works' beer garden on East Cesar Chavez, Boni's backyard on South 1st and Hula Hut on Lake Austin.

Which Austin bars are open until 2am?

Of the bars in this guide, Yellow Jacket Social Club, Deep Eddy Cabaret, The Mean Eyed Cat, The White Horse, The Dead Rabbit (most nights) and La Mezca (Friday and Saturday) stay open until 2am, as does The Roosevelt Room Thursday to Saturday.
