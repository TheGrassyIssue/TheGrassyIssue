The Oakley Golf Collection

18 Picks $20 – $360 Oakley Golf

Oakley doesn’t do golf the way the traditional brands do. There’s no country-club deference, no pastel-and-pleat playbook — the approach is rooted in sport science, materials engineering, and a willingness to look different on the course. The 2026 lineup is the clearest statement yet: the Fusion Collection, designed alongside JR Smith, puts technical construction front and center with bonded seams, sonic-welded panels, and stretch-woven fabrics that move like activewear but read like proper golf clothes.

Beyond Fusion, the collection runs deep. The Hypegolf collaboration brings limited-edition pieces with an engineered exo polo and bark pullover. The Japan-line Skull pieces — the Claw Shirt, Apex Tapered pants, Aero Shorts — push silhouette and texture in ways the US line doesn’t touch. And the Edge shoe family keeps expanding: the laceless Edge Elite, the course-to-street Aquaterra Hybrid, the flexible Edge Flex. Eighteen picks across tops, bottoms, shoes, and accessories — from a $20 trucker hat to the $360 Fusion Windbreaker.
