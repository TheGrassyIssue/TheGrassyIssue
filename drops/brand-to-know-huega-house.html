Huega House

Carlsbad, CA Est. 2022 $22 – $88

The name comes from the Danish word hygge — the art of cherishing the little things. The spelling changed because Americans kept butchering the pronunciation, but the idea stuck. Jonathan Ruley, a 30-year-old marketer from Boise State, and Jake Ryan, the former Green Bay Packers linebacker who played 48 NFL games including five postseason, are future brothers-in-law who bonded over one shared frustration: they couldn’t find a hat that fit right. So they split $10,000 and made their own.

That was 2022. By mid-2024, Huega House hit $2.3 million in annual revenue — profitable, no venture capital, fewer than ten employees. The growth came organically. Aaron Rodgers wore one on the Pat McAfee Show and at a Knicks playoff game. Shohei Ohtani, Josh Allen, Nick Bosa, and TJ Watt all started showing up in them. Nobody was paid. Golf Digest featured the brand. Jake Ryan does all the photography himself. The aesthetic pulls from 90s sports and automotive culture — vintage graphics, structured crowns, clean embroidery — and the result is headwear that reads equally well on the course, at the range, or walking the dog.

The line has grown beyond hats into performance tees, hybrid shorts, hoodies, crewnecks, and sweats. Everything runs $22 to $88. Based in Carlsbad, shipping worldwide, and building a community around their HH Run Club and ambassador program. No big-box retail. No paid endorsements. Just product that people keep coming back for.

Updated, August 2026: the machine keeps rolling. The range now runs from $14 beanies to performance packs, the Flow Running Hat gives the run-club crowd a course-ready option, and the drops keep selling through — most of the Legacy and Athletics colorways below are restocks of sellouts. The brand's Instagram tells the story best: hometown San Diego baseball graphics, studio campaigns, and the moodboard-on-a-wall design process, which is why we've added a proper In the Wild section below.

The Story — FAQ

What is Huega House?
A San Diego-area headwear and performance apparel brand based in Carlsbad, California. The aesthetic pulls from '90s sports and automotive culture — vintage graphics, structured crowns, clean embroidery — across hats, performance tees, shorts, hoodies, and sweats priced roughly $14 to $88.
Who founded Huega House?
Future brothers-in-law Jake Ryan — the former Green Bay Packers linebacker who played 48 NFL games — and Jonathan Ruley, a marketer out of Boise State. They bonded over not being able to find a hat that fit right, split $10,000, and started the brand in 2022. By mid-2024 it was doing $2.3 million in annual revenue — profitable, no venture capital, fewer than ten employees.
What does Huega mean?
It comes from hygge, the Danish concept of cherishing life's small comforts. The founders respelled it “Huega” (hue-guh) because Americans kept butchering the Danish pronunciation. The motto on everything: cherish the little things.
Who wears Huega House hats?
Aaron Rodgers wore one on the Pat McAfee Show and at a Knicks playoff game. Shohei Ohtani, Josh Allen, Aaron Judge, Nick Bosa, and TJ Watt have all been spotted in them. Nobody was paid — the brand runs zero paid endorsements, which makes the co-signs mean something.
Are Huega House hats good for golf?
Very. The Icon Performance and Aero Nylon hats are lightweight, sweat-ready course options, the new Flow Running Hat handles hot-weather rounds, and the structured high-profile styles read like '90s tour hats. Golf Digest has featured the brand's headwear, and it shows up constantly at Austin munis.
Where can you buy Huega House?
Direct at huegahouse.com, plus Hat Club carries a selection. No big-box retail, by design. The community side runs through a membership program, brand ambassadors, and the HH Run Club — and Jake Ryan still shoots the product photography himself.
