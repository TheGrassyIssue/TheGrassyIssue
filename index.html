Nov 8–9 Charity
Grey Rock Golf Club · Austin
Golf for Joy Austin
A Sunday dinner and live auction downtown, then an 11:45am shotgun at Grey Rock. The fourth Austin edition of a series that has raised over $4 million for JoyRx children's hospital programs. Spots are limited.
joyrx.org ↗
Nov 12–15 Tournament
Omni Barton Creek · Fazio Canyons
The Austin Championship (PGA Tour)
The Tour's first Austin stop since 2023: 120 players, a $6,000,000 purse, 500 FedExCup points. Formerly the Good Good Championship — the title sponsor stepped away on 8/27 and ticket sales are currently paused.
austinchampionship.com ↗
Nov 16–17 Tournament
Driftwood Golf & Ranch Club
46th Texas Joe Black Cup
A Ryder-Cup-format match between the Northern and Southern Texas PGA Sections, running since 1981 and standing at 22–21–2. Free to watch, and it lands the day after the Tour event leaves town.
ntpga.com ↗
May 17–19 College
UT Golf Club · Austin
NCAA Men's Regional Championship
The only home date on either Longhorn golf schedule all season, and the biggest golf Austin gets after November. Both UT teams play every other event of 2026–27 on the road.
texaslonghorns.com ↗
