Golf Clothes You Can Wear After the Round

23 brands in the Index

The test is simple and unforgiving: would you keep it on for dinner. Most golf clothing fails it, because the thing that makes a polo read as golf — the logo placement, the sheen of the fabric, the cut through the shoulder — is exactly the thing that makes it read as golf in a restaurant.

The brands here pass for different reasons. Aimé Leon Dore and Carhartt WIP pass because they are not golf companies and never were. Gramicci passes because a climbing pant from 1982 was always going to work anywhere. Casualist and Criquet pass because they built to the test deliberately, in fabrics and cuts borrowed from menswear rather than from performance sportswear. It is the shelf to shop if you travel to golf rather than living at a club.
