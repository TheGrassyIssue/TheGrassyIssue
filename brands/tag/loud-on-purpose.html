Loud Golf Brands, On Purpose

25 brands in the Index

Loud is a decision, not an accident, and the brands here made it deliberately. Swag Golf paints headcovers that the rest of the bag has to live up to. Birds of Condor runs prints that would be at home on a surf shirt. Malbon built an entire company on the premise that golf clothing had been apologising for itself for forty years.

The useful distinction inside this group is between loud-as-graphic and loud-as-colour. Golf Gods and Metalwood Studio work in imagery and reference; G/FORE and J.Lindeberg work in saturated blocks of colour and cut. They land very differently on a first tee and they are shopped for different reasons, which is why both sit here rather than being split.
