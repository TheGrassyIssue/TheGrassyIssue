Quiet Luxury Golf Brands

15 brands in the Index

Quiet is harder than loud, because there is nowhere to hide. Strip the logo and the graphic and what is left is fabric, cut and finish, which are the expensive parts. That is why this is the smallest of the major groups in the Index and why the price floor across it is the highest.

Manors Golf made its name rejecting technical golf aesthetics outright. Quiet Golf put the thesis in the name. A.P.C. Golf arrived from Paris ready-to-wear with the restraint already built in, and MacKenzie and Dormie Workshop apply the same logic to leather and canvas rather than cloth. The shared quality is that none of them will tell you across a fairway who made the thing you are wearing.
