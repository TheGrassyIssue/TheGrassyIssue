Independent Golf Brands

44 brands in the Index

Independent here means structural, not stylistic: no parent conglomerate, no licensing group, no outside investors, nobody upstairs to approve the range. Bettinardi is still family-run out of Tinley Park. Seamus Golf is owned outright by Akbar and Megan Chisti. No Laying Up answers to its own audience and nobody else. The twenty-five we have covered most are ranked on Our 25 Best Independent Golf Brands.

It matters to a buyer in two specific ways. Independents tend to move faster — a Gamut Golf or a Dormie Workshop can decide on a Tuesday and ship in a month. And they tend to disappear faster, which is why a good number of the brands here also carry the drops-and-vanishes attribute. Buy the thing when you see it.
