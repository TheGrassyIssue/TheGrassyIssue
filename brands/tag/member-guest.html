Member-Guest Golf Brands

4 brands in the Index

The narrowest shelf in the Index, and the one with the clearest brief: clothing that will not be questioned at the door. Peter Millar is the default answer across most of American private golf. Duca del Cosma and Royal Albartross make the shoes, from opposite ends of Europe and with opposite ideas about how visible a golf shoe should be. Manors sits here and in quiet luxury at once, which is the most accurate thing anyone can say about Manors.

One caveat: a dress code is a local document, not a national one. Everything here clears the common ones, but the club with the collar rule is the club with the collar rule.
