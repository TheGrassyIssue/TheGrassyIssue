Design-Nerd Golf Brands

43 brands in the Index

This is the largest group in the Index, and the loosest, which is usually a sign a category is real rather than invented. What connects Bettinardi milling a putter from one billet, Inside Story Socks printing a narrative into a sock, and A.P.C. Golf applying a French ready-to-wear house's restraint to a quarter-zip is not a look. It is a sequence: the design decision comes first and the golf application comes second, rather than a golf product getting a graphic applied at the end.

You can tell them apart from the merely handsome by what they will not do. A design-led brand will ship fewer colourways, hold a silhouette across seasons, and answer a question about construction with an actual answer. Edel will sell you five hosels because aim bias is measurable. Evnroll will explain a groove pattern. That willingness to be specific in public is the tell.
