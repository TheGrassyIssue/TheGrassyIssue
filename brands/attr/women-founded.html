Women-Founded Golf Brands

19 brands in the Index

Nineteen brands in the Index were founded or co-founded by women, and they are spread across every category rather than concentrated in apparel. Daphne's Headcovers and Jan Craig are among the oldest names in the whole Index. Foray Golf and Bunker Mentality work in clothing. Duca del Cosma makes shoes; ORKAI and Inside Story Socks work the accessory end.

We tag this because it is a fact about who built the company, checkable against the company's own record, and because it is a question readers ask us directly. It is not a claim about the product, which is why brands here also carry whatever taste tags actually describe what they make.
