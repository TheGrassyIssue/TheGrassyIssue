Our 25 Best Independent Golf Brands

These are the twenty-five independent golf brands The Grassy Issue has written about most. We ranked them on the record, not on a gut call: every page on the site that mentions a brand counts once, and the brands with the most pages sit at the top. Manors Golf leads with 34 posts, followed by Students Golf and Devereux Golf.

Each brand has its own page in the Brand Index, which links out to every post that counts toward its number. Read the list as a map of where our attention has actually gone in 2026: the brands we keep going back to for drops, gift guides, roundups and field notes.

How this list works

These are the brands we keep coming back to because they make good stuff, they are consistent about it, and they have a clear point of view. Every one is still run by the people who started it, or by a family, rather than by a parent company. Most are small, and a few have brought in outside partners along the way.

The order comes from our own coverage: every TGI page that mentions a brand counts once, and the brands we have written about most sit at the top. We last checked who runs each one on September 25, 2026. The list updates whenever the Brand Index is rebuilt, so it moves as we write.

  1. 01

    Manors Golf

    34 posts · London, UK · apparel

    London, 2019. Launched against technical golf apparel, then relaunched as a technical golf brand in 2023.

    Founded in London in 2019 by school friends Jojo Regan and Luke Davies, who still run it; a 2026 minority round was led by Redrice Ventures.
