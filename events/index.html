Austin City Championship

54 holes of stroke play across three Austin munis — Morris Williams, Jimmy Clay, and Roy Kizer. Open qualifiers in late July, championship rounds Jul 31–Aug 2. Ben Crenshaw won this thing three straight years as a teenager. Your muni game finally has a stage.
