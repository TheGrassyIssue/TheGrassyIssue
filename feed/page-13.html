Hancock · 1
Hancock Hole 1 — the oldest first tee in Texas.
Built in 1899, this opening par 4 plays 324 from the blues — the #1 handicap hole on the card. No warm-up. Just step up and earn your way to the green.
[Field Notes]
Roy Kizer · 15
Roy Kizer Hole 15 — the prettiest par 3 you'll ever bogey.
Short, scenic, and sneaky. At 137 from the tips it's wedge territory — but the wind and the bunkers guarding the green make club selection a mind game. Easiest hole on the card, if you find the putting surface.
[Field Notes]
Morris Williams · 7
Morris Williams Hole 7 — the blind tee shot that rewards trust.
You can't see the fairway from the tee. The ridge swallows your ball for three full seconds. Pick a line, commit, and find out what kind of lie the golf gods gave you.
