The Firecracker Open — 81 Years at the Muny
Lions Municipal opened in 1924 as Austin's first public course — nine holes cut through the oaks on Enfield Road by the Lions Club. In 1950, it quietly became the first desegregated public course south of the Mason-Dixon line. Since 1945, the Firecracker Open has drawn the best amateur players in Texas every Fourth of July weekend. Crenshaw, Kite, and Uresti all won here before turning pro. 54 holes, no cut, and a field that mixes college stars with 60-year-old regulars who've played the course a thousand times.

The Mackenzie Club: A 33,000-Sq-Ft Private Indoor Golf Club Is Coming to West Lake Hills
Dave Rogenmoser spent a decade building online startups. Now he's building a three-story, 33,000-square-foot private golf and social club in West Lake Hills — and he named it after Alister MacKenzie, because of course he did. Eight Trackman simulator bays, a PuttView putting lab, outdoor short-game greens with Hill Country views, a chef-driven restaurant, wellness studio with sauna and cold plunge, and a pro shop. Michael Hsu is doing the architecture. Membership is application-based with a vetting process — "culture matters more than anything we're building, but we're not exclusive for the sake of being exclusive." Opening late 2026. Waitlist at mackenzieclub.com.
