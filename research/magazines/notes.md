## Hiatus — Montreal (LSTW Publishing, Florence Gagnon publisher/editor; AD Carolyne De Bellefeuille). Bilingual EN/FR, ~120pp, biannual since 2025.
No.1 Sep 2025 (launched at Hiatus Golf Invitational, Club de Golf Le Champêtre; Bunker Club LA; Eastern Quebec getaways; critique of male-oriented course/clubhouse design). No.2 (store 11 May 2026): HEAD design house, Modi Oyewole, John Nichols, Amanda Corr, James Ehrlich. CAD 18.00 each, in stock ("Limited quantities" on No.2). Stockists incl. Muni Montréal, Issues Toronto, Skylight Books LA, Metalwood Studio LA, Chess Club Portland.
Verbatim own copy: "explores golf not only as a game, but as a space for expression, community, and reinvention." / "It's our love letter to print, design, bilingualism, intentional slowness, and friendships that start in DMs and land on the green." Gagnon (hiatusgolf.com): "Creating a magazine today is an act of intention … It's about slowing down, choosing stories that matter, and giving them the space and design they deserve."

## Golf Quarterly — UK. Tim Dickson (ed., ex-FT), Jon Connell (publisher, founded The Week), Fiona Hill (design, ex-Telegraph). A5, 44pp, ad-free, 4x/yr since Spring 2010. Latest listed Issue 58 (season not stated; homepage stale "Autumn 2023"); £10/issue, £35/yr. Verbatim: "combines elegant writing with sharp wit about the game and its characters." / "as quirky and unpredictable as most other magazines are worthy and dull." (contains "worthy" not "worth" — ok) Tagline "Welcome to the best kept secret in Golf".

## African American Golfer's Digest — NYC, Debert Cook founder/publisher, since March 2003. 2 print double issues + 6 digital/yr. Summer 2026 print $12 (cover Emily Odwin); July 2026 digital $5 (Gladys Lee). Print sub $59, digital $39. Verbatim: "the first wholly Black woman-owned golf magazine in America specifically targeting the African American demographic in this capacity." / "the nation's leading print publication and online portal for avid Black American golfers." Cook created National Black Golf Month (April) 2011; National Black Golf Hall of Fame 2025.

## Golf Architecture — Society of Australian Golf Course Architects annual since 1997. Issue 26 current, AUD 20 incl postage. Verbatim: "Our world renowned and sought after annual Golf Architecture magazine has its origins back in 1997…"

## Links from the Road — Sam Cooper (golf architect, Clayton DeVries & Pont; Royal Liverpool member), 225 GB links toured in a camper van 2020–22; 18 volumes planned. Vol 1 (Oct 2024, Lancashire coast/Hoylake) Vol 2 (Apr 2025, Lytham to Silloth, IoM) £19.95 each. No Vol 3; stalled. Verbatim: "It's a mixture of history, architecture, photography, and unadulterated geekery." / "I firmly believe the ball along the ground is twice as interesting as the ball through the air."
