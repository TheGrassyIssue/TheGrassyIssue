Where the game becomes a lifestyle

Golf has always existed in that strange territory between sport and tradition. Cold hands and dew on your shoes, four hours under a changing sky, and then back inside with no desire to look like you've just finished an athletic event.

A full 18 on an autumn day can feel like several seasons compressed into four hours. Comfortable, temperature-regulating and agile, knitwear is the ultimate apparel choice for the golfer who lives in their golf clothes, and it's exactly what the Knit Capsule was built for.

For the Edge of the Season

Built for the edges of the season, when the weather can't quite make up its mind, the capsule encompasses a selection of premium pieces, each cut to move easily between the fairway and the clubhouse. Golf clothing shouldn't announce itself from across the room; it should feel just as right leaning over a putt as it does settling in at the clubhouse.

Introducing a New Blend

Introducing a blend of 45% Extrafine Merino Wool (⌀19.5μm) and 55% ThermoCool fibre, engineered for mid-layer knitwear. ThermoCool's hollow, dual-direction thermoregulating core pulls moisture away from the skin while helping retain warmth, so the knit adjusts as the day does. It's moisture-wicking, machine washable, and built to transition from green to clubhouse; the same material that keeps you dry on the back nine is what lets you stay for a coffee once you're back inside.
