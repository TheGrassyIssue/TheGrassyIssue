MADE IN THE USA

What we do

We design, manufacture and distribute timeless bags and accessories for golf and travel. Every one of our bags is cut, sewn and embroidered on our factory floor in New Jersey, just a few miles from Midtown Manhattan. By controlling every aspect of the process we offer fully customized, Made To Order products, in 4-6 weeks.   Premium gifts, handmade in a New York minute.

OUR PROCESS

How They Are Made

Our bags are made here in New Jersey using traditional cutting and sewing techniques learned over generations of apprenticeship. It takes our team of artisans over 200 steps to make a single bag by hand. See some of the steps required to make a a Hudson Sutler bag below.

CRAFTSMANSHIP

World class artisans handcraft our bags using techniques that go back for generations.

INDUSTRIAL FABRICS

We don’t cut corners and certainly don’t compromise on materials. 18 oz canvas and 1000D Nylon. Built to last.

HAND CUT

Our materials are cut by hand to exacting specifications of our patterns.

HAND STITCHED

The body, gussets, liners, zippers and handles are sewn together by hand.

Quality

From leather to canvas, clips, zippers and handles, we deliver a superior quality product at a fair price.

Handmade

Every Hudson Sutler bag is crafted in over 200 handmade steps. We are committed to classic construction techniques that stand the test of time.

Timeless Designs

We build our bags with everything you need and nothing you don’t. Timeless design made for modern travel.
