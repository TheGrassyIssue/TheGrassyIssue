## Product range (FW26, 24 products live on 25 Oct 2026)
- The All Products collection lists 24 items. Source: https://albagolf.it/collections/all-products
- Every product is tagged `fw26`. SKUs begin `ASFW26…`. All were created on 15 Oct 2026 and published on 16 Oct 2026, so this looks like the brand's first (launch) collection. Source: https://albagolf.it/collections/all-products/products.json
- Categories (Shopify product_type): Outerwear (7: Crest Jacket, Shire Quarter Zip Jacket, Glen Padded Vest x3, Haar Reversible Vest x2). Tops (7: Cheviot Mockneck x2, Grian LS and SS quarter-zip polos, Voar LS polo x2, Voar SS polo, Rime Thermal Layer). Pants (5: Nevis Tech Trouser x3, Roy Tech Shorts x2). Accessories (4: driver and fairway barrel headcovers, pouch, caddie towel). The homepage tiles are labelled "Knitwear & Polo" and "Base Layer". Source: https://albagolf.it/
- Price range is EUR 50 to EUR 550, about USD 56 to USD 616 at 1.1201. Cheapest are the Alba Pouch and Caddie Towel (EUR 50). Most expensive is the Crest Jacket (EUR 550). No sale or compare-at prices. Source: catalogue.json, built from products.json and the product .js endpoints
- Stock: every product is in stock. The only sold-out variant is the Voar Ls Polo in Ivory, size L. Source: https://albagolf.it/collections/all-products/products.json
- Fabrics and mills:
  - Grian polos use "an exclusive REDA 1865 fabric, developed in a blend of Merino wool and Lyocell" (REDA Active). Source: https://albagolf.it/products/grian-ls-quarter-zip-polo
  - REDA is a Merino wool maker from Italy with roots in 1865. Source: https://www.reda1865.com/id/a-family-story
  - The Glen vest uses PrimaLoft Active Evolve insulation. Source: https://albagolf.it/products/glen-padded-vest-in-salmon
  - The Cheviot knit is 100% technical wool. Source: https://albagolf.it/products/cheviot-mockneck-in-black
  - The Crest and Shire jackets are a three-layer waterproof fabric with fully taped seams. Source: https://albagolf.it/products/crest-jacket
  - The Rime thermal layer was "developed from technical solutions derived from professional skiing and reinterpreted specifically for the demands of golf". Source: https://albagolf.it/products/rime-thermal-layer
- Product names: Glen, Haar, Cheviot, Shire, Nevis, Grian, Voar, Rime, Crest, Roy. Many of these are Scottish place names or weather words (for example Haar, Cheviot, Nevis). This is our own observation; the brand does not explain the names anywhere. Treat it as editorial colour and label it as an observation if used.

## Shipping to the US
- The US is on the shipping list. Source: https://albagolf.it/pages/terms-of-service-en (section 4.1)
- The country selector shows "United States (USD $)", so prices are displayed in USD for US visitors. Source: https://albagolf.it/pages/about
- Flat fee to the US (and to Canada and the Gulf/Asia group): **EUR 35**. Orders ship by courier from the brand's logistics centre in Italy. Source: https://albagolf.it/policies/shipping-policy
- Duties and import taxes outside the EU are paid by the customer on delivery. Sources: https://albagolf.it/policies/shipping-policy and terms section 3.1
- Returns: 14-day withdrawal window, customer pays return shipping, items must be unworn with tags. Size and colour exchanges are done by returning and placing a new order. Source: https://albagolf.it/pages/terms-of-service-en
- Payment methods: Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay. Source: same terms page
- The site sells only to consumers (section 1.2), so the terms show no wholesale. Source: same terms page
