## The collab
- Name on product: **A.P.Cph/Golf** — the two marks fused.
- Released **2026-09-02** on captainshelmgolf.com. 18 SKUs: 12 men's/unisex, 6 women's.
- Prices **¥4,400 – ¥28,600**. At ~¥160/USD (Sep 2 2026) that is roughly **$28 – $179**.

## Design details (from the brand's own JP product copy)
- Chest carries the co-branded **"A.P.Cph"** logo.
- Several tops print **RUE MADAME PARIS** across the back — the address of A.P.C.'s Paris flagship.
- The half-zip mock neck combines Cph/Golf's brand message **'NOT A GOLF GENTLEMAN'**
  with that A.P.C. address.
- Jacket is a no-collar, raglan-like cut to reduce swing restriction.
- Colours: black, navy, white, cream, light grey. Navy is the collab accent on the skirt.
- Fit reference throughout: model 183 cm wearing size L.

## A.P.C. Golf background
- A.P.C. is the Paris label founded by **Jean Touitou**; golf line grew out of his own playing.
- Golf line launched **Korea and Japan first (2021)**, later Europe — it has always been an
  Asia-first proposition, which is why a Tokyo collab is native rather than novel.
- VERBATIM, Jean Touitou, on premiering the first golf collection — quoted by Hypebeast
  (Julius Oppenheimer, 15 Mar 2023):
  > "I don't like the way that amateur players try to dress like professional golfers.
  > I just want them to be chic."
- A.P.C. branding on the golf line: the house abbreviation with "golf" in cursive beneath,
  plus a motif of a golf bag balanced against a guitar.
- A.P.C. named **Ludivine Poiblanc** artistic director in **May 2026**.

## Cph/Golf background
- Cph/Golf is the golf line of **CAPTAINS HELM**, a Tokyo label that filters a California
  outdoor-lifestyle influence through a Japanese lens.
- Streetwear-led golf apparel: printed slogans, unusual collars, rugged outer layers.

## Buying it from the US — verified
- **You cannot order direct.** captainshelmgolf.com is JP-market only.
- **A.P.C. Golf mainline IS sold in the US** — apc-us.com carries 12 A.P.C. Golf pieces,
  **$50–$350** (Jack golf cap $130, Collin polo $245, Céline polo $295, Stacy trousers $350).
  Note the contrast: the collab's polo is ~$117, roughly half A.P.C. Golf's own US polo price.
- Two indirect routes:
  - **Proxy service** (ZenMarket, Buyee) — buys on your behalf with a JP card/address, then
    forwards. Caveat: these are built around Mercari / Yahoo Auctions / Rakuten / Amazon JP;
    independent Shopify D2C stores are less well supported.
  - **Forwarding service** (gives you a Japanese address; you place the order yourself) —
    depends on the store accepting a foreign card.
- Sizing runs Japanese. Use the cm charts, not US letter sizes.
