# Hiroki Golf — research dossier
Captured 18 September 2026. hirokigolf.com

## What the brand is
Auckland, New Zealand. Sunday bags, stand bags, headcovers, towels, caps.
Shopify store `hiroki-golf-nz.myshopify.com`; `hiroki.co.nz` redirects to
`hirokigolf.com` — one store, not two. 43 published products.

## PRICING — READ THIS BEFORE QUOTING ANY NUMBER
The store runs Shopify Markets with **two separate price lists**, and the
`$` symbol is a bare `${{amount}}` with no currency code, so the site looks
identical in both:

| market | Kinloch Stand Bag | cart currency |
|---|---|---|
| United States | 350.00 | USD |
| New Zealand    | 550.00 | NZD |

NZD 550 x 0.58 = USD 320, so the US number is a separate price, NOT a conversion.

Two traps found and avoided:
1. `og:price:currency` on the product page reports **NZD** while serving the US
   number (350). Trusting it would have published "NZD 350" — wrong in both
   markets.
2. `/products.json?currency=NZD` returns 475.00 and `?currency=AUD` also returns
   475.00. The param is not honoured; both numbers are junk.

**All prices recorded below are the US market price in USD, read 18 Sep 2026.**
That is what a TGI reader pays. Verified by switching the storefront market via
`/localization` and re-reading the cart currency.

## Facts that are safe to use (brand's own statements)
- Name chosen to evoke Japanese craftsmanship and act as a filter on design.
- Military-grade aluminium clips, originally a fix for metal-on-metal noise,
  now a signature.
- Restrained neutral palette; storytelling leans fashion rather than sport.
- Site taglines: "Serious Golfers Only", "Born to Golf, Forced to Work".
