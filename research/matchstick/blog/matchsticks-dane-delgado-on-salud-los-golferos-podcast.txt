      Matchstick's Dane Delgado on the Salud Los Golferos Podcast
by DANE DELGADO
October 02, 2024
1 min read
For me, working in golf is about two things — making functional art, and meeting new people. Recently, I got to do the latter when I was invited onto the
Salud Los Golferos podcast
. 
If you're not familiar,
Los Golferos
is a latino golf brand based across the West Coast that celebrates the culture along with friendship. They make some really great products, including some
sarape towels
that I think are absolutely incredible.
It was a super fun conversation, and they're really good dudes. I'll tell you what this conversation was like: have you ever been invited to a cookout with a friend, but you're going to a house where your
friend
is the only person who knows everyone? And then you get there, and eight minutes in you're hanging around the grill with a beer with everyone like they're old friends? It was like that. 
The conversation meandered, but we generally went over:
The origin of Matchstick
Why I do what I do
Upcoming projects I'm working on
Some background about me that you may not know
And a lot more.
Give it a listed on
Apple Podcasts
or click the video above to watch the live version on YouTube.
