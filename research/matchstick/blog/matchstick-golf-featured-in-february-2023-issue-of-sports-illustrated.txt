      Matchstick Golf featured in February 2023 issue of Sports Illustrated
by DANE DELGADO
March 11, 2023
1 min read
As you may know from our 
About
 page, in my previous life I was a sportswriter. I worked for several companies, including SB Nation, Sporting News, and NBC Sports. Now that Matchstick Golf is my pride and joy, I'm still involved in sports but in a much different way. So I was surprised and elated when I found out that 
writer and golfer Gabrielle Herzig
 had featured Matchstick in her spread in the 
February 2023 issue of Sports Illustrated
.
I of course ordered several copies for myself and my family, and I'll soon get the sheets framed and put somewhere in my house. The feature includes our friends over at 
Jones Sports Co.
, as well as the guys at 
L.A.B. Golf
, 
Bluegrass
, and 
Pinned
. And of course, there's big boys in there as well — can't forget the 
Taylormade
 irons or the 
Callaway
 Paradym driver.
Thanks again to Gabrielle for looking out for us little guys. Matchstick has been a dream to run these past couple of years, and we couldn't have ended up in one of the world's biggest and most well-recognized sports magazines without all of you. Thank you for supporting Matchstick, buying the product, and telling your friends about the dope golf gear you've been getting from us. 
Can't wait to show you what we have coming in 2023. Next stop: the cover?
