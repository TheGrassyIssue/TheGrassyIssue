## THE SHAPE OF THE STORY

Four public venues opened in thirteen months — 1872 (Oct 2025), Bogey Master (Oct 2025),
Swing Station (Nov 2025), Fiber Golf (May 2026) — and five more are in the pipeline.
The growth is **suburban and exurban**, not central: Georgetown, Hutto, San Marcos, far
southwest Austin. Georgetown alone will have two by January. Meanwhile downtown Austin
still has none, and the one announced for Congress Avenue has no date.

The other real shift is a split in the format. The staffed room with a bar and a kitchen
(Golfinity, X-Golf, ROK, Spare Birdie, 1872) now competes with the unstaffed 24/7 keycode
box (Fiber, Back Nine, Bogey Master, ATX, Man Cave), and the second model is the one
multiplying. ROK sells against it explicitly on their own homepage.
