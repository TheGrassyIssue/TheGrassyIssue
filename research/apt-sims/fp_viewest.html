Floor Plans

Studio, 1, 2 & 3 Bedroom Apartments in Austin

Whether you prefer the streamlined footprint of a studio, the open flow of a one-bedroom, or the added space of a two- or three-bedroom home, the floor plans at View at Estancia are designed with elevated living in mind.
Our studio, 1, 2 & 3 bedroom apartments in Austin are all thoughtfully arranged spaces. Every residence includes granite countertops, tile backsplashes, pendant lighting, wood-style flooring, spacious closets, a washer and dryer, and a private patio or balcony. Select homes add a private fenced yard.
Located just off I-35 and SH-45, your new home pairs interior elegance with everyday convenience. Choose a floor plan at View at Estancia, where refined design meets daily livability.


Introducing Total Monthly Leasing Price

Planning your budget is essential, and we’re here to make it simpler. Our Total Monthly Leasing Price reflects all fixed, mandatory fees alongside your base rent, giving you a complete picture of your monthly costs. To customize your Total Monthly Leasing Price and plan with confidence, use our Calculate My Costs tool found within the Map view. We’ve also provided a list of all potential fees you may encounter as a resident which can be found at the bottom of the page.

Transparency meets convenience—so you can focus on finding the perfect home.
