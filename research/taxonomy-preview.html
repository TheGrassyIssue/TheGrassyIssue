Gorpcore Golf Brands

7 brands in the Index

The smallest group in the Index and the one moving fastest. Gorpcore in golf means the construction language of the outdoors — ripstop, Dyneema, webbing, carabiner hardware, bucket hats — applied to a game that spent thirty years dressing like an office.

Two routes in. Gramicci and Carhartt WIP came from actual outdoor and workwear lineages and were adopted by golfers. Sentinel Golf, Agronomy Workshop and Odd Ritual went the other way, building golf product out of outdoor materials on purpose. Realtree sits oddly and usefully between the two. If you walk your rounds and carry, this is the shelf that was designed for you rather than adapted to you.
