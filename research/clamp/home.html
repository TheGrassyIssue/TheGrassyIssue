WHAT OUR HEADCOVERS ARE MADE WITH

Our headcovers are made with a variety of different materials including faux leather, genuine leather, upholstery fabrics and many more. All our headcovers are made with a fleece inner to protect the club heads whilst all being sewn together using a bonded nylon thread which is one of the strongest, most durable threads available.

Custom headcovers

Looking for a unique and personalised touch for your golf gear? Look no further!

Because we make every headcover in house, from start to finish, we have the ability to customise each one to your exact specifications.

Our embroidery machines can stitch up to 800 stitches per minute and embroider up to 20 colors per design. This means we can add logos, illustrations, and other designs to your headcovers, making them truly one-of-a-kind.

If you're interested in creating custom head cover, please complete the form below. Our team will work closely with you to understand your design preferences and provide you with a quote. Once we receive your approval, we'll start crafting your unique headcovers!

Whether you're looking to promote your business on the golf course or simply want to show off your unique style, our custom headcovers are the perfect solution.
