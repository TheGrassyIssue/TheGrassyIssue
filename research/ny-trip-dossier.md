# Field Notes — a week of New York golf (Aug 25 – Sep 1, 2026)
Built 2026-09-02. Lenny's trip. Photos are his (iPhone, HEIC), day order from EXIF.

## Division of sourcing

  Nothing about how a course *played* is inferred.

## Sedgewood Club — Carmel, NY (Aug 25)
Sourced: founded **1928**, private, **9 holes, 3,005 yards, par 35**, set in roughly
**1,200 acres of hilltop woodland** in the lower Hudson Valley, just over an hour
north of NYC. Also two red clay tennis courts, a spring-fed lake, a boathouse.
Architect not documented.
Lenny: his parents' club, grew up on it. Elevation changes, "surprising difficulty",
familiarity. **No tee times, rarely crowded.** Deep bench of good players who just
jump on. Hudson Valley views. Almost never keeps score, still notes a birdie.
**Stella** — his dog, a mixed lab — is the one on the green.

## The Links at Union Vale — Lagrangeville, NY (Aug 26)
Sourced: **Stephen Kay** design, opened to the public **2000**, **6,839 yards, par 72**,
southern Dutchess County, under 90 minutes north of NYC. Links-style: bent and
fescue grasses, pot bunkers, natural hazards. Named Best New Public Course of the
Year by Suburban Golf Magazine.
Lenny: "absolute gem", true links style, fun interesting holes. Gorgeous day,
gentle breeze, plays quickly, easy to keep the ball in play. **Shot 84** — best
round of the week.

## Bethpage Black — Farmingdale, NY (Sep 1)
Sourced: **A.W. Tillinghast** (Joseph Burbeck officially credited, Tillinghast
consulting), opened **1936**. **Par 71, 7,468 yards** from the back. **Walking only**,
no carts for anyone. One of **five 18-hole courses** at Bethpage State Park — Black,
Red, Blue, Green, Yellow; Tillinghast did Black/Red/Blue and reworked the Green,
Alfred Tull's Yellow opened 1958. Hosted **US Opens 2002 and 2009**, the Barclays
**2012 and 2016**, the **PGA Championship 2019**, and the **2025 Ryder Cup**.
Booking through the NY State parks system, non-residents five days out. Green fees
for non-residents roughly **$140–$160** in 2025, residents about half.
The warning sign by the first tee, transcribed from Lenny's photo IMG_2503:
> WARNING — The Black Course Is An Extremely Difficult Course Which We Recommend
> Only For Highly Skilled Golfers
Lenny: got on via a **cancellation**, found by constantly refreshing the booking
site. Arriving is incredible — "golf as far as the eye can see". A rainy morning
wiped most of the morning tee times; **they went off first after the delay lifted**.
Intimidation on the first tee is real and nerve-wracking; he'd been playing the
course on a simulator, so **par on the first**. Walking only "was a slog" but the
course is incredible. Constant risk/reward off every tee. Greens all very well
guarded. Design is "a relic of a bygone era and it shows, they just do not build
courses like this." Solid front nine; back nine ramps up distance and difficulty.
