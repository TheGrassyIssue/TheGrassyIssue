# St. André Golf — notes (28 Sep 2026)
Atlanta golf sketch-comedy group first, merch second. Founder Aaron Chewning (Bridgestone/TheOrg say "co-founder"; PGA.com "founder"). Cast: Hannah Rae Aslesen, Jonathan Pawlowski. Launched on social Aug 2022. Name = St Andrews + André 3000. AJC: design partner + four investment partners incl. Collin McHugh. Bridgestone partner since Mar 2023; filmed with Tiger Dec 2022. Palm Golf glove collab. No About page, empty blog, no lookbook; press kit on request social@standre.golf. Where made: not stated.
Quotes (verbatim, read on page): MyGolfSpy 13 Feb 2025 (x2), AJC 9 Apr 2025 (x2), On The Green 5 Sep 2025 ("We wanted it to look classy on a shirt or merch..."), Bridgestone release 21 Mar 2023.
