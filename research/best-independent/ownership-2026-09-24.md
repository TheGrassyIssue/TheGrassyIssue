# Ownership check — 25 Best Independent Golf Brands (24 Sep 2026)

Verdicts and sources live in data/independence.json (the builder reads it).
Method: three research passes (web search + fetch) over the ~45 most-mentioned brands.
Rule: founder/family-owned, no parent, not listed, no named VC/PE/strategic stake.
Crowdfunding, small friends-and-family angels, debt, collabs = allowed. Named owner required.

Out (outside money or corporate owner): Malbon (~$28M, 2025), Manors (Redrice Ventures, Mar 2026),
Sugarloaf (Pro Shop, Jun 2024), Quiet Golf (Old Tom Capital, Oct 2024), STITCH (repeated raises,
non-founder chair), Eastside (EP Golf Ventures seed 2024 + Centric JV Aug 2026 — independent tag removed),
Winston Collection (Imperial, Jan 2026), Siegelman Stable (VC-backed 2023), PUMA (listed), Realtree (not a golf brand).

Unclear, held back until an owner is named: Odd Ritual, Cloud & Wind, Merrill Golf, water when dry.

Re-check before next year's list: Metalwood (founder said a raise was coming), Sounder (minority
shareholders on the Sep 2025 Companies House confirmation statement), Pins & Aces (Edel deal terms),
Walker (ASIC shareholder extract is paid).

Location notes found along the way (brands.json not changed): Random Golf Club is HQ'd in Austin;
Devereux lists Tempe, AZ; Fyfe is Edinburgh.
