## 1. Tobacco Road Golf Club — Sanford, NC

- **Access:** Fully public daily-fee (Troon-managed shop). Not a resort; optional on-site stay (Stewart Cabin).
- **Airport:** Raleigh-Durham (RDU). Club says it is "30 minutes from Pinehurst and Raleigh."
- **Architect / year:** Mike Strantz, 1998 ("EST. 1998" on site).
- **Why Mind:** The club pitches it as "asks you to think differently." From the club's own hole guide: a **blind approach to a plateau green on No. 9**, a **"blind bathtub green" approach on the par-5 13th**, a **"heroic blind drive" on 18**, and a triple-tiered green. Local rules: **no out of bounds anywhere**, and **every sand area plays as a waste area** (you can ground your club). New in Aug 2026: **The Matchbox**, a 12.5-hole synthetic-turf short course routed through the trees by Carlton Marshall and former Strantz shaper Mark White.
- **2026 green fee:** **Not published.** Booking runs through a dynamic-pricing engine (Noteefy) that blocks scraping. Third-party only: Where2Golf (2024) lists $277–284 high season with cart, **UNVERIFIED**. Cheaper winter/July maintenance rates are flagged on the booking page.
- **Walking / caddies:** "Golf carts are included in your green fee." Walking is **UNVERIFIED** (aggregator GolfN says "walking allowed"; the club doesn't say). The Matchbox is walking-only. No caddie program mentioned.
- **Solo signals:** Tobacco Road Travel builds trips "from singles and couples to bigger groups of 16." The Stewart Cabin (sleeps 4, overlooks No. 14) comes with **after-hours access to the course and The Matchbox**. Pack House counter food and bar; you can reach The Matchbox clubhouse as a halfway house from the 13th tee. "Strantz Connection" package pairs it with Tot Hill Farm.
- **Booking window:** Not published.
- **Quote:** "Nothing in golf is more exciting than pulling off a shot you thought was beyond your capabilities." (Mike Strantz, quoted on https://tobaccoroadgolf.com/)
- **Red flags:** **Only 17 holes open Jan–Feb 2027** (irrigation project, special rate). **Closed June 28–July 8, 2027** for aerification. Strict dress code (no denim or athletic wear). Divisive: reviewers call it either genius or gimmick.
- **Photography:** https://tobaccoroadgolf.com/golf/ (hole-by-hole course tour) and https://tobaccoroadgolf.com/news-and-videos/
- **Sources:** https://tobaccoroadgolf.com/ · https://tobaccoroadgolf.com/need-to-know/ · https://tobaccoroadgolf.com/golf/ · https://tobaccoroadgolf.com/stay-and-play/ · https://www.where2golf.com/usa-carolinas/tobacco-road-golf-club/ (third-party fee) · https://www.golfn.com/courses/united-states-of-america/north-carolina/tobacco-road-golf-club (third-party walking)

## 2. Bethpage State Park, Black Course — Farmingdale, NY

- **Access:** Fully public (NY State Parks). The Black is the hardest public tee time to get on this list that isn't sold by lottery.
- **Airport:** LaGuardia (LGA), JFK, or Long Island MacArthur (ISP). Distances **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Architect / year:** A.W. Tillinghast, built in the 1930s (state park page). Opening year 1936 and the Rees Jones pre-2002 work are **UNVERIFIED** here.
- **Why Mind:** Honestly, Bethpage is more brute force than puzzle. Its Mind case is the psychology: the famous warning sign, and having to manage a major-championship course on foot. It hosted the 2002 and 2009 U.S. Opens, the 2019 PGA and the 2025 Ryder Cup (state park page). **Weaker Mind fit than the design-led picks.**
- **2026 green fee (state price list dated 4/22/2026):** Black, 18 holes: **non-NY residents $140 weekday / $160 weekend**; NY residents $70 / $80. Twilight for non-residents $88 / $100. Plus a $5 reservation fee per player, a $15 no-show fee, and a $15 fee to cancel within 48 hours.
- **Walking / caddies:** **Walking-only.** The park lists carts as permitted on Red, Blue, Green and Yellow, not Black. Hand pull carts cost $7. Caddies must be booked through the Bethpage pro shop (516-249-4040). Fees are set by the pro shop and go entirely to the caddie.
- **Solo signals:** A single can claim a walk-up spot. The first 6–12 Black tee times each day go to walk-ups, plus one walk-up foursome per hour. The Heritage Club public house and bar is on site. No lodging.
- **Booking window:** Online only via the state system. **Non-NY residents book 5 days out, from 7:00 PM**; verified NY residents get 7 days. Only **one Black reservation per person every 28 days**. The person who books must pay and play. Reservations are non-transferable, and resale gets you a 1-year ban.
- **Notoriously hard:** The overnight car line. Parking spots are numbered, first come first served, and a staffer hands out tee-time tickets around 4:30 AM (GOLF.com). The Black is **closed Mondays** (except holidays) and open **April 15–Nov 15** only.
- **Quote:** "golfers so eager to play the Black that they're willing to spend the night in their cars." (Josh Sens, GOLF.com, https://golf.com/travel/bethpage-black-tee-time-how/)
- **Red flags:** The resident/non-resident price gap and booking window. Hosting the **2028 KPMG Women's PGA** will mean closures around that event (dates **UNVERIFIED**).

## 3. Pinehurst No. 2 (plus No. 10 and The Cradle) — Pinehurst, NC

- **Access: RESORT-GUEST ONLY for No. 2.** Pinehurst FAQ: "To play courses 2, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 or 11 you must be a member or resort guest." Non-guests can only walk on Nos. 1, 3 and 5 (April–October) and **The Cradle (year-round)**, booking from 1 day before at (855) 835-0771 opt 5. **This fails the "open to non-guests" test.** A 2-night minimum for No. 2 appears in Pinehurst-area CVB package listings (homeofgolf.com), **not confirmed on pinehurst.com**.
- **Airport:** Raleigh-Durham (RDU). Distance **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Architect / year:** Donald Ross, opened 1907; restored by Coore & Crenshaw (resort site). No. 10: Tom Doak, 2024. The Cradle: 9-hole, 789-yard par-27 short course, 2017 (Gil Hanse, per general knowledge, **UNVERIFIED** on the page fetched).
- **Why Mind:** No. 2 is the American textbook on **turtleback, crowned greens** (resort: "iconic sloped turtleback greens") ringed by sandy wiregrass waste. Hitting the green is a chess move, because missing on the wrong side is the whole test. No. 10 is walking-only.
- **2026 green fee:** **No standalone No. 2 rate published.** The resort sells No. 2 via packages that "bundle all golf fees and course premiums." CVB package listings show a **$250 No. 2 premium** (2026, **UNVERIFIED** on the resort site). Older GOLF.com figures (2023–24): second No. 2 round $595 peak / $360 off-peak; a $470 standalone figure from a 2023 spokesperson quote. **Treat as historical.**
- **Walking / caddies:** No. 2 and No. 4 are **cart-path only**; No. 10 is **walking only**; push carts available. Caddie $85/bag (most share between two), forecaddie $45/player, **single bag $100 (confirmed day of play only)**. Recommended gratuity is $65+ per bag.
- **Solo signals:** Big resort with many bars (The Deuce, Pinehurst Brewing, Tavern), so it's easy to be alone in company. No published singles-pairing policy. The Cradle and its walk-on rules are the casual solo entry point.
- **Booking window:** Resort guests book with the stay. Non-guest walk-ons open 1 day before.
- **Quote:** None independent sourced yet. Resort copy: "No. 2's iconic sloped turtleback greens and wide fairways with native wiregrass and sand" (https://www.pinehurst.com/golf/courses/no-2/). **Need an attributable architect or writer quote.**
- **Red flags:** Guest-only access and price. No. 11 (Coore & Crenshaw at "Pinehurst Sandmines") opens 2027 and may draw demand.
- **Photography:** https://www.pinehurst.com/golf/courses/no-2/ (course page images and Vimeo flyover). No dedicated media room found. Also https://www.pinehurst.com/news-blog/

## 4. Old Macdonald, Bandon Dunes Golf Resort — Bandon, OR

- **Access:** Resort, **open to day guests** at a higher rate.
- **Airport:** Southwest Oregon Regional (OTH, North Bend) is closest. Portland (PDX) is the big hub; drive time **UNVERIFIED**. Resort has a "How to get to Bandon Dunes" page.
- **Architect / year:** Tom Doak and Jim Urbina, as a tribute to C.B. Macdonald. Opened 2010 (general knowledge, **UNVERIFIED** on page).
- **Why Mind:** It's a living catalogue of template holes. The resort's hole guide names them: Double Plateau, Eden, **Sahara (blind tee shot over a dune)**, Hog's Back, Short, Long, Biarritz ("A two-putt on this hole is a feat of its own"), Cape, Road, Redan, Leven, Maiden, **Alps (blind approach; ring the bell when you've putted out)**, Littlestone and Punchbowl. The resort describes it as "vast greens" with "myriad angles of play."
- **2026 green fee (resort site, "2026 & 2027 Rates"):** Same rate for all five 18-hole courses. **Day guest $180 (Jan/Dec) to $425 (June–Sept)**; premium day guest (booked more than 21 days out) $360–$475; resort guest $130–$375. Oregon residents pay the resort-guest rate. Replay rounds are roughly half price.
- **Walking / caddies:** **Walking-only resort-wide.** Carts only for documented permanent disability, booked in advance, and a caddie is still required. Caddie fee **$125/bag/round plus gratuity**, paid direct to the caddie. Many caddies are Evans Scholars.
- **Solo signals:** Has a dedicated **"Single & Double Rooms"** lodging category, and the trip-planner form offers a **"Golf Trip – Solo"** option. Lots of communal spaces: McKee's Pub, Bunker Bar, Punchbowl putting course. A formal singles-pairing policy is **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Booking window:** Lodging is taken through 2027. **2028 lodging is released by lottery drawings in four windows:** Jan–Mar 2028 closed; Apr–May drawn Nov 9, 2026; Jun–Oct window open Nov 9, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027; Nov–Dec window Jan 18 to May 2, 2027. Day guests: Oregon rate only within 21 days. Booking more than 21 days ahead costs the premium rate, with tee times after 10 AM, up to a year out.
- **Quote:** "The hottest course in America right now – looks like it was lifted, cleaned and placed from the West of Scotland." (Golf Digest, as quoted by the resort at https://bandondunesgolf.com/golf/golf-courses/old-macdonald-golf-course/). Trim to fit 20 words.
- **Red flags:** Hard to book in peak months, and it's a long way from anywhere. Weather.
- **Photography:** https://bandondunesgolf.com/golf/golf-courses/old-macdonald-golf-course/ and https://bandondunesgolf.com/golf-resort-info/bandon-dunes-course-cameras/ (live turnstand cam). No press room link found.

## 5. Sweetens Cove Golf Club (9 holes) — South Pittsburg, TN

- **Access:** Fully public, by **day pass only** (no tee times, no walk-ons).
- **Airport:** Chattanooga (CHA) closest; Nashville (BNA) for more flights. Distances **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Architect / year:** Rob Collins and Tad King (King-Collins), on the old Sequatchie Valley course site. Open by 2014 (USGA article, Oct 2014). Some listings say 2011 for the rebuild.
- **Why Mind:** It's a nine-hole inland links with bold contours and lots of tightly mown short grass. A GOLF.com feature describes a **double green shared by holes 3 and 6 that rises about 20 feet** across huge interior slopes. Multiple tees and an all-day format mean you replay holes from new angles.
- **2026 rates (club site, peak April 1–Oct 31, 2026):** Thu–Sun all-day **$140 walking / $190 cart**. Tue–Wed all-day $125 walking, 18 holes $80 walking, 9 holes $30. Nov–Dec 2026: Thu–Sun all-day $100 walking. Plus a **$15 non-refundable booking fee per pass**. **2027 changes:** all-day only, peak Thu–Sun $240 walking / $290 cart. This includes a **$150 non-refundable deposit** and covers food and non-alcoholic drinks.
- **Walking / caddies:** Walk or ride. **Cart-path only Nov 1–Apr 30.** No caddie program mentioned.
- **Solo signals: strongest on the list.** "Passes equal people (one pass = one person)." Everyone gathers at the shed around **8:30 AM for course notes and a shot of whiskey**, then each player is **assigned a starting hole**. You can check in later in the day. Capacity is capped at **60 passes Tue–Thu and 45 Fri–Sun**. Food truck on site daily.
- **Booking window:** Online only. **No waitlist**; cancelled passes reappear instantly. Last-minute next-day passes may be available by calling or emailing after 5 PM CT. 2027 lodging pass wave started **Oct 22, 2026**, first through partners No Laying Up and E9 Retreat.
- **Quote:** "Writing about Augusta then, MacKenzie could have been describing Sweetens today." (Anthony Pioppi, *To The Nines*, quoted at https://sweetenscovegolfclub.com/pages/about)
- **Red flags:** Sells out. Closed Mondays (except some holidays). 2027 prices roughly 70% higher (deposit plus food included). Only 9 holes.
- **Photography:** https://sweetenscovegolfclub.com/pages/course-information (hole pages /pages/1st-hole … /9th-hole) and press at https://sweetenscovegolfclub.com/blogs/press
- **Sources:** https://sweetenscovegolfclub.com/pages/daily-rates · https://sweetenscovegolfclub.com/pages/frequently-asked-questions · https://sweetenscovegolfclub.com/pages/about · https://sweetenscovegolfclub.com/pages/golf-pass-release-lodging-options · https://www.usga.org/articles/2014/10/sweetens-cove-helps-promote-enjoyment-of-ninehole-rounds-21474873500.html · https://www.golf.com/travel/features-travel/2019/09/03/sweetens-cove-king-collins-accord-new-york/

## 6. Streamsong Black (also Red and Blue) — Bowling Green, FL

- **Access:** Resort, **open to day guests**.
- **Airport:** Tampa (TPA) or Orlando (MCO), "about 90 minutes from both" (resort FAQ).
- **Architect / year:** Black by Gil Hanse, 2017 (Golf Digest Best New Public 2018 per press). Red by Coore & Crenshaw; Blue by Tom Doak (both 2012 per general knowledge, **UNVERIFIED** on page). All built on a former phosphate mine.
- **Why Mind:** On Black the resort describes "strategic rolls and tumbles." Its featured holes: the 2nd is "a test of course management… elevated green with false edges." The **9th is a hidden punchbowl** where you aim at a windmill and play a blind shot into a funnel green. Press notes very large greens and a dual putting surface on 13. Red and Blue add Coore & Crenshaw and Doak strategy on the same property, which is three ways of thinking in one trip.
- **2026–27 green fees (resort site, Black, Red and Blue):** Oct 1–Dec 14, 2026: **resort guest $375, day guest $425**. Peak Jan 14–Apr 30, 2027: resort $395, day $445. Summer Jun 24–Sep 14, 2027: resort $170, day $220 (Fri–Sun) or $170 (Mon–Thu). Carts are an **extra $35** and require a group caddie. FL/GA resident rates also published. The Chain (short course, walking only) costs $95–115.
- **Walking / caddies:** Walking encouraged. **Walking-only Dec 24–Feb 28.** Walking caddie $120 per player double bag or **$150 single bag**, plus gratuity. A group caddie ($60/player) is required with a cart.
- **Solo signals:** On-site Lodge plus "Golf Cabins," with several restaurants and bars. **Caution: tee times before 8 AM are foursomes-only** and must use a walking or group caddie. A singles policy is otherwise **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Booking window:** **Day guests get a 30-day window** (through 5/31/2027 per site). Resort guests book further out.
- **Quote:** From the resort: No. 9 is a "punchbowl green where you need to aim for the windmill off the tee and navigate a blind shot" (https://www.streamsongresort.com/golf/streamsong-black/). A Golf Digest panelist line about "18 of the largest greens I've ever seen" showed up in a search snippet only, **UNVERIFIED** (Golf Digest blocked fetch).
- **Red flags:** **Black closed July 27–Aug 5, 2027**; Red July 11–22; Blue Aug 15–26 (aerification). Expensive for day guests in season. Summer heat. Some purists call Black gimmicky (per Golf Digest via search, **UNVERIFIED**).

## 9. Landmand Golf Club — Homer, NE

- **Access:** Fully public. No memberships, no corporate outings. Optional on-site cabins.
- **Airport:** Sioux Gateway (SUX, Sioux City) closest. Omaha (OMA): the course is "about 90 miles north of Omaha" (Golfweek).
- **Architect / year:** King-Collins (Rob Collins and Tad King); first original King-Collins 18. Opened for regular play Sept 3, 2022 (Golfweek).
- **Why Mind:** It sits on rolling Loess Hills farmland with almost no trees and no water. **Fairways run more than 100 yards wide and greens average more than 14,000 sq ft** (Cowboy State Daily, citing WSJ coverage). The flags are **windsocks**. Width means every shot is a choice of angle, not a straight line.
- **Fees:** 2026 season **$150** (widely reported: GBN and WSJ via Cowboy State Daily). **2027 rate on club FAQ: $175 per golfer, cart included, no replay rate.** $25 non-refundable deposit per tee time.
- **Walking / caddies:** Walking allowed. FAQ: "Walk 18? We'll buy your first beer." No caddie program mentioned. Max 2 carts per group, no fivesomes, no extra walkers added to foursomes.
- **Solo signals:** **"If you are a single golfer, you will be paired up with another single golfer."** On site: Farmer's Table restaurant (7:15 AM–9 PM), a comfort station at the 8th tee, and cabins. The Loess Hill Layover package ($2,500, 1 night, 4 people, 36 holes) is **sold out for 2027**. Discounted rate at the South Sioux City Marriott.
- **Booking window / notoriously hard:** The whole season goes on sale at once, historically **8 AM on New Year's Eve**. 2025 sold out in 50 minutes (Golfweek). **All 11,000 tee times for 2026 sold out in 45 minutes** (Cowboy State Daily, Mar 2026), and the WSJ called it America's toughest tee time. A Whoosh-app waitlist catches cancellations. Reselling is banned. 2027 season runs **May 15–Sept 30**.
- **Quote:** "Walk 18? We'll buy your first beer." (Landmand FAQ, https://www.landmandgc.com/faq). Alternatively, owner Will Andersen in Golfweek via Yahoo: https://au.sports.yahoo.com/landmand-golf-nebraska-sold-2025-220702718.html
- **Red flags:** Near-impossible booking. Short season. A cart-first culture (Where2Golf: "almost all golfers ride").
- **Photography:** https://www.landmandgc.com/press (press page; homepage images credited to Brandon Carter @offthemownpath and Scott Brooks @pnw_golfer, so check rights)
- **Sources:** https://www.landmandgc.com/faq · https://www.landmandgc.com/ · https://cowboystatedaily.com/2026/03/15/toughest-tee-time-to-book-in-america-is-at-a-course-in-rural-nebraska/ · https://golfbusinessnews.com/news/travel/landmand-tee-times-sell-out-in-record-time/ · https://au.sports.yahoo.com/landmand-golf-nebraska-sold-2025-220702718.html · https://www.where2golf.com/usa-midwest-upper/landmand-golf-club/

## 10. Tot Hill Farm Golf Club — Asheboro, NC

- **Access:** Fully public daily-fee. Has homeowner and annual-pass tiers, but tee times are open to all.
- **Airport:** Greensboro (GSO), Charlotte (CLT) or Raleigh-Durham (RDU). Club says it is "50 minutes from Pinehurst."
- **Architect / year:** Mike Strantz. Opened 2000 (general knowledge, **UNVERIFIED** on site). The club says Strantz built it **on horseback** (his horse Scout), the only course he did that way.
- **Why Mind:** It's the Strantz pair with Tobacco Road: "mountainous terrain with tons of movement," framed by the club around MacKenzie's "spirit of adventure" versus the "card and pencil" mindset.
