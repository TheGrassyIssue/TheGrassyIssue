# Mind / Body / Spirit trips: SPIRIT candidates

Research checked Oct 27, 2026. Official course or resort sources come first; secondary sources are labelled. "Unverified" means I could not confirm it from a source I fetched.

**Spirit brief:** profound beauty, set in nature, routed with the land rather than bulldozed, quiet and remote. Bonus angle: going alone.

**Access key:** PUBLIC = daily-fee, anyone can book. RESORT-OPEN = resort that also sells tee times to non-guests ("day guests"). Private clubs are excluded.

---

## 1. We-Ko-Pa Golf Club, Saguaro Course (Fort McDowell Yavapai Nation, AZ)

- **Access:** PUBLIC. It is a Fort McDowell Yavapai Nation enterprise.
- **Location / airport:** 18200 E. WeKoPa Way, Fort McDowell AZ. The club says the adjacent We-Ko-Pa Casino Resort is "20 minutes from the airport" (PHX).
- **Architect / year:** Bill Coore and Ben Crenshaw. Built 2005–2006 (club's Cholla vs Saguaro page); opened Dec 2006 per a secondary source. The sister course, Cholla, is by Scott Miller (2000–01).
- **Why Spirit:**
  - The club says Saguaro is "built for walking – extremely rare for the area," that "it follows the natural movement of the land, they didn't move much dirt," and that "hardly any dirt was moved."
  - Coore and Crenshaw "continuously walked the land of the new golf site for two months" before choosing a routing.
  - Golf Digest notes "360-degree views of four surrounding mountains: Superstition, McDowell, Mazatzal and the Four Peaks."
  - **No homes on the course:** one review claims there is zero residential development. I did not confirm this from an official source, so treat it as unverified.
- **2026 fee (official, Rates & Packages page, current season):**
  - Oct 23, 2026 – Jan 7, 2027: Resort Rate $239, AZ ID $179.
  - Jan 8 – Feb 8, 2027: $329. Feb 9–14, 2027: $359. Feb 15 – Apr 25, 2027: $329.
  - Summer floor (Jun 14 – Aug 26, 2027): $109.
  - Add 9% tax. 36-hole packages run from $188 to $679.
  - No dynamic pricing. No third-party tee-time resale.
- **Walking / caddies:** The course is designed for walking (club). The walking policy itself is not stated on the official site; a directory lists walking as permitted (unverified). I found no caddie program.
- **Solo signals:** Stay-and-play with the We-Ko-Pa Casino Resort (246 rooms), booked through a named concierge. Online booking up to 90 days ahead. No published single-golfer program.
- **Quote:** "This ol' boney ground has some sting to it." — Ben Crenshaw, quoted on https://wekopa.com/saguaro-course/
- **Red flags:**
  - The resort next door is a casino, which works against the Spirit mood.
  - Saguaro closed Oct 12–29, 2026 for overseed and reopened Oct 30. It also closes for summer aerification (2026 dates were Jul 20 – Aug 2).
  - Peak-season fees run $329–$359.
- **Photography:** https://wekopa.com/saguaro-course/ (course gallery). Golf Digest credits course photos to Lonna Tucker / Courtesy of We-Ko-Pa.
- **Sources:**
  - https://wekopa.com/rates-and-packages/
  - https://wekopa.com/saguaro-course/
  - https://wekopa.com/cholla-vs-saguaro/

## 2. Black Mesa Golf Club (Santa Clara Pueblo, near Española, NM)

- **Access:** PUBLIC ("Open to the Public"). It is an enterprise of Santa Clara Development Corporation and part of Santa Claran Hotel & Casino.
- **Location / airport:** 25 miles north of downtown Santa Fe (club). Nearest airports are SAF and ABQ; drive times unverified.
- **Architect / year:** Baxter Spann, 2003. Golf Digest's "America's Best New" (club).
- **Why Spirit:**
  - Golf Digest: "there is no development on or near the vast, high-desert property... flowing through a Martian landscape of buttes, sagebrush-dotted ridges and dry arroyos."
  - The club says the course "takes advantage of the natural topography, unique rock formations and natural vegetation indigenous to the region."
  - A Golf Digest panelist (2025) mentions "dirt cart paths that are mostly hidden" and "no homes or other structures in sight."
  - Rankings: #85 on Golf Digest's 100 Greatest Public, #3 in New Mexico.
- **2026 fee:** Not published on the official site; pricing lives in the booking engine.
  - Golf Digest (2026 listing): "weekday green fee of $89," and a $119 top of range.
  - Fees are among the cheapest on Golf Digest's 100 Greatest Public list.
- **Walking / caddies:** Policy unverified. I found no caddies.
- **Solo signals:** Book online (rguest) or by phone. Planet Golf says the booking window is 2 weeks (unverified). Lodging is at the Santa Claran in Española, linked from the club site. A grill is open seasonally.
- **Quote:** "This has been the most interesting and spectacular site I have ever had the opportunity to work with." — Baxter Spann, on https://golfblackmesa.com/ (21 words; trim it to the opening clause if needed).
- **Red flags:**
  - **Open Thursday–Monday only** in every season, so it is closed Tuesday and Wednesday.
  - Panelists flag conditioning (thin bunkers, burnt spots) and many blind shots.
  - The lodging partner is a casino hotel.
- **Photography:** https://golfblackmesa.com/media/

## 3. Silvies Valley Ranch (Seneca, OR)

- **Access:** RESORT-OPEN with conditions. Official FAQ: "Day guests are welcome to visit with advance reservation and deposit," and pay a per-person daily resort fee that covers a golf cart and "access to book a premium activity or tee time based on availability."
- **Location / airport:** 10000 Rendezvous Lane, Seneca OR.
  - Ranch FAQ: "Most people drive, many fly to Boise, Redmond or Portland and then drive." There is a private airstrip, and the ranch will pick up at Burns or John Day.
  - Golf Course Gurus (secondary): "three hours east of Bend."
- **Architect / year:** Dan Hixson. The golf operation opened in 2017 (ranch site; the site misspells his name "Hixon").
  - **Correction to the brief:** Craddock and Hankins are Hixson's two reversible 18s. McVeigh's Gauntlet is the 7-hole goat-caddie course, not a designer.
  - Also on site: Chief Egan (9-hole par 3 along Paiute Creek) and Claire's Course (18-hole reversible putting course).
- **Why Spirit:**
  - The working ranch covers over 150,000 deeded and leased acres (ranch). The owners describe "vital wildlife and fish habitat restoration" as part of a multi-year recovery plan.
  - **No cell service on the ranch** (FAQ); there is Starlink Wi-Fi.
  - High desert at 5,000 ft.
  - Reversible courses, "the first of their kind in the U.S." (ranch).
  - Goat caddies on Chief Egan and McVeigh's.
- **2026 fee:** Not published. Day-guest resort fee amount not published.
  - Secondary figures are undated or stale: GOLF.com lists a $160 max fee; a US Kids event listed practice rounds at $65; one package listing starts at $757 per person per night all-inclusive.
  - Call (541) 573-5150.
- **Walking / caddies:** Resort fee includes a golf cart, and the property is "golf cart accessible only." A walking policy is not stated; a secondary review says "walking encouraged" (unverified). Goat caddies are the caddie program.
- **Solo signals (strongest of the set):**
  - Small property. The 2021 SCGA FORE piece says 34 rooms and 46 beds; current counts are unverified.
  - The same piece says "nightly dinner seatings and S'mores sessions by the fire pit" encourage mixing.
  - Single king Rancher's Suites. Average stay is 3–5 days (FAQ).
- **Quote:** "But if you want to be alone and unbothered, Silvies Valley Ranch is pure gold." — FORE Magazine (SCGA), Oct 2021. https://fore.scga.org/travel/of-goats-golf-the-silvies-valley-ranch-experience-is-golf-solitude-at-its-remote-finest
- **Red flags:**
  - **Season runs early May to end of October only** (FAQ).
  - Day access requires a deposit and depends on availability.
  - Prices are opaque. This is a luxury price tier.
  - Very long drive from any commercial airport.
- **Photography:** https://silvies.us/golf-at-silvies-valley-ranch/ and https://silvies.us/golf-at-silvies-valley-ranch/about-our-goats/

## 4. Bandon Preserve (and Sheep Ranch), Bandon Dunes Golf Resort (Bandon, OR)

- **Access:** RESORT-OPEN. Day guests can book.
  - Preserve and Shorty's have one "All Guests" rate.
  - On the 18-hole courses, day guests pay more than resort guests. Day guests can book April to mid-November more than 21 days ahead at a "Premium Day Guest" rate (after 10 a.m., up to one year out).
- **Location / airport:** 57744 Round Lake Rd, Bandon OR. The nearest airport is North Bend (OTH); drive time not captured from the official site (unverified). TGI's PNW notes put the resort about 4.5 hours south of Portland.
- **Architect / year:**
  - Bandon Preserve: Bill Coore and Ben Crenshaw, 2012, 13-hole par 3 (resort).
  - Sheep Ranch: Coore and Crenshaw, with one mile of ocean frontage and nine greens on the cliff edge (resort). It opened in 2020 per general coverage; not re-verified here.
- **Why Spirit (official):**
  - "All net profits from Bandon Preserve & Shorty's go directly to Bandon Dunes Charitable Foundation supporting conservation, community, and economy on the southern Oregon Coast."
  - The Preserve protects Silvery Phacelia, a threatened plant (state-listed; a federal species of concern). The resort says the plant count grew from **6,115 in 2014 to 32,570 in 2020**, and that it is the Preserve's logo.
  - The resort covers 2,525 acres. About 600 are irrigated and an estimated 1,500 are unmanaged. The H.L. McKee Preserve conservation area covers 250 acres.
  - Fescue turf needs less water.
  - The old "Wild Rivers Coast Alliance" was renamed Bandon Dunes Charitable Foundation in 2023 (BDCF annual report).
- **2026 fee (official, "2026 & 2027 Rates"):**
  - **Preserve / Shorty's (All Guests):** Jan $60, Feb $70, Mar $90, Apr $100, May $115, Jun–Sep $125, Oct $115, Nov $90, Dec $60.
  - **18-hole courses (Sheep Ranch etc.):**
    - Resort guest: $130 (Jan) to $375 (Jun–Sep).
    - Day guest: $180 to $425.
    - Premium day guest: $360 to $475.
    - Oregon resident: same as resort guest, within 21 days.
- **Walking / caddies:** "All courses are walking only."
  - Caddie: $125 per bag per round plus gratuity, paid directly to the caddie.
  - Group caddie: $40–$60 per player depending on group size.
- **Solo signals:**
  - The resort sells "Single King" rooms (Single & Double Rooms page).
  - No official single-golfer pairing program found. Anecdotal accounts (Golf Aficionado; Titleist forum) describe solo players being paired and invited into groups.
  - Lodging is taking reservations through 2027; 2028 goes to a lodging drawing.
- **Quote:** "Scampering over dunes to half-hidden greens... we feel like kids again at the Preserve." This appears on the resort's Preserve page, but the attribution is not shown there, so the speaker is unverified. Use it as resort copy, or find the original attribution.
- **Red flags:**
  - **TGI overlap:** Bandon was a footnote in the PNW 2027 piece.
  - The resort is the most famous in the set and the least "quiet secret." It is busy, with high demand for lodging.
  - Replay rates don't apply to the Preserve.

  - https://bandondunesgolf.com/golf/golf-courses/sheep-ranch-golf-course/
  - No dedicated press or media page found in the sitemap.

## 5. Northwood Golf Club (Monte Rio, CA): the redwoods pick

- **Access:** PUBLIC. GOLF.com: "Northwood Golf Club is open to the public."
- **Location / airport:** 19400 Highway 116, Monte Rio CA, on the Russian River in west Sonoma County. Nearest airports are STS (Santa Rosa) and SFO/OAK; drive times unverified.
- **Architect / year:** Dr. Alister MacKenzie, 1928 (GOLF.com and the club). A guest blurb on the club site also credits Jack Neville. 9 holes, 2,893 yards (GOLF.com).
- **Why Spirit:**
  - The club: "The magic of MacKenzie / The majesty of Redwoods."
  - GOLF.com: "set in an ethereal glade, guarded by stands of redwoods."
  - Solar-powered carts (club).
  - Bohemian Grove sits just south (GOLF.com).
  - Ranked the world's 30th-best 9-hole course in GOLF.com's 2020 list (cited by the club).
- **Fee (official course-rates page, current, undated; time-based pricing):**
  - Weekday walking: 9 holes from $33, 18 holes from $49.
  - Weekend walking: 9 from $36, 18 from $57.
  - Senior weekday walking: $29 / $42.
  - Cart $12 (9) / $22 (18). Pull cart $6.
  - A 2026 date isn't stated on the page; treat these as current.
- **Walking / caddies:** Walking rates are the default. No caddies.
- **Solo signals:**
  - Cheap, short, and easy to slot in as a 9-hole walk.
  - The club's lodging page lists nearby options (Dawn Ranch, Inn on the Russian River with golf packages, Farmhouse Inn).
  - Online tee times and a mobile app.
- **Quote:** "Northwood is a period piece and a wonderful walk amid arboreal splendor." — Noel Freeman, GOLF.com, 2021. https://golf.com/travel/northwood-golf-club-best-course-never-heard/
- **Red flags:**
  - Only 9 holes, so it may be a day stop rather than a dedicated trip. Pair it with Sea Ranch or coast and redwoods hiking.
  - GOLF.com says it "could use some restorative touches."
  - Not remote: it sits on a highway in a small river town.
  - Possible Russian River flood exposure is unverified.
  - No carts or rentals after 5 p.m.; players must be off the course by 7:30 p.m.
- **Photography:** https://www.northwoodgolf.com/golf-course/picture-gallery
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.northwoodgolf.com/

  - https://golf.com/travel/northwood-golf-club-best-course-never-heard/

## 6. Sand Valley (Nekoosa, WI): Sand Valley course and Mammoth Dunes

- **Access:** RESORT-OPEN. "Golf-Only Tee Times" are bookable online.
- **Location / airport (official Getting Here page):** 597 Leopold Way, Nekoosa WI.
  - CWA (Central Wisconsin): 1 hr 5 min.
  - MSN (Madison): 1 hr 40 min.
  - Minneapolis: 3 hr 30 min drive.
- **Architect / year:**
  - Sand Valley: Coore and Crenshaw, routing begun 2014, opened 2017 (resort).
  - Mammoth Dunes: David McLay Kidd (resort navigation lists the course; 2018 opening year not re-verified).
  - Also Sedge Valley, The Lido, the Commons (12 holes) and the Sandbox (17-hole par 3).
- **Why Spirit:**
  - The site is an ancient glacial lakebed with sand barrens that had been turned into a red pine plantation.
  - Field Museum guide (Oct 2021), "Bringing Nature Back with Conservation Golf":
    - Restoration of 250 acres of sand barrens and prairie, and 200 acres of oak savanna / jack pine with prescribed fire.
    - Habitat for Karner blue butterflies (the guide notes Karner Blues "have declined by 99%" overall).
  - Opens with Aldo Leopold's line from *A Sand County Almanac*.
  - Geoff Shackelford's 2014 headline quotes the restoration as the "biggest sand barrens restoration in Wisconsin history" (attributed to the WI DNR; not independently verified).
- **2026 fee:** The official "2026 Golf Rates" page exists, but the rate tables did not render for me, so the figure is unverified.
  - Where2Golf (secondary, updated 2025): $295 peak (Jun–Sep), 18 holes, lodging or day guest.
- **Walking / caddies:** "The golf courses at Sand Valley are walking only."
  - Caddies are independent contractors and often carry double bags; single bags are not guaranteed.
  - Pull carts $10 per day.
- **Solo signals:** Large resort with multiple lodges and communal restaurants. No official single program found. The caddie FAQ talks about "shared camaraderie" with double bags.
- **Quote:** "On this sand farm in Wisconsin…we try to rebuild…what we are losing elsewhere." — Aldo Leopold, as used in the Field Museum Sand Valley guide (PDF link below).
- **Red flags:**
  - The resort has a real-estate arm (realestate.sandvalley.com); I did not check how visible homes are from the courses.
  - Seasonal (winter programming only).
  - Busy, Bandon-style resort energy.
  - **The Lido is not a public course;** it has its own access rules (not verified here).

## 7. Forest Dunes: The Loop (Roscommon, MI)

- **Access:** PUBLIC / resort, with daily-fee rates.
  - SkyFall, a new Gil Hanse course on the property, is a **private membership club**, so exclude it.
- **Location / airport:** 6376 Forest Dunes Drive, Roscommon MI. Airport and drive time not captured: the official airport page returned "Page not found" (unverified).
- **Architect / year:**
  - The Loop: Tom Doak, reversible, opened June 2016. Red routing on even calendar days, Black on odd (club rates page).
  - Forest Dunes: Tom Weiskopf, 2000 (secondary).
- **Why Spirit:**
  - The club: "1,300 acres in the middle of state and national forested areas that are far removed from urban sprawl." Secondary sources say the land came from the Huron National Forest.
  - The reversible routing is a meditative novelty.
  - Doak's Loop is on Golf Digest's 100 Greatest Public (#69 for Red, per the club).
- **Fee (official, "2027 Greens Fees"; the 2026 season is over):**
  - Apr 29 – May 26: $160 Mon–Wed / $190 Thu–Sun.
  - May 27 – Sep 26: $200 / $230.
  - Sep 27 – Oct 10: $160 / $190.
  - Twilight or prebooked replay $140–$200. Availability replay $80–$115.
  - Bootlegger short course $60. E-cart $30.
- **Walking / caddies:** E-carts are available ($30). The Loop opened as walking-only with caddies (GolfAdvisor, 2016); whether that still holds today is unverified.
- **Solo signals:** Stay-and-play packages. On-site Lake AuSable Lodge, cottages and villas (guest policies). The Bootlegger lets groups of up to 12 play together.
- **Quote:** "You feel like you're in a maze of golf." — GOLF.com, quoted on https://forestdunesgolf.com/play-the-loop/
- **Red flags:**
  - **Home lots for sale** (from $100,000). The club says homes are "set back from the course" and "without directly interacting with it."
  - The private SkyFall build is next door.
  - Seasonal (late April to early October rates).
  - The Bootlegger "music is played throughout the course by 16 speakers": the opposite of quiet.

## 10. Gamble Sands (Brewster, WA): **TGI OVERLAP, already featured in the PNW 2027 field note**

- **Access:** RESORT-OPEN. Troon-managed. Day guests book 120 days out; overnight guests can book all 2027 dates (TGI's Oct 12 PNW notes).
- **Location / airport:** EAT (Wenatchee) 77 miles, about 90 min. SEA about 4 hours. (TGI PNW notes.)
- **Architects:**
  - Sands: David McLay Kidd.
  - Scarecrow: McLay Kidd with Nick Schaan.
  - QuickSands: 14-hole short course.
  - Cascades Putting Course.
- **Why Spirit:** High-desert bluff above the Columbia River with wide sandy corridors. Lower remoteness than Silvies.

**Northwood**
- **History: VERIFIED from the club's own history page,** a 2000 synopsis by Lewis Don Bale. https://www.northwoodgolf.com/golf-course/course-information
  - Jack Neville, "a Bohemian Club member," conceived a course on "seventy wooded acres of land, across the Russian River from the world famous Bohemian Club Grove."
  - In 1925 he took the idea to the landowner, Claude Milleresch.
  - Neville recruited Alister MacKenzie. The page says MacKenzie and Robert Hunter collaborated on Northwood in 1928.
  - The course was built in 1928 by the American Golf Construction Company.
  - The Bohemian connection is through Neville and the location. The page does not say the Bohemian Club owned or ran the course; it was privately owned.
  - From 1960 a developer subdivided the property and sold lots. In 1970 a group of property owners bought it and incorporated it as Northwood Recreation, Inc. So there is residential land around the course.
  - Caution: the same page calls Scotland MacKenzie's "native Scotland." Don't repeat that line without checking it.
- **Still 9 holes: VERIFIED.** The history page calls it "a beautifully maintained, challenging nine hole golf course." The rates page sells 18 as two loops.
- **Landscape facts:** The site is 70 wooded acres beside the Russian River. The homepage says Golf Magazine's Joe Passov placed Northwood among his top 5 MacKenzie courses in the world (club claim).
