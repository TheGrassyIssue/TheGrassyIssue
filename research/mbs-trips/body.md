# Mind / Body / Spirit golf trips: BODY research

Researched 2026-10-27. These are public courses (or resorts open to non-guests) that are physically hard: long walks, walking-only rules, elevation, length, altitude, wind.

**How to read this file**
- "VERIFIED" means I read it on the course's or resort's own site, or an official PDF, this session.
- "SECONDARY" means it comes from press or a third party.
- "UNCONFIRMED" means I could not check it, or sources disagree. Do not print these as fact.
- It is late October 2026, so several sites have already replaced their 2026 rates with 2027 rates. I note which year each figure is.
- I did not fetch reddit.com. bandondunesgolf.com blocks automated access (Cloudflare), so Bandon's figures are secondary.

## 1. Chambers Bay (University Place, WA)

- **Access:** Fully public (Pierce County-owned, run by KemperSports). VERIFIED: "Chambers Bay is a public course — no membership required."
- **Airport:** Seattle-Tacoma Intl (SEA), "just 45 minutes" (VERIFIED, course site).
- **Architect and year:** Robert Trent Jones II. The scorecard says "Established 2007" (VERIFIED). It hosted the 2015 U.S. Open.
- **Physical demands:**
  - Walking-only across "249 hilly acres". The course calls itself "one of golf's toughest walks" (VERIFIED).
  - Caddie page: "During the round of golf, you will walk over six miles." It asks for a carry bag under 25 lb (VERIFIED).
  - Elevation change is about 200 ft (CBS/Liguori column) or 207 ft measured on site (SECONDARY; the two figures differ slightly). One source puts the walk at about 7.5 miles (SECONDARY). The course's own "over six miles" is safer to print.
  - Public scorecard (10/2025): Black tees 7,158 yd, 74.4/138 (VERIFIED). Championship setups ran 7,200–7,800+ (USGA/ASGCA, SECONDARY).
  - Wind and poa annua greens are cited on the caddie page ("ever-changing coastal winds") (VERIFIED).
- **2026 green fees (VERIFIED, tax-inclusive, golf page "2026" tab):**
  - Non-resident: $149 (Jan) to $325 (Jul/Aug), $279 Sept, $229 Oct.
  - Advanced Booking: $275–$425 (Apr–Oct).
  - WA/PNGA: $119–$259.
  - 2027 is now published: non-resident $185–$400, Advanced Booking $325–$450.
- **Walking and caddies (VERIFIED):** Walking only. Push and pull carts are included free, and personal and motorized push carts are allowed.
  - Single-bag caddie: $100 per bag plus gratuity.
  - Forecaddie: $50 per player (2-player minimum) plus gratuity.
  - Suggested gratuity is $25–$50. Book caddies 48 hours or more ahead at 253-552-4869.
- **Solo signals (VERIFIED):**
  - "Single players can join existing groups but cannot book open times." So a solo player has to find a partial group on the tee sheet.
  - The Championship Experience stay-and-play (Chambers Bay, Gold Mountain, The Home Course, plus 2 nights at Hotel Murano) is priced for 2 or more. The site adds: "Traveling solo? Our team will be happy to customize a single-golfer experience."
  - Chambers Bay Grill and The Landing are on site. There is no on-site lodging.
- **Booking (VERIFIED):**
  - Rolling 90-day window.
  - Advanced Booking opens Jan 1, 2027 for Apr–Oct 2027. It is prepaid and forfeited inside 7 days.
  - Standard cancellations: full refund outside 7 days, 50% between 7 days and 48 hours, full fee inside 48 hours.
  - Pace is 4:45–5:00.
- **Quote:** "it remains one of the most demanding walks in public golf". From the Chambers Bay Advanced Booking page: https://chambersbaygolf.com/advanced-booking/
- **Red flags:** No on-site lodging. Solo players cannot book an empty tee time. No 2026–27 closures found.
- **Official photography:** https://chambersbaygolf.com/golf/#gallery
- **Sources:**
  - https://chambersbaygolf.com/golf/
  - https://chambersbaygolf.com/advanced-booking/
  - Scorecard: https://chambersbaygolf.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Chambers-Bay-Scorecard-10-2.pdf
  - https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/newyork/news/us-open-chambers-bay

## 2. Bethpage State Park, Black Course (Farmingdale, NY)

This could also be a "Mind" pick, because of the warning sign and the booking hustle.

- **Access:** Fully public (NY State Parks). VERIFIED.
- **Airport:** Long Island / NYC area. I did not confirm a specific airport or distance.
- **Architect and year:** A.W. Tillinghast (VERIFIED, NY Parks). Golf.com says 1935 and other sources say 1936 (UNCONFIRMED). Restored by Rees Jones (Golf.com, SECONDARY).
- **Physical demands:**
  - Walking-only on Black. NY Parks says carts are allowed on Red, Blue, Green and Yellow, which leaves Black out (VERIFIED by omission). The Inquirer describes "a walking-only policy on this very hilly course" (SECONDARY).
  - ESPN quotes former head pro Charlie Bolling saying the challenge may be "the length and scope of the walk" (SECONDARY).
  - Back-tee yardage is 7,465 (GolfAdvisor), 7,468 (kimigolf) or 7,486 (Golf.com). Par 71. UNCONFIRMED: no official scorecard found.
  - The warning sign is behind the 1st tee (Golf.com, SECONDARY).
- **2026 green fees (VERIFIED, NY Parks price list dated 4/22/2026):**
  - Non-NY residents: $140 weekday, $160 weekend/holiday. Twilight $88 / $100.
  - NY residents: $70 / $80.
  - Plus a $5 per player reservation fee. No-show fee $15. Cancellation inside 48 hours $15.
  - Hand pull cart $7.
- **Walking and caddies (VERIFIED):** Only caddies arranged through the Bethpage pro shop are allowed. Caddies are independent contractors, and fees are "recommended by the Pro Shop". I found no published caddie rate.
- **Solo signals:**
  - One reservation holds up to 4 players, so a 1-player booking appears possible (VERIFIED wording; I did not test whether singles get paired).
  - The Heritage Club public house and bar is on site and open daily (VERIFIED).
  - No lodging on site.
- **Booking (VERIFIED, NY Parks Reservation FAQ, June 2026):**
  - Non-NY and unverified residents book from 5 days out at 7:00 PM. Verified NY residents book 7 days out.
  - Black is limited to one reservation per 28 days.
  - The reservation holder must check in, pay and play. Bookings are non-transferable.
  - Black is open Apr 15–Nov 15 (weather permitting) and is closed Mondays except holidays.
- **Quote:** The sign: "The Black Course Is An Extremely Difficult Course Which We Recommend Only For Highly Skilled Golfers". Source: https://golf.com/news/features/bethpage-black-sign-mysterious-history-iconic-warning-sign/
- **Red flags:**
  - Short booking window for travelers, so a trip there is a gamble.
  - Hosts the 2028 KPMG Women's PGA and the 2033 PGA Championship. Expect closures around those dates (exact dates UNCONFIRMED).
- **Official photography:** https://parks.ny.gov/visit/golf-courses/bethpage-state-park-golf-courses (NYSDED-credited images). NY Parks Flickr: https://www.flickr.com/photos/nysparks/albums/

## 3. Erin Hills (Erin, WI)

- **Access:** Public, with on-site lodging. Dining is "only those who have a tee time or lodging reservation" (VERIFIED, FAQ).
- **Airport:**
  - Milwaukee Mitchell (MKE) is named on the FAQ for rental cars. I did not confirm the drive time.
  - O'Hare is 112 miles, about 2 hours (VERIFIED).
- **Architect and year:** Usually credited to Michael Hurdzan, Dana Fry and Ron Whitten, opening in 2006. I did not re-check this on the course site (UNCONFIRMED). It hosted the 2017 U.S. Open and the 2025 U.S. Women's Open (VERIFIED, site nav).
- **Physical demands:**
  - Walking-only. "No push or pull carts are allowed", so you carry your own bag or hire a caddie (VERIFIED). That is stricter than most courses on this list.
  - Black tees 7,715 yd, par 72 (2024 press kit, SECONDARY/official PDF).
  - Walk distance and elevation are not published.
- **Green fees:**
  - The 2026 rate table is no longer on the site.
  - 2025 (official page, via search snippet): $455 peak (Jun 7–Sep 28) and $375 fall.
  - 2027 is now published (VERIFIED): $435 early/late season, $555 peak (May 28–Sep 26). Stay-and-play second round $220 in the shoulder seasons.
  - 2026 figure: UNCONFIRMED.
- **Caddies (VERIFIED, 2027 table):**
  - Caddie service fee $65 per player, plus a suggested gratuity of $75 or more.
  - Forecaddie $65 per group (3-player minimum), plus $35 per player.
  - The course has about 150 caddies. ADA carts (2 on property) must be requested 2 weeks ahead.
- **Solo signals (VERIFIED):** The Lodge has "7 single rooms" out of 11. 2027 single room rates are $395–$445. The cottages are within a pitch of the 1st tee. Dining is limited to guests, which helps if you want a bar where everyone has just played the course.
- **Booking:** Reservations at erinhills.com/reservations. Season runs early May to mid-October (VERIFIED from rate dates).
- **Quote:** "Erin Hills is a traditional walking-only course." Source: https://erinhills.com/golf/caddies/
- **Red flags:** The most expensive fee among the US picks here, plus caddie costs. Short season.
- **Official photography:**

## 4. Whistling Straits, Straits Course (Haven/Sheboygan, WI; Destination Kohler)

- **Access:** Resort-owned and open to non-guests. You book through the Kohler golf portal (VERIFIED: tee-time booking is public).
- **Airport (VERIFIED, Kohler):** MKE 72 miles. GRB 58 miles. ORD 142 miles.
- **Architect and year:** Pete Dye (VERIFIED). Opened in 1998 (UNCONFIRMED this session). It hosted the 2004, 2010 and 2015 PGA Championships and the 2021 Ryder Cup (VERIFIED, scorecard).
- **Physical demands:**
  - Walking only, no carts (VERIFIED). The course runs "two miles of rugged Lake Michigan coastline" (VERIFIED).
  - Black tees 7,790 yd, 77.2/152 (VERIFIED, official scorecard).
  - Bluffs and wind are the themes. Elevation and walk distance are not published.
- **2026 green fees:** Not published on the static Kohler pages. Pricing lives in the booking engine. Third-party figures range from $450 to $795 and look unreliable. UNCONFIRMED.
- **Caddies (VERIFIED, 2026 policies page):**
  - Caddies are "highly recommended but are not required".
  - Caddie fee $90 per person, paid in the shop. Suggested gratuity $70 per bag.
  - It is a double-bag facility. Bring a single-strap carry bag under 24 lb.
  - Twilight caddies on the Straits must be reserved in advance.
  - Twilight rates are 30% off rack. Super twilight is 45% off. Same-day replay is 45% off.
- **Solo signals:** On-site lodging at The American Club and other Kohler properties, in the village about 20 minutes away. I found no published single or pairing policy (UNCONFIRMED).
- **Quote:** "Naah, this course is popcorn! But, sometimes people choke on popcorn." Pete Dye, quoted on https://www.destinationkohler.com/golf/whistling-straits/the-straits
- **Red flags:** The fee is opaque until you are in the booking engine. Peak-season rates are very likely the highest on this list (UNCONFIRMED).
- **Official photography:** https://www.destinationkohler.com/press-releases (press). Course page: https://www.destinationkohler.com/golf/whistling-straits/the-straits

## 5. Bandon Dunes Golf Resort: Bandon Trails (plus Pacific Dunes) (Bandon, OR)

- **Access:** Resort open to non-guests. Non-guests pay a premium, and recent policy limits their time slots (SECONDARY).
- **Airport:** Southwest Oregon Regional (OTH), North Bend, about 25 miles / 35 minutes. This comes from the resort's own page via a search snippet; I could not fetch the page directly because the site blocks bots.
- **Architects and years:**
  - Bandon Trails: Coore & Crenshaw, opened 2005 (SECONDARY).
  - Pacific Dunes: Tom Doak, opened 2001 (UNCONFIRMED this session).
- **Physical demands:**
  - The resort is walking-only (resort FAQ, SECONDARY via search).
  - Reviewers repeatedly call Trails the hilliest or hardest walk at Bandon, with Old Macdonald a close second (SECONDARY: hookedongolfblog, golfpass).
  - Trails Black tees 6,788 yd, par 71 (scorecard listing, SECONDARY).
  - No published walk distance or elevation figure.
- **2026 green fees:**
  - UNCONFIRMED. The official rate page cannot be reached.
  - A third party (greenfeetracker) shows a 2026 resort-guest schedule of $130 (Jan) to $375 (Jun–Sep). Low confidence.
  - A Premier Golf blog says day guests pay about $50 more in peak season, and "Premium Day Guest" rates go up to about $475. Low confidence.
  - The OGA ran a member-only promo of $175 for Apr 20–30, 2026 (SECONDARY).
- **Caddies:** $100 per bag per round plus gratuity, paid to the caddie (resort FAQ via search, SECONDARY; matches a 2024 NCGA trip document).
- **Solo signals:** On-site lodging and several communal bars and dining rooms (well documented, but not fetched this session). I found no published singles policy (UNCONFIRMED).
- **Booking:**
  - GOLF.com reports a 2026 lottery in 3 periods (Jan–Apr, May–Sep, Oct–Dec) for lodging requests.
  - Non-guests: about a 21-day window, or earlier at a premium. Premier Golf says day-guest times are after 10 a.m. (SECONDARY).
- **Quote:** "it's the second best course there behind Pacific." A GOLF.com Insider on Trails: https://golf.com/news/bandon-dunes-the-insiders-guide/
- **Red flags:** The lottery and premium rules for non-guests make it hard to plan. I could not confirm any fee from the official site. Re-check before publishing.
- **Official photography:** https://www.bandondunesgolf.com/ (I could not reach a press or media page; ask the resort's PR contact).

## 6. Cabot Cape Breton: Cabot Cliffs (Inverness, Nova Scotia, Canada)

- **Access:** Resort open to day guests (VERIFIED).
- **Airports (VERIFIED, Cabot FAQ):** Halifax (YHZ), about 3.5 hours by car. Sydney (YQY), about 2 hours. Port Hawkesbury (private), about 1 hour.
- **Architect and year:** Coore & Crenshaw. Sources give 2015 or 2016 (UNCONFIRMED).
- **Physical demands:**
  - "Cabot courses are designed for walking… We do not offer golf carts" (VERIFIED). Medical carts require a doctor's note.
  - Black tees 6,764 yd, 74.3/144 (scorecard listing, SECONDARY).
  - The 16th is a cliff-to-cliff par 3 with a drop of about 100 ft to the ocean (On The Tee, SECONDARY).
  - Weather: the FAQ describes spring and fall as "misty rain and wind off the ocean" (VERIFIED).
- **2026 green fees (VERIFIED, Cabot rates page; CAD, plus 14% HST):**
  - Day guest, peak (Jun 26–Sep 13): CAD $565 (about USD $413).
  - Day guest, early/late season: CAD $275–$335.
  - Resort guest, peak: CAD $450. Resort guest, early/late: CAD $220–$270.
  - Same-day replay is half price.
- **Caddies (VERIFIED, CAD):**
  - "A" caddie $90 per bag plus gratuity.
  - "B" caddie (carries only) $60.
  - Forecaddie $120 per group.
  - Caddies are optional and must be requested 48 hours or more ahead.
- **Solo signals (VERIFIED):**
  - Large on-site inventory: 72 lodge rooms plus villas and homes.
  - Cabot Bar is walk-in only, and Whit's Public House is in town. Both suit someone eating alone.
  - A free shuttle runs between the two courses.
- **Booking (VERIFIED):**
  - Day guests can book 14 days ahead. Their tee times are prepaid and non-refundable.
  - Resort guests book with lodging, 12–18 months ahead.
  - The 2026 season ran May 8–Oct 25 and is now over. 2027 dates were not posted.
- **Quote:** "We do not offer golf carts". Cabot Cape Breton FAQ: https://cabot.com/capebreton/frequently-asked-questions/
- **Red flags:** Canada, so travelers need a passport. Remote, with a long drive from YHZ. Day-guest window is only 14 days.
- **Official photography:** https://cabot.com/capebreton/golf/ (resort golf pages; no separate press page found)
- **Sources:**
  - https://cabot.com/capebreton/golf-rates/
  - https://cabot.com/capebreton/frequently-asked-questions/
  - https://golf.com/courses/cabot-cliffs-golf-top-100-world/

## 7. Kiawah Island Golf Resort: The Ocean Course (Kiawah Island, SC)

This is a wind pick, not a terrain pick.

- **Access (VERIFIED):** "KIGR is open to the public and tee times can be made without renting property at the resort."
- **Airport (VERIFIED):** Charleston Intl (CHS), about 1 hour, plus 20 minutes to the Ocean Course.
- **Architects:** Pete and Alice Dye (VERIFIED). The course hosted the 1991 Ryder Cup and the 2012 and 2021 PGA Championships (VERIFIED).
- **Physical demands:**
  - Wind (VERIFIED, resort): "a player can experience up to an 8-club difference on holes". Alice Dye raised the course so the holes are exposed to the Atlantic, and Dye designed for both easterly and westerly winds.
  - 2025 scorecard: Black tees 7,385 yd (VERIFIED, official PDF).
  - The resort FAQ calls the Ocean Course "the easiest walking course" at Kiawah. The physical test here is wind, not hills.
- **2026 green fees:** Not published on static pages; pricing is in the booking engine. A third party (where2golf, 2024) says non-guest high season "from $650". UNCONFIRMED.
- **Walking and caddies (VERIFIED):**
  - Walking-only, except Jun–Aug after 10 a.m., when carts are allowed but a forecaddie is required.
  - There is no caddie fee. Recommended gratuity is $120 per player for a walking caddie and $60 for a forecaddie.
  - The caddies are resort employees.
- **Solo signals (VERIFIED):**
  - "KIGR does pair players up". The resort pairs singles, which works in a solo player's favor.
  - On-site lodging: The Sanctuary and Ocean Course Cottages.
  - The clubhouse has the Ryder Cup Bar.
- **Booking (VERIFIED):** Non-guests book no sooner than 60 days out. Resort guests can book any time for their stay. Cancel 48 hours or more ahead. Peak periods are mid-March to mid-May and mid-September to Thanksgiving.
- **Quote:** "a player can experience up to an 8-club difference on holes depending upon the wind's direction and strength". Source: https://kiawahresort.com/golf/the-ocean-course/
- **Red flags:** Fee unconfirmed and likely the highest of the US picks. Weak fit for the walking theme.

## 9. The Prairie Club: Dunes Course (near Valentine, NE)

This is the length and wind pick.

- **Access (VERIFIED):** "semi-private destination". 2026 day play is open to the public.
  - Links Magazine reports that Dunes and Pines alternate daily between public and member play (SECONDARY).
- **Airport:** No commercial service nearby. Miller Field (VTN) is general aviation only.
  - The nearest commercial airports are about 170+ miles away (Rapid City or North Platte), or Rapid City at about 220 miles (NPS figures). Sources conflict (SECONDARY).
- **Architects and year:** Tom Lehman and Chris Brands (VERIFIED). Opened 2010 (scorecard listing, SECONDARY).
- **Physical demands:**
  - Dunes Course: 5,845–8,073 yd, par 73 (VERIFIED). The longest course on this list.
  - The site describes "dramatic elevation changes" and a "wind-shaped landscape" (VERIFIED).
  - GolfPass says winds average 20 mph and quotes a caddie putting the walk at 8.4 miles (SECONDARY). The course was originally walking-only, but members pushed for carts (SECONDARY).
- **2026 green fees (VERIFIED):** Public day play $389 any day, cart included, replays free. Hosted guest $219. Sponsored guest $259.
  - Stay-and-play "Drifters" packages from $559 per player. Other packages from $754 per person. Both include unlimited golf, a cart, lodging and breakfast.
- **Walking:** A GPS cart is included in the rate. Whether walking is allowed is UNCONFIRMED on the official site.
- **Solo signals:**
  - Lodge rooms, bunkhouse, guesthouse and cabins (VERIFIED).
  - The site describes the Lodge as having "three dining areas" and a fireplace (VERIFIED). There is nothing else around for miles.
- **Quote:** "It's an all-golf, all-the-time retreat in the middle of nowhere." GOLF ADVISOR (2016), as quoted on https://theprairieclub.com/golf/
- **Red flags:**
  - The course is cart-included, which weakens the walking story. Confirm that walking is allowed.
  - Semi-private alternating days.
  - Very remote.
- **Official photography:** https://theprairieclub.com/golf/dunes-course/ (no separate press page found)
- **Sources:**
  - https://theprairieclub.com/
  - https://theprairieclub.com/golf/
  - https://theprairieclub.com/golf/dunes-course/
  - https://theprairieclub.com/packages/
  - https://linksmagazine.com/7-golf-getaways-that-mix-public-and-private-courses
