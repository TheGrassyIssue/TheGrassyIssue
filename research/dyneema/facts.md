## 1. Who makes it now

- Avient Corporation (Avon Lake, Ohio) owns Dyneema. Royal DSM sold its Protective Materials business ("chiefly consisting of Dyneema") to Avient. Agreement announced 20 April 2022; completed 1 September 2022. Enterprise value EUR 1.45 billion; DSM received about EUR 1.35 billion net in cash and anticipated a book profit of around EUR 1 billion.
  - VERBATIM (DSM press release, Heerlen, 1 Sept 2022): "Royal DSM, a global purpose-led science-based company, today announces the completion of the sale of its Protective Materials business to Avient Corporation for an Enterprise Value of €1.45 billion."
  - VERBATIM: "The transaction covers all of DSM's Protective Materials activities, chiefly consisting of Dyneema®. DSM anticipates a book profit of around €1 billion. DSM will receive approximately €1.35 billion net in cash."
  - Source: https://our-company.dsm-firmenich.com/en/our-company/news/press-releases/legacy-archive/2022/dsm-completes-sale-of-protective-materials-business.html
  - US press reported the deal as roughly $1.5 billion: https://www.plasticstoday.com/materials/avient-buys-dsm-protective-materials-in-1-5-billion-deal
  - Avient's own release: https://www.avient.com/investor-center/news/avient-completes-acquisition-dsm-protective-materials-dyneema
  - At the time the business had approx. $415M sales / $130M EBITDA (2022 est.), six production facilities, four R&D centres, ~1,000 employees (Avient acquisition announcement, April 2022): https://www.avient.com/investor-center/news/avient-announces-agreement-acquire-dsm-protective-materials-dyneema-and-plans-explore-sale-distribution
- dyneema.com About page timeline (VERBATIM entries): "1960s — An experiment run by Royal DSM creates a material of extraordinary strength." / "1970s — DSM patents a gel spinning process for making the world's strongest fiber." / "1980s — DSM commercializes this fiber under the brand name Dyneema®." / "1990s — Dyneema® is developed for applications including mooring lines, aquaculture nets, cut-resistant gloves, and protective body armor." / "2022 — Avient acquires Dyneema® for its sustainable materials portfolio."

## 2. What it is

- Dyneema is a brand of ultra-high-molecular-weight polyethylene (UHMWPE) fibre, made by gel-spinning.
  - VERBATIM (dyneema.com home): "Our ultra-high-molecular-weight polyethylene (UHMWPE) Dyneema® is founded on groundbreaking engineering."
  - VERBATIM (dyneema.com home, og:description and hero): "A fiber that's 15 times stronger than steel, yet light enough to float on water. Think it sounds too good to be true? Think again."
  - VERBATIM (dyneema.com home): "Dyneema® is the world's strongest fiber™"
  - VERBATIM (dyneema.com home): "In 1969, we invented the world's strongest fiber™."
  - VERBATIM (dyneema.com home): "And it does all this with a 90%-lower carbon footprint than generic HMPE fiber."
  - Source: https://www.dyneema.com/
- The 15x claim is strength-to-weight (specific strength), not absolute strength. Heddels (4 Mar 2026): "When that claim is made, it almost always refers to tensile strength-to-weight ratio: a specific strength, not absolute strength." Source: https://www.heddels.com/2026/03/what-is-dyneema-all-about-the-worlds-strongest-fiber/
- DSM's 2015 boilerplate (VERBATIM): "It is up to 15 times stronger than quality steel and up to 40% stronger than aramid fibers, both on weight for weight basis. Dyneema® fiber floats on water and is extremely durable and resistant to moisture, UV light and chemicals." Source: https://www.pressreleasefinder.com/Dyneema/DYNPR300/en/
- Note the date discrepancy: dyneema.com says "In 1969, we invented" while most histories (below) put Pennings's discovery at 1963 and the fibre process at 1968. Sentinel's product copy repeats the 1969 line. Use "late 1960s" or cite both.

## 3. Discovery and first production

- Discovery: Dr. Albert Pennings, a DSM (Dutch State Mines) chemist, 1963, noticed strong fibrous crystals forming around a stirrer in a polyethylene solution; by 1968 he had a process to make a usable fibre. The commercial launch came in 1990.
  - Heddels, 4 Mar 2026 (VERBATIM): "In 1963, Dr. Albert Pennings, a chemist at DSM (Dutch State Mines) in the Netherlands, was conducting research on polyethylene solutions. While stirring a solution to analyze its properties, he noticed unusual, incredibly strong, white fibrous crystals forming around the stirrer."
  - VERBATIM: "By 1968, he had developed a process to turn these crystals into a usable fiber."
  - VERBATIM: "...pushing past plenty of corporate shoulder-shrugging before Dyneema finally launched in 1990."
  - Source: https://www.heddels.com/2026/03/what-is-dyneema-all-about-the-worlds-strongest-fiber/
- Gel-spinning patent 1979; commercial production since 1990 at Heerlen, Netherlands (ScienceDirect topic page, via search snippet: "In 1979 DSM invented and patented the fiber and the gel-spinning process to produce it. Dyneema® fibers have been in commercial production since 1990 at a plant in Heerlen, The Netherlands."). Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/engineering/dyneema (page fetch was rate-limited; wording is from the search result snippet — re-verify before quoting).
- Heerlen is still a Dyneema plant today: DSM 2015 boilerplate (VERBATIM): "UHMWPE fiber from DSM Dyneema is produced in Heerlen (The Netherlands) and in Greenville, North Carolina (U.S.A.)." Source: https://www.pressreleasefinder.com/Dyneema/DYNPR300/en/
- Other supporting sources on the Pennings story: Carryology "Pro Guide to Dyneema" https://www.carryology.com/liking/industry/the-pro-guide-to-dyneema-the-modern-day-miracle-material/ ; "Romancing the Thread: the Story of Dyneema" (2015 PDF) https://sakuragiconsult.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/Dyneema_-Romancing-the-Thread_2015.pdf

## 4. Dyneema Composite Fabric (DCF), formerly Cuben Fiber

- DCF is a non-woven laminate: Dyneema fibres laid in a grid and sandwiched between thin polyester films (Heddels VERBATIM: "Instead of being spun or woven, DCF is made by sandwiching woven Dyneema fibers between thin sheets of film." — note Heddels says "woven", most technical sources say non-woven/laid fibres). Source: https://www.heddels.com/2026/03/what-is-dyneema-all-about-the-worlds-strongest-fiber/
- Cuben Fiber was the product of Cubic Tech Corporation, Mesa, Arizona. DSM Dyneema finalized the acquisition of Cubic Tech on 13 May 2015; terms undisclosed. The material was then renamed Dyneema Composite Fabric.
  - VERBATIM (DSM press release, 13 May 2015): "DSM Dyneema, the inventor and manufacturer of Ultra High Molecular Weight Polyethylene (UHMwPE) fiber, branded as Dyneema®, today finalized the acquisition of Cubic Tech Corporation. This privately owned company, based in Mesa (Arizona, USA), has specialized for more than 23 years in the custom design, development and production of innovative high performance ultra-lightweight flexible laminates and fabrics — most of them based on Dyneema®."
  - VERBATIM: "Cubic Tech's technology has been developed and refined over two decades of research and development, originating with the development of racing sails used in the America's Cup in 1992."
  - VERBATIM: "Terms of the acquisition will not be disclosed."
  - Source: https://www.pressreleasefinder.com/Dyneema/DYNPR300/en/
  - Trade coverage: https://specialtyfabricsreview.com/2015/06/25/dsm-dyneema-acquires-cubic-tech/ ; https://www.plasticsnews.com/article/20150515/NEWS/150519943/dsm-dyneema-buys-customer-cubic-tech/ ; Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyneema_Composite_Fabric
- Hawbuck's own description of the hybrid variant (VERBATIM, product page): "Made with Dyneema® Composite Fabric Hybrid-- a laminate comprised of UHMWPE fibers, films and a woven polyester face cloth for texture and abrasion resistance." And: "Material made in Arizona." Source: https://hawbuck.com/products/lean%E2%84%A2-wallet-h01-black-dyneema-composite-fabric-hybrid

## 5. Bio-based Dyneema

- Announced 12 March 2020 (Limburg, NL): DSM partnered with SABIC and UPM Biofuels. Feedstock = UPM BioVerno, made from residue of the wood-pulping process, converted by SABIC into renewable ethylene (TRUCIRCLE). Mass-balance approach, ISCC Plus certified. Available from April 2020. DSM target: at least 60% bio-based feedstock by 2030.
  - VERBATIM: "UPM Biofuels produces bio-based feedstock UPM BioVerno from the residue of the pulping process. This is then processed by SABIC to make renewable ethylene under their TRUCIRCLE umbrella of solutions."
  - VERBATIM: "Bio-based Dyneema will be available from April 2020."
  - VERBATIM (Wilfrid Gambade, president DSM Protective Materials): "By partnering with SABIC and UPM Biofuels, we are taking the next important step in our sustainability journey, and driving our industry's transition from conventional to renewable resources."
  - Source: https://www.textileworld.com/textile-world/fiber-world/2020/03/dsm-announces-partnership-with-sabic-and-upm-biofuels-to-create-bio-based-dyneema/ (reprint of the DSM release)
- FibrXL: first bio-based HMPE fibre available to customers from 1 Oct 2020 (SK99, SK78 grades). Source: https://fibrxl.com/2020/09/14/the-first-ever-bio-based-hmpe-fiber-available-via-fibrxl/
- dyneema.com sustainability pages: https://www.dyneema.com/sustainability and https://www.dyneema.com/sustainability/sustainable-raw-materials
- Caveat (Heddels): bio-based Dyneema is chemically identical to fossil UHMWPE, so it does not biodegrade. Source: https://www.heddels.com/2026/03/what-is-dyneema-all-about-the-worlds-strongest-fiber/

## 6. Notable uses

- dyneema.com sector list: Aquaculture, Maritime (mooring lines, towage/salvage), Offshore Energy, Law Enforcement and Military (armored vehicles, ballistic helmets, vest inserts, soft armor), Manufacturing and Construction (crane ropes, cut-resistant gloves, synthetic chains), Sports and Consumer (kitesurfing/paragliding lines, performance apparel and footwear, sailing lines, tents/packs/bags). Source: https://www.dyneema.com/
- America's Cup sails: Cubic Tech's laminate technology "originating with the development of racing sails used in the America's Cup in 1992" (VERBATIM, DSM 2015). Source: https://www.pressreleasefinder.com/Dyneema/DYNPR300/en/
- Body armour, tow cables, mooring lines, satellite tethers, the Costa Concordia righting, hoisting the spire onto One World Trade Center (Heddels, paraphrase of: "tethering satellites in outer space, helping flip a massive stranded cruise ship back upright off the coast of Italy, and even hoisting the crown onto the Freedom Tower"). Source: https://www.heddels.com/2026/03/what-is-dyneema-all-about-the-worlds-strongest-fiber/
- 2025: Dyneema HB330 / HB332 hard-ballistic materials, claimed 45% weight reduction in armor systems. Source: https://www.avient.com/news/dyneema-unveils-groundbreaking-hard-ballistic-product-innovations-enabling-45-weight-reduction-protective-armor-systems
