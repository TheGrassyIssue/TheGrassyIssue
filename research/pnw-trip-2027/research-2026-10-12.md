# PNW 2027 golf trip — research notes (checked Oct 12, 2026)

Official sources unless noted. Drive times are estimates unless sourced.

## Fixed courses
- **Wildwood GC**, 21881 NW St. Helens Rd, Portland OR. Public 18, 1990 (OGA). Par 72, ~5,853 yds (GolfNow). OGA (verbatim): "Mount Hood, Saint Helens and Adams dominate the skyline, while the Columbia River and Downtown Portland paint the backdrop as golfers navigate the terrain." Rate unverified ($20–$35 on aggregators); call (503) 621-3402. DO NOT LINK golfingwildwood.com (domain now redirects to a gambling site). Link: https://exploreoregongolf.oga.org/wildwood-golf-course
- **Chambers Bay**, University Place WA. RTJ II, walking only, 2015 U.S. Open, Pierce County-owned. 2026 non-resident from: Apr $220, May $249, Jun $299, Jul/Aug $325, Sep $279, Oct $229 (+15.2% tax). Advanced Booking (tax incl.) Apr $300 … Sep $375. Advanced reservations for Apr–Oct 2026 opened Jan 1, 2026 (2027 pattern unconfirmed). Caddie $100/bag + tip; forecaddie $50/player (2 min). Free push carts. Pace 4:45–5:00. 45 min from Sea-Tac. https://chambersbaygolf.com/golf/ . "Championship Experience" package: Chambers Bay + Gold Mountain Olympic + The Home Course + 2 nights Hotel Murano, $425–$849/golfer.
- **Gamble Sands**, Brewster WA (Troon). Sands (McLay Kidd), Scarecrow (McLay Kidd w/ Nick Schaan), QuickSands 14-hole short course, Cascades Putting Course (100,000 sq ft, glow balls after dark). 2027: Sands/Scarecrow $235 Apr / $315 May–Sep / $235 Oct; second 18 $170/$210/$170; QuickSands $80. Inn 2027: $350 Apr / $475 May–Oct; King Suite $450/$595. Overnight guests can book all 2027 dates; day guests 120 days out. Danny Boy (dinner), The Barn (all day). SEA 221 mi ~4 h; Wenatchee (EAT) 77 mi ~90 min. https://www.gamblesands.com/rates
- **The Home Course**, DuPont WA. 2007; "cooperatively owned and operated by the Pacific Northwest Golf Association and Washington Golf". Oct 2026: Mon–Thu $64 / Fri–Sun $70 (association $52/$57); cart $18/seat; pull cart $5. https://www.thehomecourse.com
