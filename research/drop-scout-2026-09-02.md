# Drop Scout — TGI brand universe, 2026-09-02

**Method.** brands.json carries no merchant domains, so the universe was harvested
empirically: every `product-link` href across all 176 drop pages → 198 distinct
merchant domains. The 58 with ≥8 links on the site were swept via Shopify
`products.json?limit=250`; 50 answered.

**The trap, and the filter.** `published_at` is NOT a release date — a store can
re-publish old stock and it looks like a drop. Everything below is checked against
`created_at` as well. Mogshade is exactly why: 22 headcovers "published" 24 Aug,
but 11 of them were created in **February 2024**.

Coverage: 50 stores read · 42 with new SKUs since 3 Aug · 8 quiet.
Not readable (no products.json): Manors, Metalwood, Cloud & Wind, Kingfisher,
Sentinel, MacKenzie, Gumtree, GLM. Those need a rendered check.
