## Materials and making (from product pages and the Sheep to Shirt page)
- Merino from RWS and Nativa/Shaniko Regenerative certified ranches in Idaho, Colorado, California and Nevada.
- Long-sleeve merino shirts: some knit in a "centuries old textile mill" in Massachusetts (6.5oz mid-weight, 3.5oz light-weight), others knit in South Carolina (4.5oz); handmade and embroidered in NYC; natural horn buttons; standard fit.
- Woven Merino Swing Jacket: woven in Connecticut, undyed natural merino with natural flecks, RiRi zipper.
- Woven merino hats: fabric from American Woolens. Oxford Cotton Hat: Japanese oxford, made in California, embroidered in NYC.
- Golf short: nylon/spandex mesh made in China, cut and sewn in NYC (the one non-natural-fiber, non-US-fabric piece; don't say "natural fibers only").
- Merino socks: knit and processed in North Carolina.
- Big Bag: cut and sewn in NYC from leftover Pennsylvania-made polyester mesh from 2024 mesh shorts.
- Merino Fleece Sheep Driver Headcover: ears and lining from shirt-production scrap.
- La Cupola Paperweight: hand-poured concrete and gypsum model of Whim's Milan Design Week 2026 installation, edition of 10.
- WHIMOWA: a one-of-one vintage Rimowa trunk with a putting green inside, $8,800.

## Free Golf
- "We design and build open-access putting greens in the most densely populated urban areas of the US." 9 putting-green pop-ups, 600+ combined days across Chicago, LA and NYC over six years (Free Golf page).
- Over 15,000 people welcomed to the game at the installations (CNN, Aug 2024).
- "Golf is broccoli?" framing: most people don't think they'll like golf.
- Brand partners on Free Golf greens include Reebok, Oakley, Jarritos, Topgolf, Callaway, Bettinardi and more (Free Golf page).
- 2024 Reebok sneaker collab (CNN: launched July 2024 at $130; current WHIM Reebok Classic on site is $100).

## Verbatim quotes (under 25 words each)
1. "People don't try golf the same way they don't try broccoli, because they don't think that they're going to like it." Colin Heaberg, CNN, Aug 2024
2. "If you make it so someone can walk off the street, grab a putter and try, you're going to get more golfers." Heaberg, CNN
3. "That's the only metric there is – how many people are touching clubs?" Heaberg, CNN
4. "We're making pretty good, clean stuff – just a little different." Heaberg, CNN
5. "We wanted the brand to be about something and not just a cool clothing company." Will Gisel, StockX, Apr 2022
6. "We want to wear clothes that we feel will look good on us 10 years from now." Heaberg, StockX
7. "Personally, I just don't like clothes with a lot of logos on them. I like things that start a conversation." Gisel, StockX
8. "No range-finders, bluetooth speakers, or Michelob Ultras today, just friends playing the game with unpretentious old school style." Whim About page
