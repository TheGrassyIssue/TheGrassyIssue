**Founder backgrounds**
- **Dan Fibiger:** "nearly 20 years working in the human rights and environmental lane of the apparel industry, most recently leading sustainability and philanthropy at one of the world's largest apparel companies" (https://sloat.cc/blogs/journal/intro-letter-an-ode-to-the-outerlands). The IG names the company: "VP of Global Sustainability at Gap Inc. and President of Gap Foundation." He has lived in SF for almost 20 years, surfs Ocean Beach and plays the city's public courses (https://www.instagram.com/p/DecFODFHNsa/). SF Golf also cites Gap's 2023 ESG report for both roles (https://www.sanfranciscogolf.com/stories/sloat-san-francisco-golf-brand-launch).
- **Teague Moriarty:** "spent much of his career as a chef and is now an owner of Sons & Daughters and Kiln, both two Michelin-starred restaurants in San Francisco" (Ode, above). Born and raised in Santa Cruz. Moved to SF more than 20 years ago to open Sons & Daughters (https://www.instagram.com/p/Dcr9wiMnHYR/). SF Golf says he co-founded Sons & Daughters in 2010 (citing SFGATE) and later backed alums John Wesley and Julianna Yang in opening Kiln (citing Eater SF).
- Both founders say golf and surfing "have shaped our lives in different ways" (Ode).

**Discrepancy to flag:** web-search summaries pointed to LinkedIn posts that seemed to credit a "Joe Van Hofwegen (ex-Gap)" as co-founder. LinkedIn was login-walled and I couldn't read the posts. SLOAT's own About page, Journal and IG all name **Daniel Fibiger** as co-founder, so use Fibiger. The LinkedIn item is most likely a colleague's post about the launch **[UNCONFIRMED]**.

## 2. Materials and factories

**Materials** (https://sloat.cc/pages/materials-factories)
- **GOTS-certified organic cotton.** Used in the jersey polo and tee (170gsm), fleece hoodie, crew and quarter-zip (480gsm), Day Pant and Golf Short (195gsm), Caddie Pant and Short (305gsm), corduroy short, and both hats (product pages via https://sloat.cc/products.json ; FAQ https://sloat.cc/pages/faq).
- **RWS-certified merino.** Used in the Merino Polo (200gsm jersey, $215).
- **GCS (Good Cashmere Standard) cashmere.** Listed as a core material on the About and Materials pages, but **no cashmere product is in the current 16-piece catalogue**. It may be coming later **[UNCONFIRMED]**.
- **Organic Ventile cotton.** Used in the Wind Shirt ($295), Vest ($345) and Jacket ($595). Product pages say "Ventile fabric woven in Switzerland" and "Made in Portugal". IG captions say "Spun in Germany and made in Portugal" (https://www.instagram.com/p/DdcUAUbHeg_/). Read together: yarn spun in Germany, fabric woven in Switzerland, garment sewn in Portugal (my reconciliation of two brand sources). All Ventile pieces carry a **lifetime guarantee** ("If something goes wrong, we'll make it right and repair it when possible"), per product pages.

**Certifications:** GOTS (cotton), RWS (wool), GCS (cashmere), as stated on https://sloat.cc/pages/about and /pages/materials-factories. I found no B Corp or other company-level certification.

**Japan:** the IG bio and site title say "made in Portugal, Japan, and the USA". The founders' letter is more precise: the first collection is "made primarily in Portugal, with additional work being developed in Japan and the United States" (Ode). **No Japanese factory is named, and no current product is listed as made in Japan.** Treat Japan as in development **[UNCONFIRMED which product or factory]**.

The founders' Portugal trip: "When we went to Portugal to meet with mills and factories (and surf and golf our way from Lisbon to Porto), SLOAT shifted from feeling like an idea to a real thing." (https://www.instagram.com/p/DcyZiNSIK_c/)

## 5. Notable

- **Courses tied to the brand, all from IG captions.** Golden Gate Park GC (launch party, plus Christian Dean works there), Presidio Golf Course/Club, Sharp Park (Pacifica) and Half Moon Bay Ocean Course. The name itself evokes Lake Merced and Ocean Beach. **No formal course partnership found.**
- **Collabs and ambassadors.** Patrick Grimes, a pro golfer who wrote the *Golf at the Crossroads* Journal piece. IG: "We're excited to be working with Patrick Grimes" (https://www.instagram.com/p/DeriWyxCcfK/). Note: his essay is about burnout, depression, ayahuasca and therapy, so handle it carefully if you reference it. Christian Dean is featured but not described as an ambassador. **No product collabs found.**
- **Journal contributors.** Lauren Goodman (ex-WSJ Off Duty fashion director), *Outstanding on the Green*. Patrick Thomas, *Wear Patterns*. https://sloat.cc/blogs/journal
- **Retailers.** None found. DTC only via sloat.cc (SF Golf: "an early, men's-only direct-to-consumer collection"). The site has no stockist page.
- **Positioning lines:**
  - "golf clothes could be made differently: from natural materials, with restraint, and without the overtly technical appearance of conventional performance apparel." (About)
  - "Our belief is that if we can make clothing that works here in San Francisco, it will work in many places beyond our own." (Ode)
  - The FAQ is candid about trade-offs: organic cotton "doesn't wick moisture as aggressively as a synthetic performance polo," and Ventile is "water-resistant rather than fully waterproof" (https://sloat.cc/pages/faq).
- **Price points** (live catalogue): hats $65, tee $85, shorts $145, jersey polo $155, Day and Caddie Pant $195, Merino Polo and crew $215, quarter-zip $225, hoodie $245, Ventile Wind Shirt $295, Vest $345, Jacket $595.
- **Colour story.** "The color palette for our first collection was drawn directly from the range of colors you find out here [Ocean Beach], from the sand and dunes to the ocean and sky." (https://www.instagram.com/p/Deh_zzBifoF/) Colour names on the product files include Oil Green, Seed Pearl, Stargazer, Aluminum, Stone, Navy, Black and Charcoal.
- **Look.** The visual language mixes grainy B&W and warm film colour, fog and coast, cypress trees, a surfing crossover, and a red-orange triangle logo.
