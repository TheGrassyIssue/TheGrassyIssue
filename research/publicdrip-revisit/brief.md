## 1. Fact check: what the June post claimed

| June claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Neil Tan got back into golf at Van Cortlandt Park (Bronx) in 2020 | **Holds up.** LINKS says the brand was founded in Brooklyn in 2020 "when its creator, Neil Tan, decided to get back into playing golf and teed it up at Van Cortlandt Park Golf Course." He last played in 2010, at age 24. The year he went back is implied to be 2020, not stated separately. Hypebeast (2023) adds that the SS23 collection was shot at Van Cortlandt. | https://linksmagazine.com/7-under-the-radar-golf-apparel-brands-to-know-in-2026/ ; https://hypebeast.com/2023/4/public-drip-golf-ss23-collection |
| "A Hypebeast feature" | **True, and undersold.** There are several Hypebeast/Hypegolf pieces: SS23 (Apr 20, 2023), "nine rising brands changing golf" (Aug 2023), Spring 2024, SS25 (May 5, 2025) and the Cleeks collab (Jun 2, 2025). | https://hypebeast.com/2023/4/public-drip-golf-ss23-collection ; https://hypebeast.com/2023/8/nine-rising-brands-changing-golf-malbon-women-radry-gumtree-manors-jain-public-drip-pluto ; https://hypebeast.com/2025/5/public-drips-ss25-collection-is-intentional-and-supremely-wearable ; https://hypebeast.com/2025/6/liv-golf-cleeks-public-drip-streetwear-brooklyn-collaboration |
| Cleeks GC (LIV) collab | **True, with a correction (below).** The first capsule, unofficially titled "Tradition, Refreshed", launched June 4, 2025 during LIV Golf Virginia. Cleeks calls the 2026 release its "second collaboration with Public Drip", and the LIV site describes Public Drip as "Cleeks' clothing partner". | https://hypebeast.com/2025/6/liv-golf-cleeks-public-drip-streetwear-brooklyn-collaboration ; https://www.livgolf.com/cleeks-gc/collabs/public-drip ; https://www.livgolf.com/cleeks-golf-club/art-of-golf/neil-tan |
| **Correction:** June post called the Frequency Polo and Up & Down Polo "Cleeks collab" pieces | **Not supported. Fix this.** Neither product page mentions Cleeks; both are standard SS26 "Public Athlete" jacquard polos. The Cleeks collab page lists the **Public Athlete Polo in Coffee, Pine Grove and Cream** ($95). The 2025 capsule was the Public Athlete Polo, mock necks, recycled fishing-net tees and pleated pants. | https://publicdrip.com/products/public-athlete-frequency-polo ; https://publicdrip.com/products/public-athlete-up-down-polo ; https://www.livgolf.com/cleeks-gc/collabs/public-drip |
| LINKS Magazine "brands to watch 2026" | **True, but fix the wording.** The piece is "7 Under-the-Radar Golf Apparel Brands to Know in 2026" by Shaun Tolson (Apr 23, 2026; updated May 8), and Public Drip is the first brand listed. PD's IG says Tolson found them at their PGA Show booth. | https://linksmagazine.com/7-under-the-radar-golf-apparel-brands-to-know-in-2026/ ; https://www.instagram.com/p/DXutYXBAflH/ |

Other small errors in the June post's product copy:
- The Swing Shirt is called "technical stretch fabric". Hypebeast SS25 calls it "nylon micro ripstop", and IG (Mar 2026) says it "packs neatly into its own pocket". https://hypebeast.com/2025/5/public-drips-ss25-collection-is-intentional-and-supremely-wearable ; https://www.instagram.com/p/DWHjkwBAZTH/
- The June post said "These are the pants that made Hypebeast take notice". That attribution is uncertain. Hypebeast's 2025 Cleeks piece does mention the pleated pants (first seen on Martin Kaymer at the 2025 PGA Championship), but we can't say they were *why* Hypebeast covered the brand.

**Nightshift post check:** our FW26 post says it "Dropped Aug 21". Shopify `published_at` is Aug 21, but the public launch was **Aug 28, 2026, 2PM EST** ("Available tomorrow @2PM EST", posted Aug 27; "Nightshift is here!", posted Aug 28). https://www.instagram.com/p/Dcjvs_igfVz/ ; https://www.instagram.com/p/Dcl9GkjEXJ_/
The Nightshift post also said the white and storm short-sleeve Public Athlete Polos had sold out. Both are **back in stock in all sizes** as of Oct 22, and appear in a new "Restocked Favorites" collection (published Sep 18). https://publicdrip.com/collections/restocked-favorites

  - All three carry an Akashi-Kama "NY to CA" woven label.
