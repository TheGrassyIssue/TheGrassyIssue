# Lions Muny deep dive — research dossier (2026-08-31)

## The course
- 18 holes, par 71, 5,825 yd (blues; white 5,389, red 4,660). Rating/slope 69.8/123 blue, 68/118 white (men). Course record 60.
- 2901 Enfield Rd, 78703 — Tarrytown, ~2 miles from the Capitol. 141 acres of UT's Brackenridge Tract.
- Opened 1924 by the Austin Lions Club — first public course in the city; nine holes at first, later 18. City took over the lease in 1936 (city page) — some sources say 1937; use "1936" per austintexas.gov.
- Design lineage (golfcourse.wiki): B.F. Rowe; small changes attributed to A.W. Tillinghast; modifications by Tom Penick and Roy Kizer; Leon + Charles Howard renovation (1974 redesign per Conservancy — restoration plans target pre-1974 layout).
- Character: sharp doglegs reward placement over distance; limestone ground runs hard when dry; front nine = doglegs/bunkers/elevation, back nine = water. Signature stone lion on the practice green.
- Notables hosted: Hogan, Nelson, Sandra Haynie, Betsy Rawls, Tom Kite, Crenshaw. Hosts the Firecracker Open — Texas' oldest amateur tournament (July 4th weekend). ~60,000 rounds/yr (USGA 2019).
- Crenshaw at Muny: grew up across the street at O. Henry MS; first trophy as a 4th grader; shot 74 at age ten; first ace at 11; two-time Firecracker champion.

## Desegregation (1950)
- Alvin Propps, 9-year-old Black caddie, + another Black youth played in defiance of Jim Crow; detained; Mayor Taylor Glass: "Let them play." Council opened the course to all rather than build a separate course.
- First integrated muni course south of the Mason-Dixon Line — four years before Brown v. Board (1954).
- Black golfers bused in from across Texas in the '50s-'60s; Joe Louis held clinics drawing thousands.
- National Register of Historic Places 2016 (civil rights landmark); Texas Registry of Historic Golf Courses; National Trust "11 Most Endangered" list.

## The fight
- Land deeded by Col. George Washington Brackenridge to UT in 1910 (350-acre tract) "for the purpose of advancing and promoting University education."
- Long-term lease expired 2019; since then Muny runs on short rolling extensions (month-to-month with 5-month termination notice per KXAN). City paid ~$500k/yr; UT has valued the land use at ~$6M/yr. UT has floated mixed residential/commercial redevelopment.
- Muny Conservancy (est. 2019): fundraising, Coore & Crenshaw pro-bono restoration plan (proposed 2017) targeting the pre-1974 layout; old clubhouse → education center/golf museum. Save Muny raised another $1M in 2024 (Statesman).
- Texas legislature: HB 2421 (2025) extends the Save Historic Muny District board through May 2027.
- Centennial: Oct 2024. Inaugural Muny Cup + 19th Hole Party May 8, 2026, sold out (Tribeza).

## Verbatim quotes (USGA, Mike Trostel, March 27 2019 — all verbatim)
- Ben Crenshaw: "I can't imagine this place not being here. 'Muny' means so much to this community."
- Ben Crenshaw: "As someone for whom golf has been my whole life, I see what this course means to people. It would be a horror show if Muny went away. It gives them a reason for being, a reason for friendship and fellowship in a beautiful place. And not just golfers' lives."
- Ben Crenshaw: "I am going to put up whatever reputation I have to save this course."
- General Marshall (caddied at Lions 1946-1950): "I wish I could express how good I felt. When they integrated this course, black golfers from all over the state of Texas would come here to play golf."
- Mary Arnold (Save Muny): "Lions has always been accessible to all people since it opened in 1924. It's a beautiful walk, there are many wildlife habitats and it has some of the most gorgeous trees in all of Texas."

## Practical (austintexas.gov, fees effective 10/1/2025)
- Green fees: $35 Mon-Thu / $41 Fri / $44 weekend+holiday; evening $29/$31/$34; sunset $28; junior $20; senior $27 (Mon-Thu); 9 holes $28. Cart $18 ($12 sunset). Card only, no cash.
- Resident card $5/yr for discounts. GolfATX memberships cover Lions.
- Tee times: online Mon-Thu open 9am 7 days out; weekend times drop the preceding Monday 8pm online only (account required). Pro shop (512) 978-MUNY.
- Range: irons only; $7/$10 buckets; closes Mondays 1pm; closed 3rd Monday monthly. Overseeding closure Oct 6-7 2026.
- Cisco's Muny Cafe on site. Dog friendly. Walkable.
