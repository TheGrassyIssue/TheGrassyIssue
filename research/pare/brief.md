## Collaborations (all confirmed from pare.golf product copy unless noted)
- **Imperial Hats** (Oct 2026). The brand says it is "the first Filipino-American company to partner with Imperial Hats". Manila logo hat in White/Black/Walnut/Olive, $55, for the 3rd anniversary and Filipino American History Month.
- **Bluetross** (Oct 2026). Waxed-canvas driver ($170) and blade putter ($150) covers, Made in USA. The copy says it's Bluetross's first time working outside leather.
- **Mogshade** (Oct 2026). Driver ($135) and fairway ($130) covers, "crafted entirely in Portugal using rescued 100% wool textiles", "two years in the making". Sold out.
- **Barnaby's** (Sep 2025). "What The Font" driver and mallet putter covers. Paré's first headcovers, and Barnaby's "first public release beyond gloves". Sold out.
- **Little Sundays** (Oct 2025). Toddler golf bag; the copy names Jake (Little Sundays) and Matt. Sold out.
- **Paris Golf Gallery** (2025). Snapback designed for Paris Fashion Week. Sold out.
- **HYPEGOLF exclusives** (Aug 2026). Tees, hoodie, and a Manila Tour Visor made "in collaboration with Tour Visor". Mostly sold out.
- **Supreme blanks** long sleeve ("Supreme blanks IYKYK"). Supreme blanks, not a collaboration.

## Milestones
- **Paris Fashion Week 2025, with Paris Golf Gallery.** Pierre Greilsamer of PGG followed Paré on Instagram "before I even had a product" and connected him with Gwop Meet, a golf swap meet. The showcase was in the Cadillac building next to the Paris Opera. The about page thanks "Troy, Pierre, and Malo". (Gordon College; about page; MyGolfSpy.)
- The press logos on the about page's "Featured On" strip are: MyGolfSpy, Kollective Hustle, Paris Golf Gallery, Gordon College, Barnaby's, Gwop (sticker), Urban Syndicate, Badlands, MVP (Momentum and Value for People of Color), West End Gold, plus a YouTube channel avatar. Only MyGolfSpy, Kollective Hustle and Gordon College were read in full. The rest are **UNVERIFIED** as editorial coverage.
- Podcasts: Father Time w/ De'Meco & Blair, Ep. 053 (podcastrex listing; page body didn't load). Save Par Golf also featured "Matty of Paré Golf", but that comes from a search-result snippet only, so **UNVERIFIED**.
- Global plans: after Paris he is "starting to expedite launching globally" into Europe and Asia; until then the focus was the US and Canada (Gordon College, July 2025). Whether that has happened is **UNVERIFIED**.
- Product photo credit: "Mitch Coburn / The Links Social Tour" (Off-White Snapback product page).

## Editorial flags
- The **"[HYPEGOLF EXCLUSIVE] Paré Putang Ina T-Shirt"** (in stock, $60) carries a Tagalog expletive. Handle with care if you feature it.
- Several product notes mention "bastos" and other jokey slang. The contact page tagline is "Certified Bastos Country Club".
- The founder talks openly about his faith ("I use Paré as my own ministry", Gordon College). Include it only if relevant.
- The one MyGolfSpy reader comment says most of the stock is sold out. That matches the catalogue: 26 of 112 products are in stock, and the brand runs on limited drops.
