## B. Context

### 1. Courses that allow dogs

**UK and Ireland tradition**
- **Old links and dogs:** Golf Digest says UK dogs "are more likely to be allowed at old links courses with lots of common walking ground than at newer, inland operations". The same piece says Berkshire Golf Club members may bring dogs, but visitors must ask the club secretary. Sunningdale's club historian is quoted on the number of dogs overseas visitors see. https://www.golfdigest.com/story/dogs-on-the-golf-course
- **St Andrews:** the Links Trust courses are a public park "and there are dogs sharing the grounds with golfers every day". The same source says Cruden Bay mostly lets you bring a dog for the loop. https://www.golfpass.com/travel-advisor/articles/let-the-dogs-out-already
- **Old Course on Sundays:** the course closes to golf and works as a dog park. "Stretching to 45 acres, The Old Course on Sundays is easily among the biggest dog parks in the world." The rules are to pick up after your dog and stay off the greens. (Sean Zak, GOLF.com, June 23, 2022) https://golf.com/travel/old-course-st-andrews-dogs-sundays/
- **Brancaster, Rye, New Zealand GC:** Sounder's own copy names these clubs, along with the Kent links and Surrey heathland, as places where "dogs seem to outnumber golfers". https://soundergolf.com/products/sounder-x-yeti-dog-bowl
- **Millport Golf Club (Isle of Cumbrae):** asks dog walkers to keep dogs on leads, away from bunkers and greens, and to clear up fouling. (The Golf Business, Feb 11, 2019) https://www.thegolfbusiness.co.uk/?p=912513
- **Scottish Outdoor Access Code:** people crossing a golf course must keep off greens, let players play their shot, follow paths and "keep your dog on a short lead". https://www.outdooraccess-scotland.scot/practical-guide-all/golf-courses
- **Cavendish Golf Club (Buxton, England):** has a written dog policy (see etiquette below). https://www.cavendishgolfclub.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/CGC-Dog-Policy-_-updated-July-2021.pdf
- **Padraig Harrington at the Irish Open pro-am:** he brought his Bernese Mountain Dogs, Wilson and Setanta, to the 2023 pro-am at The K Club, and the dogs wore credential badges. https://www.wftv.com/news/trending/paws-course-padraig-harrington-brings-dogs-romp-during-irish-open-pro-am/U6VQH5ZFJFA5HF6FS23PCYPQTU
- **Unverified:** I could not find official dog pages for Lahinch, Rosapenna, Enniscrone, Royal West Norfolk, Rye or NZGC, so cite those only through the Sounder copy. A Dundee story saying Monifieth allows dogs on a lead (Courier, undated) returned 403 and could not be checked.

**United States**
- **Austin (local hook), Roy Kizer:** the official City of Austin course rules (Roy Kizer scorecard, General Rules) say "Dogs must be on a leash and under control." The course page also lists a $10 non-golfer cart fee for "persons 13 & up, including pets". https://austin.widen.net/s/pjqdfbcs5x/roy-kizer-golf-course-austin-tx-scorecard-bench-craft-company-front and https://www.austintexas.gov/golfatx/roy-kizer-course
- **Austin munis and Delaware Springs (2015 claim):** a 2015 Golf Advisor (now GolfPass) column by Brandon Tucker says Austin's municipal courses allow dogs "at each", including Butler Park Pitch 'n Putt. It also covers Delaware Springs in Burnet, TX, with the rules "scoop the poop, and keep them on a leash". It mentions a puppy at Hancock. These are 2015 claims; only the Kizer rule above is confirmed as current. Call Lions, Jimmy Clay, Hancock and Morris Williams before publishing. https://www.golfpass.com/travel-advisor/articles/let-the-dogs-out-already
- **Belgrade Lakes GC (Maine):** started with a free shaded kennel for dogs left in cars, then "Heck, just let 'em out on the course." https://www.golfdigest.com/story/dogs-on-the-golf-course
- **Indian Valley GC and San Geronimo (Marin County, CA), Seaside GC (Oregon):** all named as dog-friendly in the same Golf Digest piece (2014; San Geronimo has since closed to golf, so check it). https://www.golfdigest.com/story/dogs-on-the-golf-course
- **Sand Valley (Wisconsin):** Seamus Golf's 2017 trip report says dogs came with prior approval from the resort, and the GM brought his own hound. Not an official resort policy page; confirm with Sand Valley. https://seamusgolf.com/blogs/news/sand-valley-experience
- **Poppy Hills (CA):** in a 2015 reply, the club said it is dog-friendly as long as dogs stay on leash and stay quiet while owners putt. This is a comment on the GolfPass article above; treat it as weak.
- **Billy Casper Golf (counter-example):** in 2014 the 150-course operator allowed "only trained service dogs". https://www.golfdigest.com/story/dogs-on-the-golf-course
- **Bandon and Prairie Club:** no documented dog policy found; I would not claim either.

### 2. Etiquette and typical rules (sourced)
- **Leash or control:** "Dogs must be on a leash and under control" (Roy Kizer, Austin). Cavendish asks for a lead "if you have difficulty controlling your dog". The Scottish Access Code says a "short lead".
- **Off greens, tees and bunkers:** Cavendish says "kept well clear of greens, greens approaches, tees and bunkers". The Old Course on Sundays asks people to stay off the greens.
- **Cleanup:** Cavendish says owners "must carry dog waste bags". Indian Valley's rule is "Pick up crap and rake paw prints in bunkers" (Golf Digest). The Delaware Springs and Austin rule is "scoop the poop" (GolfPass).
- **No peeing on mown areas:** Cavendish, rule 3.
- **Quiet during shots:** Golf Digest says the best golf dogs heel when players hit. Poppy Hills asks for quiet dogs while owners putt.
- **Ask first:** visitors need permission at Berkshire GC, and Sand Valley requires prior approval.
- **Training:** trainer Jonathan Klein says to treat "the first two to three rounds strictly a training experience" (Golf Digest).
- **Carts:** Tucker (GolfPass) does not think it is safe for dogs to ride in a moving cart, especially if they are tied up.
