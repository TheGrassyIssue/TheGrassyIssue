# Manors Golf — Brand Revisited dossier
Researched 2026-09-02.

## The hook
Manors launched in 2019 rejecting technical golf apparel — then in spring 2023
wiped its socials and **relaunched as a technical golf brand**. That reversal is
the story. Everything below is sourced; the arc is not editorialising.

## Who
- London, founded **2019**. Co-founders **Jojo Regan** (lifelong golfer) and
  **Luke Davies** (school friend, came to golf later). Joined by **Nick Watts**,
  Fashion Director, who shaped the aesthetic.
- Origin: the two played together through summer 2018 dressed completely
  differently — Regan in non-golf clothing, Davies in streetwear.
- Stated influences: Arnold Palmer, Gary Player, Jack Nicklaus. Brand line:
  **"personality before performance."**

## VERBATIM quotes — Hypebeast, Jack Stanley, 7 May 2021
All exact. Do not paraphrase into quotation marks.

**Jojo Regan, co-founder:**
> "It was immediately clear to both Luke and I that we never wore anything that
> was typically seen as golf. The game is very committed to focusing on the
> technical side, the performance, hitting bombs, hitting the balls harder,
> faster and further. We were just about trying to break 90, or 80 on a very good
> day. We didn't need a technical polo. If I'm wearing a sweat-wicking performance
> polo, it's not going to be the difference between me hitting 89 or 79."

> "Having grown up in the game, I've definitely seen a marked change in the
> general conversation. I think if you were to have a stereotypical view of golf,
> it is that 'country club elite' aesthetic. That frustrates us because it's
> wrong. You have hackers up and down the country who aren't part of a club, or
> are part of a club because it's a community. That message was being lost."

> "It's one of the only sports where you can actually choose what to look like and
> how to dress. That's such a unique opportunity, and I don't feel like there's
> much flair on the tour anymore."

> "We want to push that stereotype to something that is a lot more representative
> of golf and where it is now."

**Nick Watts, Fashion Director:**
> "We look at a lot of archive footage, and I couldn't believe how well the old
> golfers dressed."
> "The great thing is, everyone is dressed in these outfits. It's not just the
> golfers, it's also the audience. Even the caddies look great."
> "We wanted to get away from current products, which are tight fitting and you
> wouldn't want to be seen walking down the street in."

**Manors' own site copy, quoted by Hypebeast:** golf has become "the snobbish
sport of suburbia... The perception of the game is that it is uptight, entrenched
in tradition and deeply unstylish."

## The 2023 rebrand — "A Change of Course"
- Spring 2023: socials wiped, relaunched as a **technical** golf brand.
- Rebrand by **Stink Studios**.
- Reported results (SOURCE: sonderandtell Substack, a marketing newsletter —
  ATTRIBUTE, do not state flat): **up 859% YoY**, a shift from wholesale to DTC,
  a **Harrods** retail partnership, the Reebok collab, and MANORS as a playable
  skin in **2K PGA Tour 2025**.
  → Verify the 859% against a second source before publishing, or attribute it
    explicitly to the newsletter.

## Collabs
- **adidas Golf — "The Beautiful Game", SS21.** Golf x football, launched against
  The Open and Euro 2020. The crest swaps the three lions for **three golf irons**.
  Ian Wright involved (per Hypebeast tag/headline — confirm his exact role).
- **Reebok x Manors, June 2025** — first Reebok collab, 10 pieces, global 10 June
  2025. Footwear: **OG Pump Golf** and **Club C Revenge Vintage**.
- **Reebok x Manors II, 2026** — TGI already covered it: /drops/manors-ss26 era.
- Also endorse **Adem Wahbi**, ranked among the world's top 50 golfers with
  disabilities (age 22 as of the 2021 article — recheck current standing).
