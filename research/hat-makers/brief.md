# Three hat makers with momentum: research brief

Researched October 25, 2026. Communal (communalmfg.com), Duke + Dexter (dukeanddexter.com), Winslow (winslowla.com).
Lenny: "brands with momentum", 6 hats per brand. Every claim has a source; unconfirmed items are marked.

## Communal (Austin, TX)
- Communal's About page: "Communal started in 2020 and has spent the years since figuring out exactly what it is: a headwear company focused on American iconography. Based in Austin, TX." Its meta description says "founded in 2019". The hemp business started in 2019, the hats in 2020. Either say "since 2020" or "the company started in 2019 as Communal Cowboy". (communalmfg.com/pages/about)
- Started as Communal Cowboy, a hemp brand. They had an East Side Austin storefront ready (now Community Garden), but COVID closed it before it opened. Clothing began as a way to run ads, since hemp ads were blocked: "let's take our inspirations and brand aesthetics and create a clothing line - that way we can run ads and leave breadcrumbs to our hemp products. And it worked (better than we could have ever imagined)." They dropped CBD/THC in December 2023 to focus on clothing. (Our Farewell To Hemp, Dec 8 2023, posted under Justin Dumbeck's name, communalmfg.com/blogs/news/our-farewell-to-hemp)
- Founder: the farewell post is posted under Justin Dumbeck's name on Communal's own site, so we can name him. San Martini (a third-party retailer blog) also names Jake Spencer and Ryan Wall. That isn't confirmed by Communal itself, so don't name them.
- Positioning (About): "We sit at the intersection of heritage Americana and modern streetwear, closer to a design studio mining archives than a novelty hat brand chasing a moment."
- Every hat: $60, cotton twill, a limited lifetime warranty. Two shapes: Standard Brim (mesh flap front) and Short Brim.
- The Dang Hat started as a one-of-one by Austin artist Erik Ross (San Martini; unconfirmed by Communal). Communal's site: "The original Dang Hat." It has 86 reviews, 97% five stars (product page, Oct 25 2026).
- Stockists named by San Martini: Huckberry (confirmed: huckberry.com lists the Communal Cowboy Dang Hat), Uncrate Supply, Hat Club.
- Momentum: rebranded from Communal Cowboy to Communal MFG. New nautical drop on Sept 24 2026: C Anchor, The Captain, Trading Co. The site shows 4.93 stars from 215 reviews. They run a "Big Bag of Music" Spotify playlist.
- Local angle: an Austin hat brand. Fits TGI's Austin muni focus.

## Duke + Dexter (London)
- Founded 2015 in London (Our Story page). Founder Archie Hewlett (LDNFASHION 2021, WWD, Drapers). He started selling slippers out of his bedroom on a £9,000 loan. All shoes are handmade at the brand's factory in England (LDNFASHION). Known for penny loafers.
- Headwear is new territory. Product copy calls the satin DUKE caps and the Pit Stop cap "a first for D+D". The beanie copy: "Our first beanie. We made it a good one."
- Momentum: a BWT Alpine Formula One Team collaboration (D+D collection page), a Cole Buxton "Last Temptation" collab, the new Midnight Chateau collection (Sept 2026), and the AW26 lookbook.
- Quote option (LDNFASHION, 2021), from Hewlett on what inspires him: "Building something special with like-minded, passionate and energetic people who are all working towards the same goal."
- The Eat Dirt cap is "for the trash talkers", a natural fit for golf.
- Price: caps $90 and beanies $110 on the US store (USD market, checked live). The Cole Buxton cap is $70. Hand-painted 1-of-1 caps at $350 are all sold out.

## Winslow (Los Angeles)
- An "LA-based brand focused on timeless design and functional style. We specialize in vintage hats that capture the feel of worn-in classics with modern fit and structure" (homepage).
- "Our hats are the entry point—structured, timeless, and built to age with you. But what's coming next is bigger: full collections of vintage American streetwear" (LA streetwear page). That is the momentum: hats first, now hoodies, sweaters, jackets and pants.
- Washed caps: "Made in the USA, 100% Cotton, Distressed by Hand, Stone Washed, 5 Panel Soft Structured, Leather Strap Closure."
- The Weathered Hat copy (sold out) mentions golf directly: "Slide into the summer in our founders go-to hat at the moment. Perfect for hanging by the lake or going low on the golf course."
- No founder named anywhere public. Don't name one.
- Only 5 hats in stock. Washed Red, Weathered, Camo, Brown and White are sold out. The Gym Bag ($75) is in stock.
