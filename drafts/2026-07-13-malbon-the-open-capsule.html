Malbon Made an Open Capsule for a Links It Won't Be Playing

With the 154th Open at Royal Birkdale this week, Malbon rolled out a tournament-tied capsule built for the first tee and the flight home alike. It is the brand's usual move — clock the biggest week on the calendar, show up in its own colors instead of the tournament's. Whether Birkdale's wind cares about a bunny logo is a separate question.
