Gumtree Printed Its Range Rover, All Twenty Copies, And They're Already Gone

August's Studio Special is a fine-art giclee of Gumtree's 1993 Range Rover Classic in Ardennes Green, shot up in the Catskills and printed in Brooklyn in a numbered run of twenty. It cost $100, went to the wall as gallery stock, and sold through before most people finished their coffee. A brand that names a green after a Belgian forest and then puts it on a truck instead of a fairway is doing exactly what it says on the tin.
