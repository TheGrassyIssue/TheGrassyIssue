Devereux — Southern Draw Collection

Devereux’s Southern Draw collection pays homage to Augusta and the gravitational pull of the South, which is a polite way of saying the humidity and the charm are both inescapable. Traditional cuts get injected with unexpected color and print, and the Westward Polo is their most fashion-forward piece this spring. If your golf wardrobe needs a drawl, here it is.
