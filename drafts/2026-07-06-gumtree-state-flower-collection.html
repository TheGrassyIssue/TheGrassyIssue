Gumtree's State Flower Shirts Are Basically One-of-Ones

Gumtree's latest is twenty Japanese lace-linen shirts, each carrying a hand-embroidered state-flower patch cut from vintage American textiles from the '40s through the '70s. Twenty pieces, no two alike, which is less a collection than a small archive you can button up. It is the rare golf shirt where the tag reading "made from something older than you" is the entire point.
