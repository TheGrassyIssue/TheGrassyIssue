Manors Drops the Summer Capsule, Shoots the Lookbook in St. Lucia

Manors took Victor Cruz and Josh Denzel to St. Lucia's Point Hardy course to model the Summer Capsule — new Buttersoft Polos, Stableford Shorts, and Ranger Golf Shorts in sand-and-sea colorways. The Greenskeeper Shorts are a chopped version of the cult trouser, with front pleats and workwear pockets that actually hold things. Flying to the Caribbean to photograph golf shorts is either peak commitment or peak delusion, and honestly both work here.
