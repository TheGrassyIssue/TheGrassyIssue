Dimple & Divot: May Drop

D&D's monthly drop lands with what we assume is another round of their design-forward brushes, divot tools, and caddie towels in new colorways. The brand bills itself as "Created in 2020, Perfected in 2026," which is either confidence or a dare. Either way the brushes are genuinely good and nobody needs permission to own a nice one.
