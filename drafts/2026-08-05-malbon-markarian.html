Markarian Brings Its Red-Carpet Habits to Malbon's Back Nine

Markarian, the New York eveningwear house that usually dresses people for galas, has made Malbon a 13-piece women's collection of florals, soft knits, and tailoring — plus headcovers. It runs from a $34 sock to a $378 bomber, and yes, the $148 floral driver cover costs more than the polo it rides next to. Somewhere a starter is squinting at a Gia Dress on the first tee and deciding, correctly, not to say anything.
