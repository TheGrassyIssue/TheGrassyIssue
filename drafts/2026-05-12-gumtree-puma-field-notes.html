Gumtree Golf x PUMA: The Field Notes Collection Says Slow Down, Please

PUMA and Gumtree Golf & Nature Club dropped Field Notes on April 22 — a full apparel capsule shot in the Rockies and built around the radical idea that you don’t need to be in a hurry out there. The lineup runs from a landscape-toned polo ($95) and Conservation Dept. fleece ($130) to a wildflower reference tee and unstructured 5-panels ($40). Natural materials, muted palette, zero corporate energy — basically a nature walk that happens to include wedge shots.

Price range: $40 – $130
