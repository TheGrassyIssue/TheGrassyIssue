The Window
June 1 – June 20
Applications opened today. You have exactly twenty days to submit at masters.com. Log in or create an account, pick your days, pick your ticket count (1–4), and hope for the best.
How the Lottery Works
1 Go to masters.com. Create an account or log into an existing one.
2 Select the days you want to attend. You can apply for every day — practice and tournament.
3 Choose 1–4 tickets per day. One entry per person. No gaming the system.
4 After June 20, Augusta runs a random lottery. Winners get notified by email. Everyone else gets a polite no.

The Fine Print
What You Should Know
• You can apply for every day but can only win one day.
• Tournament tickets are drawn first. If you lose, you're re-entered for practice rounds.
• Resale is strictly prohibited. Augusta will revoke your badge at the gate.
• Series badge holders and Augusta employees are ineligible for tournament rounds.
