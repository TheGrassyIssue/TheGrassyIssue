Malbon Softens the 9TWENTY Down to an Everyday Cap

Malbon's New Era 9TWENTY is back in an unstructured, low-crown cut across a quiet palette, with M-logo, Script, and bird-print options. It is the un-showy end of the Malbon catalog — a hat you can wear off the course without announcing anything. Which, for a brand that usually announces plenty, is the interesting part.
