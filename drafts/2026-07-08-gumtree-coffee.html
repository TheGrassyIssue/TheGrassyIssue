Gumtree Is Selling Coffee Now, and a Headcover Made of Sacks

Gumtree's latest is a full coffee line — Brooklyn-roasted beans, a moka pot, a mug set — plus a headcover sewn from coffee sacks and limited to seven. Beans start at $23; the sack cover starts at $125 if you can land one of the seven. A golf brand that also wants to fix your morning is either overreach or exactly the point.
