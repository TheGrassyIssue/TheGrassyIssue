Reflo's Open Capsule Is Made of Recycled Everything

Reflo is back with an officially licensed capsule for the 154th Open at Royal Birkdale, its second year running, all recycled materials with a Stonechat bird motif and dune-and-fairway graphics. Every piece is individually numbered and retired once it sells out, so the run is finite by design. A tournament capsule that plans its own extinction is at least honest about the hype cycle.
