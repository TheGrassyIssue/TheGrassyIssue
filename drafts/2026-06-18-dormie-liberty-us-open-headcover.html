Dormie's Lady Liberty Headcover Arrives for U.S. Open Week

To mark the U.S. Open's return to New York, Dormie cut a leather driver cover bearing the Statue of Liberty's face, raised through an inset applique that catches light like real patina. Each one is numbered and the run is tiny, the same playbook as their PGA Championship eagle cover, which was 25 units and gone the same day. It's $200 for a headcover you will likely guard more carefully than the driver living inside it.
