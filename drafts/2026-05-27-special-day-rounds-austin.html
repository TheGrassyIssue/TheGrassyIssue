8 Special Day Rounds Near Austin
Arthur Hills carved Wolfdancer through Lost Pines like he had a grudge against flat land. The greens are fast, the elevation changes are real, and the stretch from 14 to 18 is one of the best closing runs in Texas. You do not need to be a Hyatt guest to play, which makes this the most accessible great course in the Austin orbit. Thirty minutes east and a world away from your Tuesday nine at the muni.
