Malbon x Daniel Arsham: Chapter Three Lands in Oxidized Green

The third Arsham x Malbon collection went live today, hats, outerwear and accessories all soaked in the artist's signature oxidized-green patina, with the Buckets logo redrawn through his round-eyeglass treatment. It reads less like a brand borrowing an artist and more like an Arsham object that happens to have a golf figure buried inside it, which appears to be the whole brief. The caption promises gear "built for the course, made to live beyond it," which is a generous thing to say about a bucket hat until you see it in this color.
