Students Golf Takes Its Performance Line, Course Studies, to Paris

Students is back at the Paris Golf Gallery this week to show its Spring/Summer 2026 season and, for the first time, Course Studies, the performance line it has been sitting on. The pitch is technical fabric without the technical billboard, built around the brand's tidy "study the game" framing rather than the usual neon and logos. Appointments are by email only, which is either exclusivity or a very small room, and honestly both are on brand.
