Devereux Goes Full Gorpcore With the GORP Golf Collection

Devereux took the gorpcore trend, pointed it at a fairway, and called it a collection. The GORP Golf line features Alien Camo — a chaotic pattern of deep greens, vibrant pinks, and blacks that looks like a hiking trail had a disagreement with a laser tag arena. Headcovers, polos, tees, and accessories round out the drop. It is not subtle, and that appears to be the entire point.
