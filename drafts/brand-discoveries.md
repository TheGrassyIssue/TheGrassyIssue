## Discovered May 21 (Still Tracking)

### VESSEL (Carlsbad, CA)
- **URL:** https://vesselbags.com
- **Why they fit:** Known for premium golf bags, VESSEL just launched their debut apparel collection (SS26) on May 14-15. Same obsessive design DNA applied to performance wear. ### Olydoe (UK)
- **URL:** https://olydoe.com
- **Why they fit:** Launched October 2025 by Scott Matthews. Born from wanting to buy a decent polo at a course and finding nothing. Early-stage brand with grassroots energy. Watch for growth.

## Discovered May 20 (Still Tracking)

### Radry Golf
- **URL:** https://radry.com
- **Why they fit:** Self-describes as "a never-ending art project disguised as a golf brand." Minimalist, art-inspired, lifestyle-first with relaxed modern cuts and unisex-friendly fits. Already drafted their Chasing Light collection. Fits TGI perfectly — consider adding to extended watchlist.

### Rebolf (Barcelona, Spain)
- **URL:** https://rebolf.com
- **Why they fit:** Fuses vintage golf fashion with modern streetwear playfulness. European indie energy from Barcelona. Worth a deeper look for TGI's international coverage.

### Featherie
- **URL:** https://www.featherie.com
- **Why they fit:** Founded by a 14-year-old competitive golfer (Kate Korngold), now in 100+ pro shops. Women's golf apparel with built-in tee holders and scorecard pockets. Not core TGI aesthetic but the founder story has Mo Norman underdog energy. Recently featured in LINKS Magazine.

## Previously Discovered (Still Tracking)

### CPH (Captain's Helm) — Japan
**URL:** https://captainshelmgolf.com/en
**Why they fit:** Japanese streetwear-led golf apparel with fun printed slogans, non-traditional collars, and rugged outer layers. Anti-corporate energy with genuine craft. The kind of brand Mo Norman would wear if Mo Norman were a 28-year-old in Tokyo.

### Erthe Golf
**URL:** https://erthegolf.com
**Why they fit:** Positioning themselves as the "beyond Malbon" alternative. Design-forward golf streetwear with a sustainability angle. Publishing editorial content about the indie golf scene.

### Late Nine (Stockholm, Sweden)
**URL:** https://late-nine.com
**Why they fit:** Scandinavian minimalism meets '90s Ryder Cup nostalgia. Wool trousers with double pleats, ribbed cotton polos, water-repellent windbreakers — all made in small European factories. Almost zero branding on any piece. Anti-corporate to the core.

### Agronomy Workshop (San Francisco, CA)
**URL:** https://agronomywork.shop
**Why they fit:** Design-led, functional golf apparel from a single designer (Rob Junge). Heavyweight cotton mock necks with hidden tee holders, 5-panel rope hats with integrated pencil holders. Craft-obsessed, detail-forward, zero flash. Getting strong press buzz in 2026 — consider elevating to core watchlist.

### Heathlander (New York, NY)
**URL:** https://heathlander.com
**Why they fit:** Won Best New Product at 2025 PGA Show. Golf shoes with heathland-inspired colorways. Now expanding into cashmere knitwear.
