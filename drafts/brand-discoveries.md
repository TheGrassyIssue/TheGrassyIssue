# Brand Discoveries — May 14, 2026

New brands spotted that fit the Grassy Issue aesthetic (design-forward, anti-corporate, Mo Norman energy):

## Agronomy Workshop
- **URL:** https://agronomywork.shop
- **Why they fit:** San Francisco label founded by designer Rob Junge. Heavyweight cotton mock neck work shirts, towels, and caps with hidden details like integrated pen holders and tee slots. Small range, high craft. They care about natural materials in a world going full polyester. Mo Norman would approve of the simplicity.

## Casualist
- **URL:** https://casualist.co (West London)
- **Why they fit:** Natural fibres, wearable tones, silhouettes built for layering. No synthetic slop. The kind of brand that thinks "performance fabric" is a phrase invented by people who don't understand cotton. Already collabed with 3Putt Round.

## Left of Field
- **URL:** https://lofgolf.com
- **Why they fit:** Sydney-based, inspired by skate and surf culture. Premium performance fabrics with relaxed fits. "A thoughtful alternative to the golf style that's dominated pro shop racks for decades." Anti-pro-shop energy in the best way.

## Kadet
- **URL:** https://kadet.us
- **Why they fit:** Old-school cuts (open-neck polos inspired by vintage football shirts, '90s pullover windbreakers) built from newly-developed technical fabrics. Retro without being costume-y.

## Students Golf
- **URL:** https://studentsgolf.com (Los Angeles)
- **Why they fit:** Founded 2022, leaning away from street origins into considered takes on classic coursewear. Pleated pants, printed polos, pullover jackets. Growing steadily, feels like a breakout year.

## 3Putt Round
- **URL:** https://3puttround.com (Milan)
- **Why they fit:** Quirky golf accessories — tees, towels, hats — with fun, unconventional packaging. Italian design sensibility applied to the stuff you lose in your bag. Not taking themselves too seriously.
