# Brand Discoveries — May 15, 2026

## Late Nine (Stockholm, Sweden)
**URL:** https://late-nine.com
**Why they fit:** Scandinavian minimalism meets '90s Ryder Cup nostalgia. Wool trousers with double pleats, ribbed cotton polos, water-repellent windbreakers — all made in small European factories. Almost zero branding on any piece. Co-founded by the creative director behind CQP. This is Mo Norman energy in Swedish form. Anti-corporate to the core.

## Agronomy Workshop (San Francisco, CA)
**URL:** https://agronomywork.shop
**Why they fit:** Design-led, functional golf apparel from a single designer (Rob Junge). Heavyweight cotton mock necks with hidden tee holders, 5-panel rope hats with integrated pencil holders. Craft-obsessed, detail-forward, zero flash. Already on the watchlist but worth highlighting — they're gaining quiet momentum.

## Heathlander (New York, NY)
**URL:** https://heathlander.com
**Why they fit:** Won Best New Product at 2025 PGA Show. Golf shoes with heathland-inspired colorways (dark green midsole, pink outsole — heather flowers). Now expanding into cashmere knitwear. Founded by two ad guys who grew up on English heathland courses. The brand name alone tells you everything. Already on the watchlist but the cashmere expansion is new.
