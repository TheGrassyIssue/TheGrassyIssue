# Brand Discoveries — May 18, 2026

## CPH (Captain's Helm) — Japan
**URL:** Search "Captain's Helm Golf" — Japanese brand
**Why they fit:** Japanese streetwear-led golf apparel with fun printed slogans, non-traditional collars, and rugged outer layers. Anti-corporate energy with genuine craft. The kind of brand Mo Norman would wear if Mo Norman were a 28-year-old in Tokyo. Mentioned in multiple 2026 roundup lists as an emerging name.

## Erthe Golf
**URL:** https://erthegolf.com
**Why they fit:** Positioning themselves as the "beyond Malbon" alternative. Design-forward golf streetwear with a sustainability angle. Publishing editorial content about the indie golf scene. Worth watching to see if the product matches the editorial voice.

## Previously Discovered (Still Tracking)

### Late Nine (Stockholm, Sweden)
**URL:** https://late-nine.com
**Why they fit:** Scandinavian minimalism meets '90s Ryder Cup nostalgia. Wool trousers with double pleats, ribbed cotton polos, water-repellent windbreakers — all made in small European factories. Almost zero branding on any piece. Anti-corporate to the core.

### Agronomy Workshop (San Francisco, CA)
**URL:** https://agronomywork.shop
**Why they fit:** Design-led, functional golf apparel from a single designer. Heavyweight cotton mock necks with hidden tee holders, 5-panel rope hats with integrated pencil holders. Craft-obsessed, detail-forward, zero flash.

### Heathlander (New York, NY)
**URL:** https://heathlander.com
**Why they fit:** Won Best New Product at 2025 PGA Show. Golf shoes with heathland-inspired colorways. Now expanding into cashmere knitwear.
