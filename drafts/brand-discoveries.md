# Brand Discoveries — May 14, 2026

New brands spotted that fit the Grassy Issue aesthetic (design-forward, anti-corporate, Mo Norman energy).

---

## Added to watchlist (May 14)

### Agronomy Workshop
- **URL:** https://agronomywork.shop
- **Location:** San Francisco
- **Why:** Heavyweight cotton mock neck work shirts with hidden tee slots and pen holders. Small range, high craft. Mo Norman simplicity.

### Casualist
- **URL:** https://casualist.co
- **Location:** West London
- **Why:** Natural fibres only, wearable tones, layering silhouettes. Anti-synthetic-slop energy.

### Left of Field
- **URL:** https://lofgolf.com
- **Location:** Sydney
- **Why:** Skate/surf-influenced relaxed golf. Premium fabrics, relaxed fits. Anti-pro-shop.

### Students Golf
- **URL:** https://studentsgolf.com
- **Location:** Los Angeles
- **Why:** Founded 2022 after a health scare led founder to golf. Streetwear roots evolving into considered coursewear. Honest about the frustration of the game.

### Late Nine
- **URL:** https://late-nine.com
- **Location:** Stockholm
- **Why:** Scandinavian restraint. Wool trousers, merino knitwear, polos closer to menswear than sportswear. Everything looks like it was edited twice.

### Sugarloaf Social Club
- **URL:** https://www.sugarloafsocialclub.com
- **Location:** McLean, VA
- **Why:** Golf purist brand born from a college group chat. Arrowcord grips, collabs with Sentinel and Devereux. Creative agency energy.

### Public Drip
- **URL:** https://publicdrip.com
- **Location:** Brooklyn
- **Why:** Muni golf culture. Clothing for the "public athlete." Founded at Van Cortlandt Park. Design-forward, not logo-driven.

### Whim Golf
- **URL:** https://whimgolf.com
- **Location:** Chicago / NYC
- **Why:** 100% merino wool, made in USA. Small runs. Also runs "Free Golf" pop-up putting greens in cities. Half clothing brand, half social experiment.

### Forewind Golf
- **URL:** https://forewindgolf.com
- **Location:** Kittery, Maine
- **Why:** Makes bags and headcovers from retired racing sails. Each piece carries its previous life — creases, stitching, boat numbers. Possibly the most TGI brand on this list.

### Hame Golf Co.
- **URL:** https://hamegolf.com
- **Location:** Kingsbarns, Scotland
- **Why:** Harris Tweed, traditional tartans, copper ball markers made with the founder's retired coppersmith grandfather. Deeply tied to where golf started.

### Sounder
- **URL:** https://soundergolf.com
- **Location:** UK
- **Why:** Named after the clubs Seve used early in his career. Apparel, accessories, and a surprisingly good golf ball. Refreshingly sincere.

### Bluegrass Fairway
- **URL:** https://www.bluegrassfairway.com
- **Location:** Louisville, KY
- **Why:** Leather scorecard holders and yardage books cut from serious American hides. Develops character the more you use them.

### Field Day Sporting Co.
- **URL:** https://fielddaysportingco.com
- **Location:** USA
- **Why:** Founder spent years designing for Ralph Lauren Golf. Mid-century golf style updated with fabrics that actually perform. Old ideas, executed properly.

### Tremont
- **URL:** https://tremontsportingco.com
- **Location:** Boston
- **Why:** Started as custom hats in a BC dorm room. Now a proper workshop producing leather headcovers for serious clubs.

### Dormie Workshop
- **URL:** https://dormieworkshop.com
- **Location:** Canada
- **Why:** Handmade leather headcovers. The operation has grown but pieces still feel personal and handcrafted.

### Winston Collection
- **URL:** https://winstoncollection.com
- **Location:** Detroit
- **Why:** Handcrafted leather headcovers, made in USA. Full grain American leather. 10-year-old covers still in rotation.

### Seamus Golf
- **URL:** https://www.seamusgolf.com
- **Location:** Oregon
- **Why:** Wool headcovers and golf tools. Artisan craft paying homage to European golf roots. Jack Nicklaus energy.

### Dimple and Divot
- **URL:** https://dimpledivot.com
- **Location:** USA
- **Why:** The Hickory Golf Brush — a beautifully simple club cleaning tool carved from hickory wood. The kind of object that stays in your bag forever.

### Radmor
- **URL:** https://radmorgolf.com
- **Location:** Pacific Northwest
- **Why:** First brand in golf to eliminate virgin polyester. Recycled REPREVE, organic Pima cotton. Sustainability without the lecture.

### Head Golf
- **URL:** https://headgolf.com
- **Location:** Global (designer: Ka Ho Kam, ex-Marc Jacobs)
- **Why:** Legacy sports brand revived for golf by a former Marc Jacobs designer. Desert-inspired Americana through a fashion lens. 65-piece collection, 55% women's.

### Heathlander
- **URL:** https://heathlander.com
- **Location:** UK
- **Why:** One golf shoe, named simply "The Golf Shoe." English heathland-inspired colorways (green midsole, pink outsole). Won Best New Product at PGA Show.

### Macade
- **URL:** https://macadegolf.com
- **Location:** Europe
- **Why:** Modern European golf look. Sleek silhouettes, technical fabrics from contemporary sportswear. Sharp without feeling forced.

### Local Rule
- **URL:** https://local-rule.com
- **Location:** Scandinavia
- **Why:** Restrained, style-forward. Clean lines, minimal design. Closer to modern menswear than golf kit.

### Fantl Sport
- **URL:** https://www.fantlsport.com
- **Location:** New Zealand
- **Why:** Built for real weather — wind, rain, sun, sometimes on the same hole. Technical enough to handle it, restrained enough to look normal.

### Rebolf
- **URL:** https://rebolf.com
- **Location:** Barcelona
- **Why:** Vintage golf meets streetwear. Fun tees, pleated trousers, collared sweaters. Golfwear you'd wear on a date.

### Angus and Grace Go Golfing
- **URL:** https://angusandgracegogolfing.com
- **Location:** Sydney
- **Why:** Founded by ex-P.Johnson Tailors designer. Good clothes first, golf as context. Shirts and knits that move between life and a round.

### Merrill Golf
- **URL:** https://merrillgolf.com
- **Location:** USA
- **Why:** Vintage Americana. Mohair cardigans, western-motif tees, five-panel caps with tackle shop energy. No collared shirts. Casual rounds only.

### FlagBag Golf Co.
- **URL:** https://flagbaggolfco.com
- **Location:** USA
- **Why:** Golf bags made from actual course flags. You choose the flags — favorite rounds, meaningful courses. The bag carries personal history.

### Shapland Bags
- **URL:** https://shaplandbags.com
- **Location:** UK
- **Why:** Classic carry bags, improved. Lightweight, thoughtful details, just enough customization. Designed by someone who studied what came before.
